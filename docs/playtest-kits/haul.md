# Playtest kit: the haul

Step 1 of [find the fun](../plans/find-the-fun.md): is hauling fun as a toy, with no goal? Written for one experienced
player; it should take minutes. Its card is on [boogle.club/protos/](https://boogle.club/protos/).

Open https://boogle.club/?proto=haul&room=proto-haul (any `proto-` room; a fresh name gives a clean save). The **Haul
kit** panel (top right, tap its title to fold it) has pour feel for this tab, room numbers (section and hut cost, hard
cap, logs per take, takes per bundle, refill), **Reset haul** (tap twice), **Defaults** and **Copy values** (one line
with every number, to paste with your take).

Without the flag nothing changes. If hauling is a chore, say so plainly:
[boogle-2.md](../proposals/boogle-2.md) says to test a different core loop before building trains on it.
