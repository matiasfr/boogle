## Design

- Keep exactly one cargo owner: existing `back.big === 'beam'`, `placeBig`, pickup, deposit and departure recovery. A helper never receives a second beam, stock, or a transferable cargo claim. No chained bodies, joints, forced movement or ownership transfer.
- Start with **2.2 m/s solo**, **5.0 m/s assisted**, with no sprint in either case. These are hypotheses for the requested owner playtest, verified as actual movement before reporting. The boiler keeps walking speed.
- Move `GREAT_TREE` from local `(4, -86)` to **`(-4, -138)`**, world `(996, 30, -138)`. Numeric inspection puts it **52.85 m** from unchanged T3, versus **28.77 m** before. `safeTreeFall` finds a noncompact fall for all 16 approach headings; the nearest catalog tree is 56.8 m away. Browser navigation remains required; numeric clearance alone is not acceptance.
- Preserve `makeTree` index/order, tree-harvest identity, partial work, the accepted `safeTreeFall` yaw and `trunkAt()` behavior. Never hardcode the fallen pickup to the standing tree position.
- Implement a small prototype module `src/proto/place/great-carry.ts`, loaded by one side-effect import in `index.ts`. It consumes `back`, current player and remote snapshots and registers its own Interact targets; do not add branches to `verbs.ts` or change the optional seesaw.
- **Owner helper state:** additive presence `placeGreatBeamHelp: carrierPeerId | null`. `Help carry` at the carrier's beam end joins; `Let go` releases. Use the existing Interact action, keyboard/pad/touch mappings and guide row; no action or pad slot is added. Helping requires empty hands/back and no optional-beam grip.
- Validate presence as a bounded string, require `placeCoopTrees1`, current visible/entered peer, fresh samples, carrier `placeBig === 'beam'`, and physical proximity. Use `r.snaps.sample(now - INTERP_MS)`; stale, missing, invalid or departed fields mean off. A helper can join within 4 m and remains valid only within 5 m and 2 m vertical separation. Select one qualifying helper deterministically by peer id; multiple helpers never stack speed. Moving away releases assistance. Helper remains independently controlled and can walk alongside the beam.
- **Local movement:** an `onFrame` hook at `prePhysics`, order `-60`, runs once after player intent (`-80`) and before physics. Set `me.sprint = false`, and scale the newly rebuilt `me.wish` by `targetSpeed / 5.6` for the carrier; suppress helper sprint while assisting. Do not repeatedly scale in fixed steps: intent is rebuilt once per frame. No core controller, simulation or lockstep changes are needed.
- **Local presentation:** keep one visible beam. Position the helper interaction at the free end of the existing carried beam and make its shared support read in the two-player capture. If inspection shows the existing shoulder mesh does not read as shared support, adjust its Local carried pose through a small data hook in `chop.ts` (no reverse import of `great-carry.ts`, no second beam or new material). Do not silently accept two avatars near a beam as proof that the grip reads.
- **Recovery:** helper departure/release removes only the bonus; the original owner still carries and can finish solo. Carrier departure clears helper state and uses existing `coop.ts` recovery to return the unconsumed trunk to `trunkAt()` at the new source. Delivered/in-flight stock retains the existing consumed guards. Presence getters return null immediately when the relationship stops. Reconnection must not resurrect a helper grip or stale shoulder.

The source move **does touch beautiful-corner's approach composition** and supersedes its recorded 29 m source approach under this explicit owner request. T3 receiver, machine, feeder fit, collision envelopes, crossing and landings remain unchanged. Inspect both the new source approach and the existing `corner=1` receiver/feed view; do not begin beautiful-corner step 3.
