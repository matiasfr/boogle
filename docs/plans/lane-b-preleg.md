# Lane B · Pre-leg works

A guest at the Village can chop a starter back of eight logs, leave it at the existing recovery rack, see their name on the station board and go back to playing. A friend at the works sees the bundle arrive on Line 2. The [course-change checkpoint](course-change.md) measures the guest's first useful action within two minutes of joining. Delivery follows the train timetable. In the hand-fed version below, the friend still has to carry the load to advance the bill. Substituting for free kit alone cannot demonstrate remote bill progress.
