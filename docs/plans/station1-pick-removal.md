## What main does today

- The choice has **no row of its own**. In `src/sim/railway-components.ts` the room saves two booleans, Rail.pack and Rail.jump. The first job's start tick plus both flags false makes `line1PickReady` true. Either take sets its own flag. Neither the taker nor the site is saved. The stand, hold, camera and carrier are local. Two true flags at a later checkpoint cannot identify which was taken first.
- The two extra ways into the rules are `rail.pack` and `rail.jump` with station T1. The wire scrubber forwards station, host admission checks the selected stand, sim apply checks the bill, the adapter decodes the site, and local prediction makes a special exception for the competing pack take. The host normally omits the predicted pack event to its sender. The adapter therefore synthesizes an accepted T1 pack from the acknowledgement. Step 1 removes both halves of this exception.
- Direct readers of readiness are the two sim systems, the stand controller and the frame view. The accepted site's presentation readers are the stand controller, tools and ability arrival. The forge board also hides its promised pack in an early-pack room. Other readers use possession, independent of site: capacity, inventory and carry displays, pack on avatars, jump charge, the forge errand, trophy plinth, secrets, shared arrival/flourish and prediction correction. Preserve these consumers (`railway-queries.ts`, `railway-effects.ts`, `line-cargo.ts`, `tools.ts`, `forge-rewards.ts`, `trophy-plinth.ts`, `finds/secrets-view.ts`). Keep the ordinary pickup action marks and their ops registrations.
- A2 instantiates two records at runtime, `railway-pick-pack` and `railway-pick-jump`, from one compiled prefab. The pack block stands at (991.62, −137.89) and the drum at (999.62, −137.89), each within 3 m reach. Searching authored and built levels finds zero placed pick-stand records. The compiled record lives in `content/built/prefabs.json`. The forge's pack stand, the pillar drum, the T1 frame and its separate personal cosmetic are other objects.
- Both A3 direct links are hidden from the jump menu. They call the same funded-T1 seed with an optional take. Its no-argument path already feeds both forge-last seeds. A separate look builder has a third early-grant stage. Ordinary downstream fixtures can have a completed T3 with the optional jump still unclaimed.
- On main, the T1 loop node costs three eight-log carries from a nearest source 13 m away. Restoring the original gates costs a pack-first solo room five extra round trips across T2, T3 and the first forge bill, per the A1 comparison. Bills and source distances are fixed here. These are modeled trips. The evidence for the change is the owner's report, not a time target.
