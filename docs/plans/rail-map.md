## The ends audit

The rule: a track that ends ends in buffer stops and is clearly marked as a terminal (in the world: buffers, an
end-of-line board, a platform arrangement that reads as an end; on maps, a terminal symbol); otherwise it runs on
into another line or closes in a loop. A scissors crossover turns trains, but both of its tracks still end past the
platforms, so on its own it doesn't comply.

### The shape it gives the network

Five services instead of nine:

1. **Line 1**, Village to Camp: two terminals. Unchanged.
2. **The ring** (line 2 grown): Village → Quarry → Far Head → the pier → Ring Island → High Gorge → Canopy Town →
   Port Quay → Saltpan → West landing. One line, built from both ends: line 2 from Home's east side (v26, v27, the first
   branch), the cave line from West landing (stage 6), joined by the White Wall line (stage 7), so "the ring closes"
   is the moment the two halves meet. Its two ends are both at Home, 100–130 m apart on foot (ruling 6's Firelink hub).
   It's the greybox's own full loop (`FULL_LOOP`), now as one train. About 8.7 km: by generic-lines' wait bound
   it wants about a dozen trains, which is a lot of life on the horizon (round 4's "lots of trains running").
3. **The bay line**, West landing → Bell Rock → Canopy Town, on to Lantern Notch at stage 8.
4. **The north–south line**, High Gorge → Bell Rock → the Original Island: the incline and the causeway.
5. **The bay trestle**, Ring Island ↔ Bell Rock, only if Canopy is first: two terminals.

track) still covers every junction: Ring Island (the ring, the trestle's bay platform), High Gorge (the ring through,

north–south line and the bay line, see question 4) and West landing (two terminals). Nothing needs through-running

## Continuity and curves

`npm run level-check` now runs a **Track continuity** report (`dev/level/track-continuity.ts`, over
`dev/level/rail-network.ts`): every track of the network as the game draws it, both tracks of the formation, the
crossover, the v27 crossing, its loop and every tentative line. It measures kinks (heading change at a joint), the
tightest radius and curves entered without easing, chord spacing, grade and grade breaks, the up track's turnouts
(divergence and radius), platform edges against both tracks, the fouling points, and the joins between separately
defined pieces. It fails only on a gap, a reversed chord or a kink over 25° on track a train is drawn on today;
planned and tentative tracks report the same measures and list what would fail. Thresholds and why: the file's header
(a 2° kink swings a car's end 5.6 cm within about 7 frames at 20 m/s, the visible twitch; 25.6° is where two cars'
inner corners touch across a joint).

**How a train is posed today.** On Place's rails: `poseCarOnLine` (`src/proto/place/rail.ts`) takes each car's
heading from the chord between points 1.1 m either side of its centre, on Place's line, a Catmull-Rom spline through
hand-placed points resampled every 1 m. So no vertex snaps (the worst joint is 1.7° beyond its neighbours); each car
is a rigid box posed on its own, 3.7 m apart, so on a tight curve consecutive cars differ by 3.7/R (18° at 11.8 m).
The sim's `RAIL_LINES[...].path` (10 m) is never drawn (only `railLinePoint`'s near-train check reads it), which is why
its 25–41° joints don't matter. The up track isn't posed yet (v26: "offset 4 m left, easing across each turnout"). v27
and the future lines are polylines: posed directly, every vertex would snap by its full angle. **The cheapest fix for
small kinks is a smoother pose (view only)**: pose v27's and later lines on a spline through their points, as
`makeLine` already does, rather than moving sim points.

- minimum radius 40 m, and only on loops at their reduced speed; 60 m on running line;
- an easing (curvature rising linearly) of at least 20 m into any curve under 200 m;
- a vertical curve of at least 10 m per 1 % of grade change (40 m from 4 % to level), and no grade over 4 %;
- at most 2° of heading change at any vertex a car is posed across, or a spline through the points;
- turnouts leave at 7° or less (a 4 m shift over at least 50 m), and no stop mark inside a fouling zone;
- every join between pieces within 5 cm and 2°.
