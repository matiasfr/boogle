Economy prototype

Play the whole economy before building it in 3D: haul by hand, connect the islands, upgrade the works and watch machines take over, with one, two or three players on the Sickle Isles. Every bill, rate, upgrade, price and the away credit come from the ring economy model; every position and distance comes from the map prototypes' Sickle Isles.

How to play it

  1. Pick 1, 2 or 3 players and press New run. You are player 1; your friends are bots. Turn on Watch the bots to let player 1 play itself too.
  2. Move: tap or click a place on the map, or pick one with ← → and press Enter. Short hops are walked; longer ones ride the train over the lines built so far.
  3. Work: the buttons under You (keys 1–9): gather a material where it's found, carry it to the step's site and deliver it, place stock the machines have stacked in the yard, or explore the step's place. A step is done when its bill is in and its place is explored.
  4. Time: Space pauses; [ and ] change the speed (real time to 1200×). A sitting lasts 1.5 hours of play; then the world pauses for a day and the next join credits the yards once. L leaves early.
  5. Choices: the world pauses at the fork (High Gorge or Canopy Town) and at the first Roundhouse upgrade. After the ending, spend the shared wallet on the end-game goals.
  6. Riders and Boogles: once the Village line is built, little guys wait at each town and ride to Home, paying a fare each way into the one shared strongbox at the ticket window (it fills to its mark and then fares stop landing). Spend Boogles on the Village's third cottage plot and the next houses: each brings riders, and the crowd it makes asks for seats. With seats on Materials (the default) the bill is hauled to Home and the seats go in with no Boogle spent; with Boogles you buy them instead. Four or more waiting stop a train. Every number is from the trade model, which this page runs the rules of, so the two cannot drift.
  7. Read it: each player's bar splits hauling, exploring and waiting; blue lines in the log mark where machines overtake hands. Export the timeline as CSV or JSON at the bottom.
