Open questions

  1. Does anyone but the finder get a card that stops them? Recommended: no; everyone else gets a flourish that never stops them, and the full card is the finder's alone.
  2. Where's the gathering spot? Recommended: a trophy plinth at the finders' bay in the village, playing automatically the first time each player passes; never required.
  3. Do the rings appear on everyone at once, or get fitted at Home? Recommended: at once, wherever you are; Home keeps the trophy, not your rings.
  4. Any summon at all? Recommended: no moving players; at most an optional beckon later.
