Ring economy

A co-op incremental you explore like a Metroidvania. Every new material starts in your hands; its producer starts slower than you, then its upgrades overtake you. The world is a ring of places around Home, with shortcuts through the middle and a free choice of direction after Ring Island.

The ring and the hub

Home is the hub. You leave by the first sea crossing, go around the ring, and later build spokes back to Home as shortcuts. Closing the ring turns the railway into a loop line.

How resources flow

Hands start every tier. Machines take over the bulk, and hands always place the last piece.

By hand

A new material is gathered and carried by whoever finds it. Each person's pack is their own until delivered.

Producer and its yard

Connecting a place starts its producer, slowly. Its yard shows stacks; a full yard pauses it.

Trains

Freight is limited by trains, wagons and speed, shared between every remote producer.

Home Yard

Shared stock for everyone in the world, in bays you can see. Nobody's wallet: it only builds shared things.

Upgrades

Rail segments, spokes, producer levels, yard bays, engines and wagons. Hands carry each piece from the rack.

Manual assists raise the hand line: carry frame, fibre sling, proper axe, powered pick.
Producer, wagon and engine upgrades raise the machine line until it passes a crew.
Sinks are finite builds. No fuel, repairs or decay. Away, producers only fill their own yards.
