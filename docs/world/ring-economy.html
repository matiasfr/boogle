Ring economy

A co-op incremental you explore like a Metroidvania. Every new material starts in your hands; its producer starts slower than you, then its upgrades overtake you, until at the end everything is automated and a new tier of goals opens. The world is paused while nobody plays. Revised after the owner's answers of 6 November.

The ring and the hub

Home is the hub. You leave by the first sea crossing, go around the ring, double back with new abilities, and build spokes home as shortcuts. Closing the ring turns the railway into a loop line. This circle is a schematic: the real layout is being prototyped separately and will be irregular, and the model's distances live in one editable table (Links), so a new layout only changes those numbers.

How resources flow

Hands start every tier. Machines take over the bulk, and hands always place the last piece.

By hand

A new material is gathered and carried by whoever finds it. Each person's pack is their own until delivered.

Producer and its yard

Connecting a place starts its producer, slowly. Its yard shows stacks; a full yard pauses it.

Trains

Freight is limited by trains, wagons and speed, shared between every remote producer.

Home Yard

Shared stock for everyone in the world, in bays you can see. A full yard's surplus sells to the town: one shared wallet anyone may spend.

Upgrades

Rail segments, spokes, producer levels, yard bays, engines and wagons, with numeric costs. Hands carry each piece from the rack until the end-game cranes.

Manual assists raise the hand line for everyone (progression is shared): carry frame, fibre sling, proper axe, powered pick.
Producer, wagon and engine upgrades raise the machine line until it passes a crew.
