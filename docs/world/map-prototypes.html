Map Prototypes

Five top-down layouts for the world after Ring Island, at the real scale: Home, Split-rock Works and Ring Island sit where they are on the map today, and the grid squares are 1 km. Each one has a mostly linear unlock order, one order choice where you visit both sides, and at least one double-back to a gate you saw early. Every place is on the main path. Step through the stages to feel the order; tap anything for details. The art-surface table near the end counts what each layout asks the art to dress: smaller islands with sea between them mean less land, each dressed more densely.

      Ride from Home

      Tap or hover a place, line, gate or secret on the map.

      All five at the end of the main game (first pick on the left)

      Rides are computed from the drawn lines: length at 20 m/s, plus 10 s to speed up and slow down and 8 s dwell per link, plus 20 s to board, the same settings as the economy model. Hours are the model's solo pacing, moved to each layout's order; they are allowances, not measurements.

      Art surface: what has to be dressed

      New land only; Home is about 0.25 km² as drawn. Walkable land is reachable on foot and gets dense dressing; backdrop land is seen from the line but not walked (hatched on the map). Track on land is seen up close; track over sea is one repeated trestle kit; tunnels are interiors. Sea in view is the share of the ground within 1 km of each station that is water: sea and sky are cheap, readable and already look good in the renderer. The last column is the most land within 1 km of any one station, a rough measure of what must be resident at once.
