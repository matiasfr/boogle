Assumptions, sources & next decisions

Review question: Does each connection feel worth making, and is there enough to do when you arrive? This is a 2D scoping study, not eight hours of proven content.

Future island footprints, route geometry, bills and upgrade sites are proposals. Version 2 starts after Line 1; the earlier spine starts after the full pier. Ring Island keeps the existing FAR anchor, roughly 751 m from the pier; the old 1.2 km crossing test does not set its distance.
