Trade economy

The passenger-trade economy model, played over the route's hours. Four rooms ride the same route and the same stand-in numbers from the brief: one that never spends a Boogle and adds no seat (A), one that builds houses and hauls for seats as crowds appear (B), one that spends on source upgrades first (C), and the second room again with seats priced in Boogles, as before the owner's answer of 10 November (D). Time runs across the route's hours; the shaded minutes are the route standing still while the room hauls for seats.

Every number is a named tunable at the bottom of the page: change one and all four rooms play again. The 5% and 10% bounds are not rules (owner, 10 November); the page reports the extra hauling and the hours saved and does not judge them. Stages: .
