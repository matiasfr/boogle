Upgrade tree

A design aid for the upgrade-choices brief, to react to, not a game UI. Each part waits in the world beside the machine it changes, and one press from anyone fits it for the room. Nodes are in play order. Click one to see what it repays and where its part would wait, then fit it to see what changes down the line.

Names are working labels, not final on-screen copy; there are no lines for The Boogler here. Numbers marked ≈ are estimates from the code's constants (walk 5.6 m/s, train 20 m/s), not measurements.
