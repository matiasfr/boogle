Home station layout

Home's tracks, platforms, turntable and stalls as one terminus station, drawn to scale. Every generated number comes from dev/level/home-station-page.ts and the same layout and sim anchors used by the game (source commit ). North is up; one unit is one metre (Place-local; world x is local x + 1000). Walk lines are straight-line distances at Buddy's 5.6 m/s, so real walks are a little longer.

Clearance check

Run on Home's geometry: track within 1.75 m of a building (half the sleepers plus a walkway), a stall wedge within 0.5 m of a building or 0.3 m of another track, a line passing a turntable it doesn't serve, and anchors standing on a track, in the pit or inside a building.

    One terminus; the forge is the goods side

    Both lines leave buffer stops beside a open-fronted hall, either side of a island, and meet the outer lines at line 1 s 60 and line 2 s 84 with every s past the joins unchanged. Before trains run the island is a kerb outline round its apron; when they run its deck and canopy drop in, with the trains. Each platform edge's halt board carries a picture of where its trains go. The turntable stands on its own spur off road 2, with three radial stall roads at 160°, 190° and 220°: the middle stall open, the other two ruin outlines. No line train turns on the table.

    The cart rides out road 1, across the leg (, dashed when shown) and back down road 2 to rest at its forge stop by the buffers, so CART_ROAD.forgeCm is . The track layer, once the forge has turned it, drives the same way round from road 1 s 19 to road 2 s 7: (the orange dashes).
