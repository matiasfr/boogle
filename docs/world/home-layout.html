    What is off today (measured)

    1. Two stations. The 22 m halts stand 30.5 m apart on the outer sides of the fork and face away from each other.
    2. No throat. Both lines start at the turntable's centre and leave it 94° apart; no line train ever turns on it.
    3. The stall isn't on the table. Its road leaves the rim at 225°, kinks about 25° and 70°, and ends at a 4 × 9 m ruin that is not radial.
    4. Toy-short train. Cars are 3.2 × 2.2 m (2 BH long, 1.4 BH wide); real coaches are about 11 BH long and 1.7 BH wide. The four-car train (14.2 m) is about the forge's length.
    5. Turntable 3.2× the loco (real: about 1.1×), and a raised disc rather than a pit. Against shunter plus footing wagon (7.6 m) it is 1.26×, which is fine.
    6. Goods on the passenger side. Crane, dock and boiler pedestal stand 3.5–6 m off line 1, on the line-1 platform's side.
    7. Q1's frame stands in the fork between both lines; the cart-call handle is 3.96 m from the table's centre, on the disc's footprint (the handle is being cut).
    8. The town faces a junction with no station. Pip's house, the shop, the museum and the wood-1 hall all turn their doors toward the table; the nearest platform is 44–53 m from each.
    9. Line 2's tightest curve (15 m, s 32–34) starts just past its platform, and half that platform runs beside a 47–55 m curve.

    B: one terminus; the forge is the goods side recommended

    Both lines end at buffer stops by a 13 × 7 m concourse where the town's doors already point, either side of a 4 × 23 m island under one canopy. The buffers are staggered 1.74 m (line 1) and 0.59 m (line 2) so each line's length to its join (s 82, s 86) is unchanged. The turntable moves 21 m onto an engine spur off road 2 (R 10 m, 90°) with three radial stalls, the middle one open. Crane, dock, forge rack and pedestal line road 1 on the forge side.

    Costs. TRAIN_STOPS and every s value stay. Re-pinned x/z values: LINE_HALTS, Q1 and Q2 frames, LINE2_FORGE rack, LINE2_BOILER, SHED_TABLE, SHED_STALL, WORKS_SEAMS[0]. The cart uses a crossover at the throat (dashed) and reverses instead of turning on the table, so CART_ROAD changes shape. WORLD_NS bump: likely, at a session boundary; a planner confirms. Invalidates the Home legs of the bot route, cart proofs and the shunter and stall seeds; proofs past s 86 hold. The stone yard moves (scenery), and the footings walk grows from 11 m to 24 m.

    Why B: it is the only option with one station, a throat where the lines meet, a turntable on its own road with radial stalls, and a station building where the town already faces, and it moves no lockstep s value.

    C: one station in the vee (minimum move)

    Lines unchanged. One wedge platform runs s 5–28 on the inner faces of both lines, with an 8 × 6 m building on the bisector and a canopy over the forecourt; the trains' doors face each other 19 m apart. The stall turns radial where it is: the open stall at 235° (over today's cracked slab) and a ruin outline at 265°; a third at 295° would land on the curio shelf. Q1's frame moves out of the vee.

    Costs. Only position values change: LINE_HALTS, the Q1 frame and SHED_STALL. WORLD_NS bump: no. No stop change. Invalidates bot waypoints for the halts, Q1 and the stall. The table stays the junction (as at Bembridge) and stays 3.2× the loco on the running lines.
