Quick or Strong, on the lines as built

A per-line trade: Quick boiler (25 m/s, the same cars) or Strong boiler (20 m/s, one more car), one price, swaps free. This page asks whether that trade means anything on the North–south (ns) and Bay (bay) lines that greybox leg 2 built, and what would make Strong pay without new freight. It is an editable design model, not the garage UI or a played route. The plan it serves is garage-step4.md (step 1).

MEASURED means read from the sim on main (seeds incline-mid, bay-mid, bay-reached, 6,000 ticks each, or leg 2's own measurements). ARITHMETIC means computed on this page from trainLegTicks' profile (the leg() function below) and stated assumptions; nobody has played or timed it.

The two lines as built

Each line runs two trains between its depot (halt 0) and Bell Rock, nonstop unless a rider asks for head 1. Each leg is two sections of 20 logs + 20 rails. One train carries a whole section's kit, and the rider goes with that kit: section 1's rack is at the depot, and the player rides the section 2 kit train to head 1. Once a leg is laid, the line has no towns, no riders and no freight. The trade is offered at the leg's last lay (bell-reached, bay-reached), so it never applies while its own line is being built.

At 1, 2 and 3 players

On today's lines the only thing the trade can change is a ride, and a ride takes the same time at any player count: the players' car has four seats, so three players ride one train together. Riding is all a player waits for here: the leg is laid, so there is no kit to wait for.

Saved per end-to-end trip = the mean wait at a depot (arithmetic, below) + the ride. A player who splits from the group (leg 2's beat 7) rides alone and saves the same per ride.

Variants that could make Strong pay without new freight

What already exists, from greybox-leg2.md and the rail decisions: the players' car seats four (10 November, "Four seats"); the riders' coach is a separate car and does not count toward a boiler's car allowance, so Strong's car stays cargo (16 November); one kit a train, and a section's bill is one train load; the next leg's kit can already ride aboard before its rack exists ("the kit rides ahead"); a train can already hold at its depot while kit loads (legKitLoad returns hold). Leg 3 (bell-canopy, the Bay line's next leg, three sections, kit loaded at the West landing) is specified in railway-route.ts but its rules are not built. The North–south line's next leg is the causeway: one section of 6 logs + 6 rails, in area 9.

Leg 3 on the Bay line: where a hold could pay

ARITHMETIC Leg 3 is not built, so this is the only number on the page that describes a future route. Section k's rack stands at its start. After a lay, the players stand at the section's rack and wait for the next kit train to come from the West landing, then ride it one section on. With Strong as a hold, the train that brought section 1's kit also carries section 2's and stands at the railhead until section 1 is laid, so section 2's wait is only the hop. Section 3 is waited for as today: the second train cannot stand at the same railhead (an assumption, unmeasured). The best case is assumed throughout: a loaded kit train leaves the depot the moment it is needed. The extra-wait input below adds a late train (leg 2's worst settled wait was 107.5 s, measured).

Defaults: bay stops s 1217 to s 2430 (railway-world-lines.ts), three equal sections (sectionsOf); feed 30 s for 40 units solo and lay 30 s from leg 2's beat table (arithmetic, not measured), feed split evenly between players; dwell 5 s (TRAIN.dwellTicks).

Steel · the built Works casts 12 and then stops

The built route spends no steel: every leg is greybox kit (20 logs + 20 rails a section, no footings). The steel works casts STEEL.total = 12 and nothing casts more, so the supply after the works' 12 defaults to 0. A room can also reach stage 4 without lighting the works at all (the stack leg opens on the rivet), with 0 steel landed. Negative balances show missing supply, not borrowed steel.

railway-ring-layout.ts STEEL.total = 12; railway-works-layout.ts INSTALL_STEEL 3: 6, 4: 4; the 1 steel trade price is the plan's call 19 (a stand-in). The old page's 1 steel a minute was its own stand-in: no production rule exists. The old bills are the economy's stand-ins (dev/loop/standins.ts), kept as an alternative.
