    Assumptions, scale and source snapshot

    This is a local design diagram, not gameplay or an accepted site. The one engine and one wagon are schematic proposed envelopes on a 12 m table. The bay is 10 × 7 m. The free fitting adds visible wagon side racks without changing the train length. A new branch connects to Line 1; Line 2 remains reachable through the existing town junction. Spur curvature, turnout and access are not engineered.

    The sweep is a conservative top-down construction reservation, including the magazine and working reach, not a measured mesh union. It does not imply ground-level collision everywhere: the gantry reaches roughly 4.4 m high. Roofs, trees, station frames, terrain and vertical clearance need a later survey.

    Buildings include all town growth stages and the three walk-in rooms. Rectangles use authored widths, depths and yaw; landmark bounds include their wider tops. Ordinary roof overhang is not included. Rails are sampled every 4 m from the actual Float32 layout, without changing scale. Paths use their exact authored vertices with an assumed 2 m walking corridor.
