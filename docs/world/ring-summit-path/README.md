# Ring Island's summit footbridge

Checkpoint stills for [the summit path plan](../../plans/ring-summit-path.md), step 4, taken 18 November 2026 on
4c0c05cc with `dev/look/evals/ring-island-walk.ts` (`--query ringview=<name>`), 1200 x 750 WebGPU captures.

- [bridge](bridge.png): from the halt-side rim along the deck (seed `crossing-done`). The path runs straight onto the
  deck at a with no step or gap; the abutment's concrete shows under its right edge.
- [cut](cut.png): from track level in the cutting (seed `stack-laid-1`). Both bents stand on the foot of the cut
  walls, clear of both tracks; the orange deck reads against the pale rock and meets each rim.
- [halt](halt.png): the island halt's west platform, the player's first sight of the bridge. An orange bar with both
  bents in the notch, the path zigzagging up to it; the fingerpost stands left of it.
- [rim](rim.png): 15 m uphill of the summit-side rim looking back down. The rim rocks mark the edge; the drop itself
  does not show. The path leaves b along the rim.
- [deck](deck.png): on the deck 5 m from the halt-side end, looking toward b.

No ride under the bridge was staged. Not tested on a device.
