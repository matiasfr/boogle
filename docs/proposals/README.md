# Proposals

Directions and ideas that aren't decided yet. Unlike [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md), nothing here is committed to. Pick, mix, cut. Each file cites the research it rests on, in [docs/research/](../research/README.md).

- [boogle-2.md](boogle-2.md): **start here for Boogle 2.0.** The owner's design decisions (rounds 1–8), standing preferences, where the artifacts and paintings are, open questions, and the agreed next step: find the fun with mechanics-only prototypes.
- [directions.md](directions.md): eight broad directions for the game (an idle bazaar, exploration by raft and buggy, a mystery told by the island, toybox physics, an asynchronous coconut post, staying tiny, plus the storm tide and the landmark restoration from the newer reports on `main`), the two plumbing fixes they depend on, the principles each serves and bends, and ways to mix them.
- [boogler-tech-tree.md](boogler-tech-tree.md): *Business for Beginners*, a lore-driven tech tree. The stuck pages of The Boogler's washed-up business book unlock the stall and Boat Fund, vehicles, the coconut post, Oyijon's kitchen, suzani pattern magic and Gary's boats, with three possible endings. It is split into finishable seasons and wraps the communal report's landmark restoration.
- [vehicles.md](vehicles.md): feel rules and starting cannon-es numbers for the soapbox buggy, land yacht, melon raft and cart, plus how they sync.
- [methodology.md](methodology.md): working habits from the designers studied (phases before lore, reveals, feel passes, heatmaps, reactivity, integer incremental maths, anti-patterns).
- [hub-and-minigames.md](hub-and-minigames.md): the Club Penguin-shaped direction. The island as a hub, feel-first mini-games in place (with elevators only where needed), vehicles and exploration as the way between venues, and personal looks, knacks, a hut and an earned currency. Maps the earlier proposals onto it.
- [story-and-finale.md](story-and-finale.md): a goofy story across the Boogle 2.0 eras (the mayor's opening days, running gags: Gary, the fourth melon, coconut mail) and three communal finales that work when not every friend is online.
- [vertical-slice.md](vertical-slice.md): the build spec for eras 1–2 (the wood age → steam wagons) on top of the 0.1 haul loop: era props, the timetabled steam wagon on rails (riding feel first), opening day (any player starts it, everyone watches), the mayor, the soapbox run, the era hat, and the round 7 decisions.
- [fourth-melon.md](fourth-melon.md): a brief for the writers on The Boogler's fourth-melon gag: where it comes from in his lines, what it does for the story, three drafts of the reveal, and what changing away from melons would cost.
- [island-layout.md](island-layout.md): three layout options for eras 1–2 on home (lighthouse line, west cove, east bay; the lighthouse line recommended), checked against the level-design research, a rough archipelago plan, the interactive layout lab and `?layout=` to walk an option in greybox.
- [visual-target.md](visual-target.md): how good the island can look on an M4 iPad Pro with code-only graphics: the `?proto=look` lab (haze, shadows, material detail, grass, AO, SSGI, TRAA, bloom, grade, toon and more), cost per layer and preset measured at iPad resolution, stills, and a recommended `ipad` preset with a frame budget.
