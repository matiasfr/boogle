## Three independent-review points

1. **No box clamp.** The only gameplay writes to `Till.boogles` are adding accepted fares and subtracting an
   accepted purchase, in [railway-sys-trade.ts](../../src/sim/railway-sys-trade.ts). `ridersArrive` uses
   `min(fares, max(0, boxCap - boogles))`, so a reduced cap stops new fares without taking money away.
   [checkTrade](../../src/sim/railway-check-trade.ts) checks integer bounds and conservation, not `boogles <= cap`
   or historical payments against today's prices. The added saved-world test retains an above-cap balance and
   a plot 3 payment of 6,000 through load and 1,200 ticks; arrivals occur and are counted as lost fares.
2. **The pinned golden moves; v54 → v55.** Only `house-built.after` changes, **1683160544 → 332299744**.
   Its seed tick remains 7619 and seed hash 3599873888. All other stage rows are identical. A controlled replay
   with only the cap restored to four passes all 107 old golden checks, despite the new plot price. The first
   divergence is tick **8439** (+820): train slot 1 boards its fifth rider, `wait1` falls from 1 to 0, and
   `boarded` becomes 36 instead of 35. The till is identical at that instant. Re-pinned deliberately after the
   dated v55 entry in [namespace.ts](../../src/world/namespace.ts), not to hide a refactor defect.
   `rail-schema.json` is unchanged. The server host trace also needed a namespace re-pin: its v54-only control
   matches all 8,600 records. The v55 trace changes only 205 namespace/checksum envelopes and 2,528
   `snapBytes` samples; all simulation state, hashes, commands and replica packets stay exact.
   [Host trace audit](../../.look/first-town-lesson/host-trace-diff.json). L1 must use the next free namespace, not its planned v55.
3. **No client `checkTrade` against a different cap.** Production validation is the server-only checkpoint
   loader in [railway-server.ts](../../src/world/railway-server.ts), through `railwayCheck` → `checkTrade`.
   [railway-replicate.ts](../../src/world/railway-replicate.ts) ingests validated replica fields and publishes
   `cloneWorld(world)` at complete packet boundaries. The adapter predicts via `cloneWorld` and `railPredict`,
   without running `railwayCheck` or `checkTrade`. The current build shares `TRADE.riderPlaces` for rules and
   rendering; v55 also separates the room namespace. No compatibility fence or migration was added.
