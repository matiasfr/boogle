# Why changes have taken so long, and what changes

**27 November 2026.** The owner asked: "I want you to review why these changes have taken so much time up until now. And what we can do to improve the flow." Read by one orchestrator session from `git log` on `origin/main` at `5c4ab2023` (world v75), `gh` run lists, the backlog, the state file and the playtest record. Line counts include generated fixtures and pages, so read them as proportions, not exact sizes. The plan this serves is [course-change](../plans/course-change.md); the rules it sets are in the [decision](../decisions-process.md#d-2026-11-27-flow-one-pass-then-refine).

## The short answer

The machinery is fast. The work it was pointed at was not the work that mattered, and each piece of it was built in many small, fully proven steps.

## Six causes, largest first

1. **The proof was aimed at the wrong question.** Bots, the loop, level checks and rejoin proofs show the route *can* be finished. Nothing measures whether a new person knows what to do. On 25 November the checkpoint offered "Fresh-player first hour" beside "Route to the ending", and the route was recommended and picked. The route then passed every proof while the problem the playtests keep showing went untouched.
2. **The greybox was not grey.** Each of six legs went catalog, rules, seeds, views, bot, loop, checkpoint: five to seven steps a leg, most its own pull request with an independent review (up to five rounds), a world bump, regenerated golden fixtures, a frame-time reading and a rejoin proof. Geometry faults were found after the rules were built, not before: the Wall "unbuildable where drawn", Port Quay's apron 0.88 m under its platforms, the 2 m slew at the Gorge. Each one reopened a finished leg.
3. **Too much is open, and rows do not close.** The backlog has 22 rows and 17 of them read `building`. A built row stays "until he has played it". Eight job slots are filled by rank every hour, so turnouts, the cliff kit, the rider coach, garage trades and cargo trains all advanced while the first thirty minutes stayed unreadable to a guest.
4. **Bookkeeping is a large share of the traffic.** The state file changed 345 times in 16 days. On 27 November, 20 of the first 55 pull requests were bookkeeping. Every one runs CI and a deploy, and a deploy reloads every open page: twelve reloads in the owner's 88-minute session on 14 November.
5. **The owner is the only source of words and looks.** Guide lines ship as `owner's line: …`. Rows wait on "his look" and "his words". Each plan's design review adds calls for him (one plan: 5 Decided, 10 Default, 6 New, five questions). Most proceed on defaults, but nothing that needs his voice ever finishes.
6. **Review depth is the same everywhere.** A view-only greybox step and a server rule get the same fresh reviewer and the same fix rounds.

## What changes

Each is recorded in the [decision](../decisions-process.md#d-2026-11-27-flow-one-pass-then-refine). Safety rules are untouched: `src/sim`, `src/world`, `src/net`, `server/` and the wire still get their independent review.

| # | Change | Replaces |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **A person is the proof.** The checkpoint's exit is a number from fresh players: seconds from landing to their first accepted contribution. Bots stay as regression guards | A bot reaching the end |
| 2 | **Layout once, in 2D, then build once.** Any route or area geometry is settled on one data file and its 2D page, with the level check run on that data, before anything is built in 3D. Then the whole pass is one branch, one world bump, one review. Findings after that are collected and fixed as one refinement pass a week | Five to seven steps a leg, a bump and a review each |
| 3 | **Greybox proof tier.** Greybox and view-only work gets one proof look and one review at the end of its pass. No frame-time readings, rejoin proofs or per-step checkpoints until an area leaves greybox | Production proofs on stand-in content |
| 4 | **Three lanes.** Free slots go to the checkpoint's lanes first. A row marked parked gets no job. A row leaves the table when its "done when" is met; what is owed to the owner's eyes moves to one "for him to see" list | Rows that stay open for his play |
| 5 | **One bookkeeping ship an hour,** from the heartbeat. A live session's claims ride with its next work ship. A backlog row's next step is one sentence; history lives in its plan | One a turn |
| 6 | **Drafted words ship as words.** A guide's line is written in character by the drafting model and marked for his edit in the lines file, never shown to a player as `owner's line: …` | Debug strings on screen |
| 7 | **One page of questions a day,** at most five, each with its recommendation chosen. Everything else proceeds and is logged | Questions per plan |

Recommended and not yet done, because each changes CI or a standing owner call (state file, open owner questions): a docs-only merge should not reload open pages; world bumps batched to one a week.
