"Not instant fast travel" bears on the 1 November "only hub stations are fast travel" (a jump): the brief reads the express as that call built as a ride. Not built.
  _Archived 10 November 2026; superseded by Rail, 2026-11-10, open calls answered: the express and interchanges ("This turns the idea logged above ... into a direction to build")._
