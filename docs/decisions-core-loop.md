(1) Both branch areas are installed, High Gorge first, in a fixed order; the choice of order can return for Beta (recommended). (2) The route models about 3 h 11 solo against the six-hour target: add puzzles and discovery to the small areas, never larger bills (recommended). His note: "we dont need to author the puzzles now but it feels ok that someone could bum rush the game at 3 hours, but if they interact with the world, npcs, abilities, puzzles, it will be closer to 4-5". The orchestrator's reading: a three-hour rush is acceptable; the target for a player who engages is four to five hours, not six; puzzles are not authored during the greybox. (3) The Original Island at Alpha: the causeway ride, the meeting with The Boogler, one shared moment and a thanks card (recommended). His note: "don tknow about the thanks card, bu tmeeting the boogler is a fun ending potentially. although im interested in scoping a sort of post-game twist at some point, its cool when games do that". The orchestrator's reading: the meeting with The Boogler is the ending; the thanks card is undecided; a post-game twist is an idea to scope later ([ideas](ideas.md)). (4) The mainland is a backdrop only (recommended); High Port is the port you walk. (5) Saves start surviving updates at the start of the Alpha phase (recommended); wipes stay free through the phases before it. (6) The fresh-player session is at target quality after Ring Island build 2, on the built route (recommended). (7) Both remaining cuts stand (recommended): three double-backs instead of five (the sea cave, the first branch's hidden section, the train through the ring; the Closest Pew and Split-rock's upper seam move to Beta), and one upgrade per ability (hook reel, glider cloth) with no ladders and no bombs. **Changed:** (4) archives the undated "The mainland port city" entry (the 7 November backdrop call in [map-prototypes](world/map-prototypes.md) stands); (1) bends 2 November "The network: preset links, your choice of order" for Alpha, and (2) the 6 November "roughly six hours" target for a player who engages; each is a note on its rules line.
