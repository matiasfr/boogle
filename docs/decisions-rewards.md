## Decided

- <a id="d-2026-11-10-boogles-buy-town-upgrades-source-upgrades"></a>**2026-11-10, Boogles buy town upgrades, source upgrades and passenger cars; never engine or cargo parts, never materials** (owner, an hour after answering "Anything, including engine and cargo upgrades": "Maybe googles doesn’t buy train parts but does buy town upgrades? Or supply upgrades for resource sources?" [Boogles]; the orchestrator proposed recording "Boogles buy town upgrades, source upgrades and passenger cars, never engine or cargo parts, never materials" and he answered "Yes"). **Changed:** this replaces the same day's "What Boogles may buy: Anything, including engine and cargo upgrades" in the riders and Boogles entry below. Engine and cargo upgrades stay priced in materials, so steel, fibre and copper keep their use and nobody buys speed early. Town upgrades (more houses, opened buildings, a better station) make more riders and so more fares. Source upgrades change a rate (a bigger yard, faster regrowth, a loader); the room still hauls the material, and "materials and Boogles never convert" stands. Passenger cars and seats are bought with Boogles because they earn fares. No upgrade has two prices and no route step has a Boogle price. The [passenger-trade brief](brainstorms/passenger-trade.md) body still describes the earlier answer and is reworked before its model step.
- <a id="d-2026-11-10-open-calls-answered-riders-and-boogles"></a>**2026-11-10, open calls answered: riders and Boogles** (owner, on the decisions page, 10 November; "question: answer"). First step: "Add fares and seats to the economy model and the 2D page (about a day), then a flagged prototype on today's game: riders Village to Far Head, a till at Home, one car to buy". Where Boogles come from: "Fares only; materials and Boogles never convert". Fares while nobody is in the room: "Nothing runs; the next join finds one capped coin sack on the ticket counter that anyone empties, played for everyone". The Boogler's face on the coin: "Yes, his face; I'll decide his line later". **Changed from the recommendation:** What Boogles may buy: "Anything, including engine and cargo upgrades". Personal wearables: "The shop stays free". Settles [passenger-trade](brainstorms/passenger-trade.md)'s open questions 1 to 5 and the coin's face. **Supersedes** the brief's rule that Boogles never buy a part or a step of the main route: "Boogles never buy engine/cargo upgrades" was only that brief's proposal, never a decision, and is not adopted. The wallet's name (Boogles) and shared use (6 November) stand. Wearables stay free, so Fine Prices' "$0" joke stays. No line is written for The Boogler: his copy is untouched and his line is the owner's to decide later. Orchestrator's reading: "Anything" opens engine and cargo upgrades as Boogle purchases; "materials and Boogles never convert" still holds, so logs, steel and rails are not bought with Boogles, and whether a main-route gate may be bought with Boogles is not decided here (the brief's owner puts it to him).
- <a id="d-2026-11-10-the-currency-is-called-boogles-passenger"></a>**2026-11-10, the currency is called Boogles; passenger trade is under brainstorm** (owner: "I want to design a new potential mechanic. Passenger trains if purchased should have a benefit outside of just for players, as players max out once there are a few and don't need more space. It would be cool if the little guys take trains between cities and generate like trade revenue that provides a income source that can later be used for upgrades, so there's a fly wheel in addition to resources that is currency that can also be used. currency should also be \"Boogles\""). **Decided:** the shared wallet's currency is **Boogles** (his pick). It replaces the working name "Bucks" ([money](brainstorms/money.md), the 6 November "may cost Bucks", `dev/economy`'s `bucks` fields), which code and docs adopt when they next touch it. **Not decided:** little guys riding trains between towns and paying fares into the shared wallet as an income flywheel is an idea being brainstormed, not a decision to build; the shape, numbers and first build are the [passenger-trade](brainstorms/passenger-trade.md) brief's proposals, and its questions are open. The wallet itself stays decided (6 November) and unbuilt.

### Progress and rewards

- <a id="d-2026-11-06-economy-principles-amended"></a>**2026-11-06, economy principles amended** (owner approved the [principles audit](research/economy-principles-audit.md)'s wording, and added: "let's make progression completely shared. If there's a shared currency, the upgrades should apply for everyone"; "really tuned to 1 to 2 to 3, maybe 4 people, unlikely more than that ... The simplest is 1 to 3 people"; "we can still have The Boogler and the NPCs treat money in a comical way, but have real use cases for resources"; "the cosmetics can still be shared. Or maybe certain cosmetics and secret crates can be personal, which makes sense"). DESIGN.md 1, 6, 8, 9 and Server hosts every room changed. **One shared wallet** of town sales, anyone may spend it. **All progression is shared:** every upgrade and ability bought or earned (carry sling, powered pick, double jump) applies to everyone in the world. **Cosmetics:** most can be shared and are bought from the wallet (train looks, town and station decorations); what you wear from secret crates, and certain wearables, stay personal. **Money in dialogue** stays a joke; resources and the wallet do real work; NPC copy is unchanged ([conflicting lines](brainstorms/ring-and-economy.md#npc-lines-for-the-owner)). **Group size:** design and tune for 1–3 players, tolerate 4, don't design for more. **Paused world:** an empty room doesn't simulate; the next join applies one capped away credit to producers' yards and finishes the funded job. Supersedes "no shared wallets", "three things you earn", money as only a score, both "while nobody plays" entries and production core's offline contract; code follows after R1b ([orchestrator state](agents/orchestrator-state.md)).
- <a id="d-2026-11-01-resource-management-stays-light-never-survival"></a>**2026-11-01, resource management stays light, never survival-craft** (owner). The hotbar stayed for now, though he wasn't sure about it (changed 2026-11-02: cut, see "Decided" and "Changed").
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-harvesting-has-an-action-and-the"></a>**2026-11-02, harvesting has an action, and the magnet starts sooner** (owner: "we need some kind of action"; the animation "should be quick to do and satisfying"; "there's a delay before the magnetism happens on the resources. Maybe we can have that start sooner once you've pressed X on the resource"). A short swing plays the moment you press, and the pull follows straight after it. Goes into the harvest prototype, with [harvest-feel.md](research/harvest-feel.md) once it lands.
- <a id="d-2026-11-01-resources-will-be-harvested-not-picked"></a>**2026-11-01, resources will be harvested, not picked up** (direction, not built): wood comes from chopping trees and the like; what falls to the ground is collected with the magnet, perhaps differently per resource.
- <a id="d-2026-11-01-the-back-bundle-stays-the-way"></a>**2026-11-01, the back bundle stays the way to haul** ("Default is quite good"). The magnet he loved moves to pickups elsewhere, later, and pulls without a button.
- <a id="d-2026-11-01-double-jump-and-glide-are-unlocks"></a>**2026-11-01, double jump and glide are unlocks, not starting moves** ("abilities that should be unlocked later in the game"), for platforming and a sense of expanded movement. (research: [movement-abilities](research/movement-abilities.md))

- <a id="d-2026-11-01-carrying-is-a-simple-back-bundle"></a>**2026-11-01, carrying is a simple back bundle** (haul playtest, solo). Logs sit on the character's back in a compact bundle that fills out rather than towering; no sway, and so no jump spill (nothing would warn you of it). Don't over-index on Death Stranding's carrying. Replaces the wobbly stack, see Changed.

  - **Open:** how play feeds progress is left to the mini-games pass (below).
