- <a id="d-2026-11-11-boogles-prices-are-stand-ins-to"></a>**2026-11-11, Boogles: prices are stand-ins to refine from playtests; fares kept; each next house costs more** (owner, 11 November, after seeing the first trade model numbers). His question: "Would it help to consider that houses don't all have to cost the same? In general subsequent purchases can be more expensive". The orchestrator showed room B solo with each next house in a town costing double again (x1, x2, x4 on the brief's prices): mean wait per buy 9 to 17 minutes, fares lost to a full box 165,000 to 90,000, last town buy at hour 5.7 to 5.9, goals still 18 minutes after the route; fares at 0.4 times alone gave 50-minute waits, both together 86. It recommended keeping the fares, adopting the steeper curve and treating the late-game shortfall as a content question (more plots or dearer goals). His answer: "Okay, I'll take your recommendations. Let's go with these for now. Once we have more of the game established, we'll be able to refine based on playtesting." **Decided:** all Boogle prices and fares are stand-ins, to be refined from playtests once more of the game exists; the fare formula stays as it is; the model's default is a house-order multiplier of 2 (house n in a town costs the brief's base price times 2^(n-1), kept as one tunable, `house.order`). **Not decided:** what closes the late-game shortfall (more plots a town, dearer goals or other town goals): an open content question. The seat hauling the model found (about 59 minutes, 16% of the route) is untouched by this and stays an open finding. Not built.
