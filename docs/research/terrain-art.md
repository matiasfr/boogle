# Terrain art for the Place starter area

_Research report — 2026-11-05. Live primary-source research and source inspection at `68ee082d`. Scope: coastal landforms, nine game references, terrain authoring, materials, water contact, collision and device budgets. Research and a proposed plan only: no game changes, generated art, browser runs, fresh GPU measurements or owner playtest._

## Answer and evidence

Give Place a believable coastal structure, then simplify its shapes. Keep the heightfield for paths, track beds, beaches and broad land; add a small mesh kit where cliffs need vertical faces, broken silhouettes and ledges. Start with one west-cove cliff meeting a pocket beach. Choose the degree of realism in a matched previz before building the kit. More noise, a global resolution increase or a different water shader will not solve the present absence of an authored terrain composition.

The proposed common ground between realistic and stylized is **coherent geology at three scales**: headlands and coves; faces, strata and fallen blocks; restrained surface detail. The current warm stone and soft path edges remain the comparison baseline. The sea should reveal the shape beneath it, rather than conceal a land/water mismatch with foam.

Evidence labels: **[C]** inspected current code; **[P]** live primary source, including explicitly identified developer interviews; **[R]** earlier repository evidence, not rerun; **[H]** recommendation, inference or provisional budget. All measurements below say whether they are arithmetic, historical or proposed. A linked GDC abstract is not a watched talk; reference-game interpretations are not claims about their proprietary shaders.

## Geomorphology: a coast with causes

The following is a proposed landform grammar, not a simulated geological history. Pick a mostly bedded, warm sedimentary rock for the starter corner; make its layer orientation and block shapes continue from cliff to fallen rock to submerged ledge. The geology can be simplified without becoming arbitrary. **[H]**

| Landform                        | Physical explanation from primary sources                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Translation into Place                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sea cliff and wave-cut platform | Rock strength, layering and exposure affect cliffs. Erosion can leave a platform at their feet; sheltered platforms can carry shallow rocky reefs. [NPS rocky coasts](https://www.nps.gov/articles/rocky-coast-landforms.htm), [NPS Shag Rock](https://www.nps.gov/places/000/shag-rock-overlook.htm), [BGS Kimmeridge Bay](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/discovering-geology/maps-and-resources/office-geology/kimmeridge-bay-dorset/). **[P]** | Use a steep face, an occasional basal recess and an irregular seaward-sloping bench. Vary the bench along the shore; reserve a gentle exit where swimming meets the route. Do not give every face the same horizontal notch. **[H]**                                                                                                                             |
| Coves and headlands             | Wave refraction concentrates energy around projecting headlands; sheltered recesses can retain pocket beaches. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/rocky-coast-landforms.htm). **[P]**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | Let the landing occupy a recess between stronger shoulders. Curvature, shelter and sediment explain the beach; avoid a continuous sand ribbon around every cliff. **[H]**                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Stacks and caves                | Weaknesses admit erosion; isolated stacks can remain after headland or arch breakdown. [NPS](https://www.nps.gov/articles/rocky-coast-landforms.htm). **[P]**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | A future stack should resemble the adjacent headland's rock and strata. One readable landmark beats many unrelated spikes. Caves and traversable arches require a separate physical-surface design; omit them from the first pilot. **[H]**                                                                                                                      |
| Strata and submerged structure  | Hard/soft bedrock differences help explain coastal relief; mapped offshore bedrock continues the onshore structure. The seabed is not universally sand. [British Geological Survey, Jurassic Coast](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/geology-projects/sea-floor-marine-geoscience/seamless-bedrock-map-of-the-jurassic-coast/). **[P]**                                                                                                             | Agree a layer frame and two or three unequal bed thicknesses. Continue them through neighbouring kit pieces and waterline rocks. Use broad resistant shelves with softer recesses, not evenly spaced painted stripes. **[H]**                                                                                                                                    |
| River gorge                     | Flowing water and transported sediment cut channels; gradient, confinement and differing rock resistance shape the result. [NPS New River geology](https://www.nps.gov/neri/learn/nature/geology.htm). **[P]**                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | A river needs a connected drainage direction, upstream source and downstream outlet. Today's three sea-level arms are better treated provisionally as a drowned gorge/tidal inlet than three arbitrary rivers flowing away from a peak. Retain gameplay geometry; resolve the intended hydrology with the macro plan before adding rapids or waterfalls. **[H]** |
| Scree and fallen blocks         | Rockfalls move fragments downslope from exposed rock; talus is evidence of that supply. [Utah Geological Survey](https://geology.utah.gov/hazards/landslides/rockfalls/). **[P]**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Put angular blocks below plausible fractured faces, with a few larger pieces at the toe and quiet aggregate between them. Wave-exposed toes can be cleaner than sheltered ones. Keep the walking surface broad; scree is not a field of capsule-catching collision triangles. **[H]**                                                                            |
| Beaches and shallow shelves     | Waves, currents and sediment supply redistribute sand; beaches have landward boundaries and locally varying profiles. [NPS sandy coasts](https://www.nps.gov/articles/sandy-coast-landforms.htm). **[P]**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Give the cove a dry upper beach, wet lower slope and submerged continuation. Allow exposed rock beside it and sand collected in depressions. Make depth colour follow those actual forms. Do not flatten all shallow water or turn the entire starter area tropical by adding white sand. **[H]**                                                                |

**Composition before realism:** work in plan and section together. Draw the cliff crest, beach limit, low-water bench, submerged break, path and rail envelope on one map. Then choose the visible silhouette from arrival, the first uphill turn and the train. A physically plausible coast can still hide the route; the map must satisfy both geology and [“Telegraph everything” / “Show, don't tell”](../../DESIGN.md). **[H]**

## Representation: spend geometry where heightfields fail

| Approach                           | Useful capability                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Cost and recommendation                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Heightfield plus cliff kit**     | Efficient broad ground and grade control; meshes provide near-vertical faces, caps and isolated boulders. [Rapier](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/colliders/) documents heightfield storage advantages and fixed trimesh use. | Preferred. Keep the current heightfield authoritative for the route. A non-overhanging face can cover a steep backing slope, provided the collision boundary and silhouette agree. A hidden slope must not block a visible recess. **[H]**                                  |
| **Local SDF/density mesh**         | Density fields represent caves and overhangs; [Geiss, GPU Gems 3](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-i-geometry/chapter-1-generating-complex-procedural-terrains-using-gpu) describes polygonizing them.                     | Useful offline for one difficult arch or eroded mass after previz. Bake to an ordinary mesh and collision; do not ship a runtime volume system for this pilot. **[H]**                                                                                                      |
| **Whole-area voxels/SDF with LOD** | Arbitrary topology and excavation.                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Sampling volume, meshing, collision rebuilds and LOD transitions create a new terrain system. [Transvoxel](https://transvoxel.org/) exists specifically to solve differing-resolution mesh transitions. No current starter-area requirement justifies this scope. **[P/H]** |
| **Finer heightfield everywhere**   | Smoother slopes, denser boundary paint.                                                                                                                                                                                                      | It still cannot represent two floors at one x/z. It multiplies geometry on already quiet areas and does not author a cliff. Prefer a bounded patch if an approved silhouette actually requires it. **[H]**                                                                  |

Standard engines reinforce the division. [Unreal's Landscape guide](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/landscape-technical-guide-in-unreal-engine) separates component/LOD choices from collision costs. [Godot's heightmap shape](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_heightmapshape3d.html) documents the overhang limitation. [Unity's terrain holes](https://docs.unity.com/en-us/engine/6000.0/manual/creating-environments/script-terrain/terrain-tools/terrain-paint-holes) coordinate visual holes with other systems. Boogle's fragment masks do not provide that coordination, and Godot's support for NaN holes is **not** evidence that Boogle's Rapier facade supports them. **[P/C]**

Proposed first kit: straight face, convex shoulder, concave join, crest/cap transition, sloping foot/bench, detached block. Make two or three controlled silhouette variants only after joins work. Share a bedding frame, metres-per-unit, contact edges and material vocabulary. Rotate around the vertical axis within geological limits; arbitrary rotation or extreme nonuniform scaling can make strata and fractures nonsensical. The pilot needs only the subset its shape uses. **[H]**

The authoring result should have one inspectable physical description producing render boundaries, collision, seabed input where applicable, material masks and no-dressing corridors. This extends the existing numeric [Place bake](../../src/proto/place/terrain.ts) and [island stamp](../../src/island/stamps.ts) patterns; it is not a proposal to derive gameplay from a scene mesh. A render LOD may simplify interior faces, but shared borders, walkable edges and water contact must stay fixed. **[H]**

## Materials: broad forms first, quiet detail last

Use a scale ladder rather than equal-strength noise at every frequency. Starting comparison ranges are **proposals**: 20–60 m land masses; 4–8 m major face breaks; 0.3–2 m joints and blocks; centimetre-scale grain expressed only in shading. These are art handles, not geological constants or instructions to tessellate all four scales. Check them against Buddy and the normal camera. **[H]**

Retain the palette and soft transitions in [Place look](../../src/proto/place/look.ts). Introduce explicit grass/rock/sand/path coverage when implementing, instead of treating painted colour as enduring material identity. Slope can favor exposed rock and gravity can favor deposited sediment, but slope alone cannot distinguish a worn path, vertical beach backwall or submerged rock. Combine authored coverage, broad geology and slope; let path coverage override noise without making its edge razor sharp. **[H]**

Begin with vertex colour and low-frequency material variation. Compare a texture-free baseline against **one shared packed pattern** only if the close view needs it. Apply triplanar sampling to the rock class where UV stretch warrants it. The installed `three/src/nodes/utils/TriplanarTextures.js` uses three projection samples, normalized absolute-normal weights and local coordinates by default; pass a common world/island frame for separate cliff pieces. The [TSL API](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/TSL.html) and [Geiss's triplanar explanation](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-i-geometry/chapter-1-generating-complex-procedural-terrains-using-gpu) support this approach. Tangent-space normal projections require reorientation before blending; a naïve RGB average is wrong. **[C/P/H]**

Do not sample four full albedo/normal/roughness stacks everywhere. Share packed detail, use suitable colour spaces, mipmaps and distance/footprint filtering, and fade fine contrast before it shimmers on a train ride. Keep derivatives in uniform control flow under the [TSL rendering contract](../../src/render/reference/tsl.md). Avoid a new post pass, global AO, parallax displacement or displacement that changes apparent foot contact. Lighting and a coherent silhouette should carry the corner before a texture is approved. **[H]**

## The shore is a shared boundary

Four related effects need different inputs; using one mask for all four makes a coast less convincing.

1. **Depth colour and underwater visibility:** the finished submerged surface supplies bathymetry. Preserve [Place water's](../../src/proto/place/water.ts) triangle-aware bed and provider validity bounds; never register a cliff roof or rail deck as seabed. A real rock shelf should continue through the waterline rather than end at the visible beach. **[C/H]**
2. **Breaking/swashing foam:** a shallow, gently sloped beach and an exposed rock wall need different treatment. Keep sheltered-cove foam restrained and spatially tied to the existing wave/contact system. Rare's [paper](https://history.siggraph.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/2018-Talks-Ang_The-Technical-Art-of-Sea-of-Thieves.pdf) is evidence for distinguishing wave and intersection foam, not for adding another screen-space pass. **[P/H]**
3. **Wet substrate:** lower-beach colour/roughness should respond continuously near the waterline. Build the dry material first, then preserve [water receiver](../../src/render/water/receivers.ts) patches for wetness, swash, absorption and caustics. The current continuous wet normal/roughness behavior fixes an earlier pale-triangle problem. Its up-facing masks do not guarantee convincing wetness on vertical cliff faces; test a small rock-specific receiver adaptation only if needed. **[C/R/H]**
4. **Waterline rocks:** a large solid rock needs a physical collider and appropriate wave obstruction/contact; a decorative tiny pebble does not need to consume a global contact entry. [Shore providers](../../src/render/water/shore-providers.ts) support bounded bed data and separate obstacles; [contacts](../../src/render/water/contacts.ts) has a finite list traversed in the shader. Batch or omit insignificant contacts rather than registering every fragment. **[C/H]**

A roughly 2 m bed core cannot promise faithful 20 cm rock contours. Do not solve that by raising the entire atlas resolution or inserting an overlapping provider and assuming it wins: current providers are sorted and first-match, not a general overlay-priority system. First use the existing object-contact/obstacle route for isolated rocks. If a locally refined physical shelf truly requires finer bathymetry, design its sampling and seam behavior explicitly in that later task. No false shallow platform to produce turquoise water. **[C/H]**

## Collision, navigation and resource budgets

The physical proof is more demanding than “the height values match.” Validate mesh diagonal, heightfield axis order, transformed cliff geometry, shared borders and actual capsule/ball contacts. [Rapier](https://rapier.rs/docs/user_guides/javascript/colliders/) recommends fixed rather than dynamic triangle meshes and documents internal-edge handling. Boogle already uses internal-edge/duplicate-vertex flags in [the backend](../../src/physics/backend-rapier.ts); those flags do not weld independent colliders or repair an invisible backing slope. **[P/C/H]**

Keep simpler collision away from touchable silhouettes. At foot level, preserve the visible standing edge and eliminate little snagging teeth; large climbable-looking blocks must be solid. Do not leave double floors, tiny wedges between meshes, a smooth invisible ramp over visually deep scree, or a viewable alcove blocked by heightfield earth. The first pilot can avoid overhangs entirely. If later geometry creates stacked floors, `H(x,z)` is no longer enough: consumers need a downward physical query with elevation/surface context, rather than always snapping to the top. **[H]**

The current [level checker](../../dev/level/check.ts) **does include Place**, unlike an older report's inventory. Its 2 m flood and authored traversal links are useful broad-route checks, not swept character, camera, train or prop-collision proof. The [spatial checker](../../dev/level/spatial.ts) has limited creator coverage; `--physics` currently exposes T3/stack regions, not a shore region. Extend those existing adapters with the pilot's real collision and coverage reporting before claiming physical validation. Missing geometry is inconclusive. There is no new general navmesh project in this plan. **[C/H]**

### Proposed budgets, not measured capacity

Apple's [WebGPU guidance](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2025/236/) emphasizes resource reuse, fewer passes, avoiding unnecessary updates and memory bandwidth on its tile-based GPUs. This supports static terrain buffers, shared materials and bounded detail; it does not provide Boogle frame-time predictions. Preserve full precision where terrain/seam coordinates require it. **[P/H]**

## Numbered recommendations for Boogle

1. **Author a coastal structure before surface detail.** Draw one coherent headland/cove/bench/bed system over [Place layout](../../src/proto/place/layout.ts), [terrain](../../src/proto/place/terrain.ts) and [shore layout](../../src/proto/place/shore-layout.ts). Treat the current gorge as a drowned inlet provisionally; resolve any river story before adding directed flow. Preserve existing progression and the [low-island plan's](../plans/island-low.md) separate scope.
2. **Choose the style with a matched two-direction previz.** Compare naturalistic bedded rock with simplified broad planes at the same corner, using current Buddy, camera and lighting. Follow [owner calls](../agents/owner-calls.md); keep the current soft paths and water. Neither this report nor an attractive generated image approves a new world style.
3. **Use heightfield plus a bounded cliff kit.** Retain [land.ts](../../src/proto/place/land.ts) for grades and beaches; add only the face/cap/foot pieces the pilot needs. Reserve SDFs for later offline special pieces, and reject global voxel conversion or global tessellation for this task.
4. **Make visual, physical and submerged surfaces agree.** Extend the existing [terrain bake](../../src/proto/place/terrain.ts) rather than hiding displacement in a material. Preserve catalog inputs and [triangle-aware water](../../src/proto/place/water.ts); audit physical height-query consumers and add the pilot to [existing spatial checks](../../dev/level/spatial.ts). Do not claim uncovered collision is tested.
5. **Build materials from explicit coverage and a shared geological frame.** Evolve [groundMaterial](../../src/proto/place/look.ts) with quiet macro variation, broad beds and soft authored paths; compare zero textures with one packed pattern. Keep high-frequency detail optional and preserve [receiver patches](../../src/render/water/receivers.ts).
6. **Separate seabed, wetness, swash and rock contact.** Use [shore providers](../../src/render/water/shore-providers.ts) for actual bed/obstruction and [contacts](../../src/render/water/contacts.ts) for bounded object effects. Extend exposed bedrock beneath the water, retain sand in sheltered pockets, and avoid a universal foam or sand ring.
7. **Prove one corner before rolling out the coast.** Hold provisional geometry/memory/time caps, inspect normal-camera stills and movement, exercise paths/track/swim recovery, then stop for the owner's playtest under [verification](../agents/verification.md). Passing checks establishes coverage, not aesthetic acceptance or fun.

### What agents can author

Agents can produce a georeferenced macro plan, protected-corridor overlays, sections, parameter tables, numbered comparison views and a source/collision/bed manifest. After the selected direction is authorized for implementation, they can author parametric Blender recipes or code meshes for bounded kit pieces; assemble them against the current game; bake licensed/original masks; export simplified collision and LODs; extend existing coverage checks; and record matched visual/performance evidence. Keep editable sources, units, pivots, seeds, shared edges and exported hashes together. No ROM assets or extracted reference-game geometry/textures enter the workflow.

The agent's responsibility is reproducibility and honest evidence, not deciding that the art is accepted. Preserve resource IDs, existing copy and progression. An offline tool is optional; a new tool subscription or generalized terrain editor is not a dependency for the corner.

### What needs previz first

Use an actual current-game screenshot as the anchor and preserve Buddy, camera, route, structures and water. Compare **A: naturalistic bedded warm stone** with **B: fewer, larger stylized planes using the same geology**. Include one arrival composition and one close shoulder/foot view. Hold lighting constant; keep the accepted baseline alongside both. Label any image-generated geometry as illustrative and physically unproven.

Before modelling selected pieces, provide front/side/back and contact-detail references at metre scale, including the grass cap, layer direction and underwater continuation. Any cave, arch, sea stack, large gorge silhouette replacement or changed route needs its own spatial previz. Do not let a heroic free-camera shot replace the normal player view.

### How it stays playable

Freeze the landing pad and its blend, path cores **and shoulders**, rail formation and train clearance, bridge approaches, build interactions and catalog exclusions. In Place, the rail's 4/8 m shaping distances and path widths are lower bounds on existing authoring intent, not permission to squeeze other collision into them. Account for Buddy, carried objects, balls, the follow camera and train overhang; inspect the actual swept envelope before placing a cliff.

Test the route both directions, standing at the waterline, swimming out, falling from the rim, rolling a ball along seams, and camera movement past the face. Inspect resource bases and reach points after terrain changes. Use the existing Place route checks plus explicitly extended physical coverage, and retain an old-build comparison. Cosmetic quality levels may remove grain and tiny dressing, but never a floor or a solid blocker. Keep water/render animation Local; no new room/save state is required by this proposal.

### First pilot: one west-cove cliff and shore corner

Choose a **24–32 m coastal reach on the cove's positive-z shoulder**, beside rather than across the climb to town. A provisional search box is local x `-220…-180`, z `26…58` (world x `780…820`); this is a candidate area, **not an inspected or approved placement**. Final selection requires a current screenshot, slope/height section and overlap check against the landing fade and every path shoulder. Move or shrink the pilot if the protections consume it; do not move the route to accommodate the art.

The complete pilot contains one broken cliff face and cap, one irregular rock foot continuing under the sea, a small pocket-beach transition, two or three substantial waterline rocks and a restrained scree patch. No cave, arch, new route, new water system or entire-coast dressing. First compare untextured shapes; then the selected material; then wet contact. Each step must retain the previous comparison.

Deliver a source/asset manifest; matching render/collision/bed evidence; normal arrival, uphill-return, low-water and reverse views; motion evidence for seams and shimmer with its viewing limits stated; matched Mac and named mobile measurements; and route/swim/camera/ball results with missing coverage listed. If the corner fails, revise it locally. If it passes the technical checks, stop at the owner playtest: **“Does this shore feel like a place you want to walk into?”** The answer, with route clarity observed in play, decides whether the grammar extends to the rest of the starter area.
