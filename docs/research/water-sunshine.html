Sunshine water board

Own diagrams and a toy renderer for the plan in docs/plans/water-sunshine.md. No game frames, no textures from any game: the wave texture here is generated noise. Costs are from code, not measured.

1. Two scrolling layers and a sparkle band

A tiny ray-cast sea at low resolution. One generated wave-cell texture is sampled twice with different scrolls; the second is added at double weight. The band of sparkle is picked by how much texture one pixel covers. Switch Band source to see why the plan computes that footprint instead of relying on authored mip levels: with authored mips, the band moves when the resolution changes.

2. Passes per frame

Each red bar is a pass split: the scene target is stored and reloaded so the water can read it. On the owner's Mac (4× MSAA, 5.7 MP) one split moves about 275 MB; on a phone without MSAA, about 7 MB.
