# Boogle v2 — settled calls (do not re-open)

Progression
- A preset railway (like Death Stranding 2's highways), built in short sections and upgraded through about 7 ages: wood, iron, steel, diesel/concrete, electric, monorail, maglev.
- Stations bring goofy characters. Ancient stones rising reveal the route (leaning).
- Each age's own track-laying machine lays a stocked section. One player starts the run; everyone sees it.
- A Big Walk-style miniature model of the island at stations is a display only.
- Ages advance through discoveries and a room-wide evolution, not by reaching a station.
- About 4 hours for a group (Grow Home pacing), 8 at most. Incremental-game feel. Many stations, short sections.
- Nothing decays, breaks or needs repair. No hazards, no timers. Mini-games never gate progress.

Resource loop
- Haul stack: one material per age, carried on your back as a wobbly stack. Soft cap 20: past it the stack wobbles and spills on a jump. This is decided; tune it in playtesting.
- Wagons are part of the loop: you and the helpers load them, from the diesel age onwards.
- Pour by walking into a work site's ring. Each site has a Track ring and a helper-hut ring.
- Helpers are tiny visible workers who chop, carry in lines, push wagons and tip loads into sites. They work offline, capped at one section's worth.
- Three investments: which route next; track or helpers; personal upgrades from contribution tiers (like ranks in a guild).
- Your stack is your own. Costs are fixed; more players just go faster.
- The beach ball is a toy only and never produces income. Materials use real names for now.
- NEW REQUIREMENT: every system must be ignorable. A player who never pours, only rides, bops the ball and plays mini-games must:
  (a) never be blocked from any place or toy;
  (b) still see progress happen around them;
  (c) never feel judged (plaques give credit only; no "slacker" readout).

Don't re-propose
- A currency that sprays from everything ("Boogs").
- Groves or anything driven by a timer.
- Income from the ball.
- Digging, the water sim or wash-ups: all were removed from the game on 2026-10-30.

Toys that exist today (docs/game-design.md on origin/main)
- Palms: shake for fruit, which regrows after 4 minutes.
- Throwing, with scripted flight.
- The beach ball: Owner physics; it knocks fruit loose.
- Mushrooms: plant, water and bounce. Seeds and the watering can come from the stall.
- Windmill sails: solid, they bat the ball.
- Caves: a cosmetic mine, an underground pond and a wine cellar.
- The lighthouse frisbee perch: refills 60 s after the frisbee is taken.
- The Boogler: friendship and launches.
- Critters, day and night, honk and ping, the dino and the guest.
