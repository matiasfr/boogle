# Breath of the Wild paraglider: numeric comparison targets

_Research report, 2 November 2026. A source audit and plotting brief for the BotW-like glide in [round 4, step 5](../plans/round-4.md), extending [movement-abilities.md](movement-abilities.md). Live searches covered papers, speedrun techniques, public actor/camera parameter documentation, reverse-engineering code, mods and measurement videos, including Tears of the Kingdom. Boogle code was checked at `eedddacf`. No game was played or frame-counted in this session. Published measurements were inspected, not independently replicated; proposed curves below are Boogle hypotheses, not recovered Nintendo algorithms._

## What an agent can rely on

**BotW's neutral descent has a published measurement; its normal camera has useful datamined settings. A complete measured BotW glide curve was not found.** TotK supplies a useful forward-flight measurement, but it cannot fill BotW's missing cells as fact. Start the Boogle comparison at **10 m/s forward and 3.2 m/s down**, explicitly a proposed hybrid reference, and keep the unknown transients visibly separate from those anchors.

This serves [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md)'s **Toys before chores** and **Slapstick over precision**: height should buy enjoyable, forgiving travel. The [owner's decision](../proposals/boogle-2.md) is to match BotW closely, in Boogle's own way, as a later unlock. This report proposes tuning, changes no controller or camera, and does not finish round 4's combined research-and-pictures track.

### Labels, units and confidence

- **M — measured:** an identified author reports an in-game experiment. It does not mean Nintendo supplied it or that we reproduced it.
- **D — datamined:** a literal setting in a public parameter listing. Confidence in the literal can be high while confidence in its physical interpretation is low.
- **E — estimated:** an assumption, proxy or conditional unit conversion. **C — calculated:** arithmetic from stated inputs. **H — hypothesis:** a proposed Boogle target. Unknown means no defensible value was established by this search.
- **High / medium / low** describe confidence for the stated use, not statistical confidence intervals. An unknown cell has unresolved confidence, not a zero-valued measurement. Proposed tolerances are engineering comparison bands, never error bars on a source.

**Scale S1:** Leicester uses the BotW flight mini-game's displayed metres. **Scale S2:** TotK experiments take one displayed coordinate unit as one metre; [the run-speed experiment][run] explicitly relates this to BotW's distance mini-game, but publishes no calibration trace. **Scale S3:** interpret spatial BotW actor/camera units as metres, consistent with that convention; parameter names alone do not establish a time unit. All seconds here are real elapsed gameplay seconds, excluding pauses, rather than the accelerated day/night clock. Do not rescale by a guessed real-world height for Link, and do not multiply every actor value by 30 just because a capture runs at 30 fps.

## What the sources actually establish

### Measured descent and forward flight

McKee et al.'s 2019 student paper, [_A1_3 Paragliding through Hyrule_][paper], deliberately removes horizontal movement. It publishes approximate height/time and a visual settling judgment, without a velocity trace, uncertainty interval or game patch. Its discussion of aerodynamic lift is a modelling assumption, not a demonstration that forward input changes the game's sink. Its separately assumed gravity is not required to use the measured descent.

In [elprophet's TotK experiment, 19 May 2023][glide], the author walks off the Temple of Time, trims a clip to 10 s and reads the coordinate endpoints. The paraglider row goes from `(453, −626, 1472)` to `(455, −529, 1438)` in the author's horizontal/horizontal/altitude notation: approximately 97 m horizontally and 34 m down. This is a segment average including deployment, not an instantaneous cruise cap. Wind, patch, repeats and exact opening latency are undocumented. The post's claim that forward input reduces sink concerns its **skydiving rows**, not a neutral-versus-forward paraglider comparison.

**TotK has not been shown here to sink 0.2 m/s faster than BotW.** These experiments differ in input, timing and measurement precision. No controlled cross-game comparison was found. The [TotK run report][run] supplies useful scale checks, but its claim of exactness exceeds its disclosed method; use 5 and 8 m/s as approximate community measurements.

### The useful datamine is in the action, not just GeneralParamList

The [ZeldaMods format documentation][baiprog] identifies AIProgram as the actor's action/AI parameters. The inspected public snapshot is pinned to `f50b984f5e645dcf71b1c6340a8834bb1dee0153`; its game platform/patch was not established here. In `Player_Link`, inspect **Action_35, `PlayerParashawlGlide`**, rather than the similarly named Cucco action. `GlideSpeedMax` is 0.02 and `Lv2GlideSpeedMax` is 0.35; turn fields include max 1, min 0.1, rate 0.2 and speed 20; overspeed decay is 0.002. These are **D literals with unresolved units**, not m/s, m/s² or degrees/s. [Public parameter listing][player-params].

The [reverse-engineered glide base][glide-code] loads those fields but does not expose the full movement calculation in the inspected file. Thus **10.5 m/s is a conditional estimate**, not a datamined speed in SI units. `GlideRotSpeed = 20` must not become “20°/s”; `GlideSpeedMax = 0.02` must not be renamed an acceleration. Neither the meaning of “Lv2” nor the relevant time normalization was verified.

The actor's [GeneralParamList][general-params] contains `MaxSpeedInAir = 0.3` and `LookPosOffsetY = 1.5`; neither proves glide speed or camera lens height. [Bgparamlist documentation][bgparam] also distinguishes defaults from actor-specific settings. This is why searching only that file, or applying a blanket ×30 conversion, produces misleading precision. The incomplete [jump implementation][jump-code] likewise does not turn the base jump-height field into a verified moving-jump apex.

For camera work, [the generated CameraChase documentation][camera-doc] corroborates the preset fields. The radius is an orbit/target quantity: use the 5.3 m setting and 50° vertical lens as a **reference framing preset**, not as an observed glide shot. A Y offset is not the camera's height above terrain or Link's feet. The file does not establish a glide pullback, bank, pitch offset or FOV ramp.

### Community and mod evidence: useful boundaries, missing curves

- BotW runners document that a [stasis launch can retain speed after the paraglider opens][stasis]. A normal cruise setting therefore need not be a universal hard clamp. This supplies no normal acceleration or decay rate.
- TotK runners document [Tulin pumping][tulin]: close with B and reopen with X near the end of a gust to preserve speed. Exclude gusts and pumping from a stock, unpowered comparison; do not treat releasing the deployment button, releasing the movement stick and closing the canopy as the same event.
- A [10× paraglider mod][speed-mod] documents a multiplier and increased rotation sensitivity, but no calibrated vanilla SI baseline. Mod/free-camera settings are not evidence of the shipped follow camera.
- [hyper_T's Cucco/paraglider comparison][cucco] shows approximately matching flight and descent in TotK. Its 0.3 gravity multiplier is for `KokkoFlyingGravity`; it is not a verified paraglider sink or evidence of 0.3× ordinary player gravity.
- [YukinoSan's BotW speed experiment][speed-video] is a strong follow-up lead: its indexed description specifies OCR, a 100 m course and Switch 1.6.0. Video playback could not be inspected here; no number was transcribed or inferred from the title. The [Nintendo GDC session][gdc] was located, but its full presentation was not watched and supplies no numerical evidence to this report.

No defensible source found in this search supplies BotW's normal acceleration/braking trace, steering step response, fast-fall opening trace, close transition or glide-specific camera curve. This is a limit of the verified evidence, not a claim that no recording or unpublished research exists anywhere.

## Boogle's starting point and the comparison contract

At this checkout, [player state and steering](../../src/entities/player/state.ts) use **5.6 m/s walk, 9.5 m/s sprint, 14 m/s² air steering and a 20 m/s fall cap**. [Constants](../../src/constants.ts) give 60 Hz physics, gravity 18 m/s² and jump launch 7.6 m/s; falling uses 1.7× gravity, or **30.6 m/s²**. There is no active glide in that controller. Its held-jump apex is approximately **1.68 m** under the current piecewise gravity, a continuous calculation rather than a measured run; a direct copy of the nominal 0.58 m BotW field would change unrelated platforming.

The [camera state](../../src/player/state.ts) starts at radius **5.2 m**, pitch **−0.22 rad**. The [camera entity](../../src/entities/camera/index.ts) places its pivot **1.35 m above the body**, **0.55 m to the side**, positions the lens behind it and aims a further **8 m** along the forward vector. [The lens](../../src/render/scene.ts) is **62° vertical**, not 62° horizontal. The existing [collision arm](../../src/player/camera-arm.ts) pulls in immediately, holds for **0.12 s**, then recovers with a **0.3 s time constant**. These are Boogle facts, not BotW measurements.

The earlier [movement proposal](movement-abilities.md#glide-spend-height-choose-the-landing) starts at 8/2.5 m/s, horizontal acceleration/braking 18/12 m/s², vertical time constant 0.12 s with an 80 m/s² upward cap, and a 6.5 m glide camera. **Keep that as a named feel variant; do not call it the measured BotW reference.** Its ratio is 3.2:1, close to the proposed 10/3.2 ratio, despite taking longer to cross the same distance. Ratio alone cannot establish matching feel.

An exponential time constant of 0.12 s takes `−ln(0.05) × 0.12 = 0.36 s` to remove 95% of a velocity error before acceleration limits. An 80 m/s² cap alone requires at least `(20 − 3.2) / 80 = 0.21 s` to arrest a −20 m/s fall; easing adds time. Neither is interchangeable with the paper's 0.067–0.133 s observation.

### Proposed curves an agent can plot

**Everything in this subsection is H/C unless explicitly marked otherwise.** These are provisional output targets, not a claim about Nintendo's controller implementation. They refine the comparison branch of the earlier proposal without changing the approved unlock sequence. All tolerances below assume level input, still air, no collision, no moving platform and normal time; collisions and externally supplied momentum need separate traces.

Let `t` be seconds after the named event, `h` be metres above a fixed starting datum, `v` horizontal speed in m/s, `vy` up-positive vertical speed, and `u` normalized stick magnitude. Start with `V = 10`, `S = 3.2`. At Boogle's 5.6 m/s walk, flight is **1.79× walk**; for a game walking at 5–6 m/s it is **1.67–2×**. Keep that absolute starting target instead of automatically scaling the uncertain BotW numbers by character height or sprint speed.

For the deploy curve, define `t = 0` at **physical response onset**, and log the command-to-onset interval separately. Before `T`, integrate height as `h(t) = h0 + v0 t + (−3.2 − v0)T(q³ − q⁴/2)`; afterwards continue at −3.2 m/s. At `T = 0.10 s`, losses are **0.16 m from v0 = 0**, **0.66 m from −10**, and **1.16 m from −20**. Allowing one fixed step of −20 m/s fall and `T ≤ 0.12 s` gives about **1.73 m**; use **1.8 m maximum command-to-settled loss** as a provisional fast-fall target. This is an output envelope, not a promise of survival when opened below that clearance.

Opening while rising should arm the glide until descent, preserving the existing jump rather than injecting lift. Repeated opening must not restart a transition in a way that extends it forever: **continuously open is the upper height envelope** for the same descending initial state and input. Test rapid close/reopen against that envelope; merely asserting `vy ≤ 0` would still allow an infinite slow-fall exploit. Input release alone leaves a toggle open; distinguish that from the explicit close event in logs.

For camera-height comparisons, retain Boogle's existing body-relative pivot first. At pitch −0.22 and radius 5.3, the unobstructed lens is **2.51 m above the body origin**, about **5.77 m from that origin** including the shoulder offset; neither equals the nominal radius. ### Range, scale and what to measure next

The proposed settled ratio is **3.125:1**: a 10 m drop buys **31.25 m** of ideal horizontal flight. That says nothing about deployment, turning, acceleration or landing clearance. As a conservative first layout estimate, reserve 2 m vertically and 20% horizontally as in the earlier proposal: `(drop − 2) × 3.125 × 0.8`, then subtract the entry-acceleration deficit. From rest with 18 m/s² acceleration that deficit is approximately **2.78 m** if the flight lasts at least 0.556 s. A 16 m drop therefore suggests **32.2 m** before turn/obstacle costs, compatible with testing the earlier 30 m return landing, not proof it is reachable. Keep actual route certification with [the place layout](../../src/proto/place/layout.ts) and [level checks](../../dev/level/check.ts).

The missing Zelda evidence needs matched trials, not another guessed constant: same platform/patch, flat takeoff, no bonuses or gusts, fixed camera for movement trials, and several repeats of neutral/forward descent, 90°/180° input steps, rest-to-forward, forward-to-neutral, opening at known low/high descent speeds and explicit closing. Record native frames and input timestamps. Report the first response, 50/90/95% response, steady segment and lost height separately. Integer HUD coordinates over ten seconds can estimate mean speed; they cannot resolve a 0.1 s deployment curve. [TotK Speedometer][speedometer] samples OCR about three times per second and averages ten readings, useful for cruise but inadequate for those transients.

For Boogle, use the existing [simulation harness](../../dev/sim/README.md) for fixed-step position/velocity samples and [look workflow](../../.claude/skills/look/SKILL.md) for real-time motion and actual lens positions. Compare local physics, not remote 20 Hz interpolated presence. A plot should identify whether each line is **published M, parameter-derived E, or proposed H**; a straight line fitted between TotK's endpoints is not a recovered acceleration curve. This report adds no tests or harness: implementation should follow the repository's test diet and record the actual traces when the ability exists.

## Numbered recommendations for Boogle

1. **Use 10 ±0.5 m/s forward and 3.2 ±0.3 m/s sink as a clearly labeled comparison hypothesis**, with a 3.125:1 nominal ratio; retain 8/2.5 as the slower feel variant ([round 4](../plans/round-4.md), [movement proposal](movement-abilities.md)).
2. **Plot acceleration and neutral braking separately**, starting at 18/12 m/s², 0.50 s to 90% speed and 0.83 s to stop; allow ±0.10 s and keep neutral sink within 0.2 m/s of moving sink ([player steering](../../src/entities/player/state.ts)).
3. **Try heading response capped at 360°/s with a 0.12 s error time constant**, reaching within 5° of a 90° turn in 0.39 ±0.10 s without more than 10% speed loss; mark it unmeasured in BotW ([player intent and steering](../../src/entities/player/state.ts)).
4. **Target physical opening within one fixed step and settled sink by 0.15 s**, checking 0/−10/−20 m/s entry speeds, ≤1.8 m fast-fall height loss and no height advantage from repeated toggling ([player state](../../src/entities/player/state.ts), [fixed clock](../../src/constants.ts)).
5. **Preserve velocity on explicit close, then restore Boogle's ordinary air steering and fall curve**, keeping toggle-button release and neutral stick separate; leave existing walk, sprint and jump scale intact ([player state](../../src/entities/player/state.ts), [controls](../../src/player/controls.ts)).
6. **A/B the 5.3 m / 50° reference framing against Boogle's 62° lens and optional 6.5 m pullback**, plotting actual lens height/distance as well as nominal radius; no BotW glide transition has been established ([camera](../../src/entities/camera/index.ts), [lens](../../src/render/scene.ts), [collision arm](../../src/player/camera-arm.ts)).
7. **Budget crossings from measured height loss and entry speed, with a 2 m vertical reserve and 20% horizontal margin**, then verify the 30 m return landing instead of treating glide ratio as a reachability guarantee ([movement layout proposal](movement-abilities.md), [place layout](../../src/proto/place/layout.ts), [level checks](../../dev/level/check.ts)).
8. **Capture fixed-step curves and real-time motion before accepting fidelity**, labeling every reference line and keeping the missing BotW transients open; the owner's single gut question is “Does height buy the same easy freedom to choose a landing?” ([simulation](../../dev/sim/README.md), [look](../../.claude/skills/look/SKILL.md), [round 4](../plans/round-4.md)).
