# 2. Discovery as progression for Boogle 2.0

*Research for the eight-era archipelago concept, 30 October 2026. **[V]**: matched word for word on the linked page. **[S]**: from secondary coverage, a reprint or a wiki. Check [S] wording before reusing it.*

**In short:** games that make discovery the progression either make **knowledge the key** (Outer Wilds, Tunic, The Witness, Fez) or make **finding things feed a tech tree** (Subnautica, Minecraft, Valheim, Pikmin). Boogle can use both:
- Discoveries become **leads**. Any 3 leads open an era's blueprint, the island builds it together, and the world changes.
- A mini-game is only one possible lead, so it can't stall progress.
- No designer source explains how knowledge gates survive co-op. Section 4 proposes a split: the **gate is communal**, the **witnessing is personal**.

There are three families:
- **Understanding** (Outer Wilds, The Witness, Tunic): the reward is a rule you can now use.
- **Cataloguing** (Pikmin, Animal Crossing, Korok seeds): the reward is an entry.
- **Fragments to tech** (Subnautica, Minecraft, Doodle God): the reward is something you can build.

Boogle's eras want fragments to tech, where each fragment is an understood rule about the island's toys.

## 2. Discovery in co-op and multiplayer

- **Shared vs personal knowledge.**
  - *Valheim* unlocks recipes per player, once that player "has discovered all the necessary ''types'' of ingredient" **[S, wiki]** ([wiki](https://valheim.fandom.com/wiki/How_to_play)). Groups work around it by dropping items for friends to pick up, or with the RecipeSync mod, so that not "everyone [must have] picked up every item at least once" **[S]** ([Thunderstore](https://thunderstore.io/c/valheim/p/Rock3t/RecipeSync/)). Per-player unlocks in a shared world are friction players route around. No source confirmed the brief's "anyone nearby" unlock.
  - *Minecraft* advancements are earned per player and announced to the whole server in chat **[S, wiki]** ([wiki](https://minecraft.wiki/w/Advancement)). This hybrid is useful: it is personal, and everyone hears about it.
- **Discoveries while a friend is offline.** Grounded's Shared Worlds restore progress "regardless of who hosts the world next" **[S]** ([Obsidian](https://grounded.obsidian.net/news/grounded/grounded-heads-into-the-home-stretch)). Knowledge belongs to the world, not to the host.
- **Community-solved secrets.**
  - *Fez:* players brute-forced the monolith; Fish: "What surprised us the most is how the community brute forced the monolith puzzle" **[V]** ([Eurogamer](https://www.eurogamer.net/fez-at-10-years-old-phil-fish-resurfaces-for-a-rare-interview); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2012-04-20-how-the-community-solved-fezs-hardest-puzzle.html)).
  - *Animal Well:* each player gets one piece of a 50-piece bunny mural **[S]**.
  - *Destiny 2:* Bungie's Stevens says secret missions are rare because they were "some of the most challenging content for our teams to build" **[V, as quoted]** ([GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/bungie-responds-to-player-feedback-on-secret-missions-in-destiny-2/)).
- **Naming and credit.** In No Man's Sky the first uploader names the discovery. Anyone who doesn't upload can lose the claim to someone else **[S, wiki]** ([wiki](https://nomanssky.fandom.com/wiki/Discoveries)).
- **Spoilers between friends.** No designer source was found. The partial patterns are Minecraft broadcasting *that* something happened but not *how*, and Valheim making each player handle the item.

## 3. Pitfalls

| Pitfall | Source | Boogle guard |
|---|---|---|
| **Pixel-hunting** | Gilbert: "If the solution can only be reached by trial and error or plain luck, it's a bad puzzle" **[V]** ([Grumpy Gamer, 1989](https://grumpygamer.com/why_adventure_games_suck)) | Big, telegraphed discoverables: steam visible from the beach, wash-ups that twinkle. Being near something and acting on it counts. |
| **Wiki-dependence** | The 1.12 recipe book replaced learning recipes from the wiki; Noita's per-seed recipes defeat lookups **[S]**. No designer quote found. | Several leads per era, so no single obscure secret blocks progress. Obscure secrets stay optional, like Fez's anti-cubes. |
| **Chore** | 900 Korok seeds for a joke reward **[S]**. Gilbert: "The fastest way to turn a player off is to let the game drag on with no advancement" **[V]** | About 3 to 5 leads per era, and every lead is a toy (principle 1). |
| **Checklist / towers** | Dan Hay: "the reason why we removed the towers from the game and built situations where you meet people is because that's what you do in the real world" **[V]** ([TheSixthAxis](https://www.thesixthaxis.com/?p=311160)) | No map reveal and no markers. The journal records what you saw, not what to find. |
| **Repetition kills surprise** | Compton: "I can easily generate 10,000 bowls of plain oatmeal … But the user will likely just see a lot of oatmeal" **[V]** ([Compton](https://galaxykate0.tumblr.com/post/139774965871/so-you-want-to-build-a-generator)). The phrase is "10,000 bowls", not "15 quintillion". | A few dozen hand-made discoveries. New islands bring new rules, not more of the same. |

## 4. A design for discovery-driven progression in Boogle

### 4.1 What can be discovered

Everything should be a toy before it is progress.
- **Places:** a steam vent in the caves, a wreck that shows at low tide, islets reached by rail.
- **Materials:** ore from the mine's glowing veins, coal.
- **Toy combinations:** the ball over a vent; water reaching a windmill's foot.
- **Techniques:** a channel that keeps a pool fed.
- **Creatures:** fish that follow channels, gulls nesting on built structures.
- **Older-era artefacts:** new wash-ups such as a rusted valve, a rail spike or an insulator. They are appended to `gift-catalog.js`, and their weight rises once the previous era is open.
- **Era technologies:** the blueprints the leads point to.

### 4.2 From lead to world

1. **Lead.** The first time anyone witnesses something, the island log records it with the finder's name and tick. Every tab shows a small sting, like Minecraft's toast plus No Man's Sky's credit. The log says *what* and *where*, never *how*.
2. **Blueprint.** Each era lists 5 to 7 possible leads and needs **any 3**. This is redundancy like Subnautica's many fragments. One dull mini-game or obscure lead can't stall progress. A mini-game result, such as a wagon race time, can be one lead, never a required one.
3. **Build.** A communal site with 3 to 5 *different* wants: things to bring, plus one together-moment using the existing 3-player chord detector (`communal-progression.md`).
4. **World.** The build changes the island permanently and gives the ball a new toy (principle 2). The next era's artefacts then start washing up.

The split follows principle 6:
- **Communal:** found leads, blueprints, builds and the world. A friend who misses a night never falls behind, which avoids Valheim's per-player friction.
- **Personal:** your **field journal**. A page gets your own sketch only when *you* witness the thing, even if the island logged it already. You also keep "first finder" credits, titles (Tinkerer, Rail Baron) and cosmetics. Like Sable, each player could pick an era outfit at the end.

### 4.3 Drop-in play and spoilers

- **Point, don't explain.** A lead found while you were away shows in your journal as a silhouette, the place, and "found by Ana". You fill the page by going to see it. The spoiler is only that something exists and where, never the trick.
- **Since you left.** On join, the tab compares the island log with the copy it saw last (localStorage) and shows a postcard of what's new, beside the new builds in the world (principle 9). This needs the snapshot server and the catch-up op first (`communal-progression.md`).
- **Two layers, as in Fez.**
  - The gates use loud, telegraphed leads.
  - Optional secrets gate nothing and give titles only. They are what the friends' group chat solves together.
- **Nobody blocked.** A solo player can reach every era. More *different* finders speed up builds (principle 4).

### 4.4 Three example chains

**A. Castaway to steam wagons**
- *Discover:* a **rusted boiler valve** washes up. In the cave hub, a new **steam vent** puffs every 20 s on the shared clock.
- *Witness:* throw the ball over the vent and it bobs in the plume. That is a toy in itself, and it is the lead "steam lifts".
- *Unlock:* any 3 of {valve, vent lift, coal from the mine, the minecart pushed to the end of its rails, smoke seen from the lighthouse} give the boiler blueprint.
- *World:* a build at the cave mouth wants driftwood, the valve, coal and a 3-friend chord to light it. Then a steam wagon runs from the caves to home. Players and the ball can ride it, and its plume lifts the ball as it passes.

**B. Iron and coal**
- *Discover:* hitting a glowing vein with the shovel or the ball knocks loose **ore**. A **rail spike** washes up.
- *Witness:* throw ore into the lit lighthouse lamp at night and glowing iron drips onto the deck. That is the lead "fire melts ore".
- *Unlock:* any 3 of {ore, lamp smelting, rail spike, a full wagon ride, fish scattering from a hot ingot in the sea}.
- *World:* a forge at the lighthouse foot lays iron rails and a footbridge to the first islet, a new place with new leads.

**C. Steel and harbour**
- *Discover:* the **glass float** (an existing wash-up) and the existing fish school.
- *Witness:* dig a channel from the sea into a deep pool and the fish follow it in ("fish follow channels"). Throw a float in and they gather beneath it ("floats gather fish").
- *Unlock:* any 3 of {fish channel, float gathering, the wreck at low tide, a 4-player rally over water, a steel plate from the forge}.
- *World:* a harbour in the dug bay with a jetty, a crane and a boat to islet two. Gulls nest on the crane, which is a lead for the diesel era.

### 4.5 Sync kinds and the smallest prototype

| State | Kind |
|---|---|
| Detecting "I witnessed X" | **Local**. The observer's tab checks the condition and submits `lore.find {k}`, which is idempotent. |
| Island log: found bits, finder name, tick | **Lockstep**. A new `lore` state (a few hundred bytes, with `version`). The sting fires in `onApplied`, not in `apply`. |
| Blueprints and build wants | **Lockstep**. Derived from `lore`, plus a `build.give` op. |
| Built world (rails, boiler, harbour) | **Lockstep** flags. The meshes are Local and read them. |
| Era wash-ups | **Lockstep**. Appended catalog kinds. The gifts rule reads `lore` as a sibling rule. |
| Vent puffs, wagon on a fixed track | **Lockstep without state** (a function of tick). A rider is **Owner**. |
| Journal, titles, "since you left" | **Local**, in localStorage `boogle.journal.<room>`. |

**Smallest prototype.** Behind a prototype flag:
- A `lore` rule with one op, `lore.find`.
- One discovery that needs no new art: "the ball batted by a windmill sail".
- A toast on every tab, and a one-page journal with a silhouette.

Tests:
- *Unit:* finds are idempotent, the first finder is kept, and the snapshot round-trips. Add the op to `edits` for `world-replay.test.mjs`.
- *Playtest:* two friends play at different times. Did the second go and witness the sail bonk for themselves? Did the postcard make them grateful to the first?

If both answers are yes, build the vent and chain A.
