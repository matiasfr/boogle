## Findings

**Seated contact:** the original full-route failure on `1b19093f` remains unexplained. Baseline contention, Jump/Interact exit and reseating passed. In the final 45-second stress probe, both local seats remained assigned throughout 450 / 386 samples. P2 lost `carry.on` in one sampled state, then regained it in the next, still seated and grounded. This is not the earlier sustained detachment followed by standing on deck. The strict original route monitor remains unchanged; its error now includes seat state, last release reason, platform, body pose/velocity, coyote and swimming. Local seat diagnostics distinguish input, unreachable grants, network/lead changes, unavailable seats and anchor-distance release. No physics epsilon or timeout relaxation was added.

**Grounding:** the near-support symptom reproduced in both 1x repeats, with and without injected stalls. After approaching the earlier T3 location, the local contact API returned no contacts while the body was almost motionless above a walkable ray hit. Captured foot gaps were about **0.36–0.88 mm**. In the final unmodified-clock run, sampled coyote minima were **−5.97 / −2.42 seconds**; both recovered before the final jump check and rose **1.550 / 1.550 m**. The no-stall repeat reached −5.97 / −3.93. These are controller-state readings, not machine-performance measurements.

This narrows the symptom to contact eligibility, but does not establish the underlying Rapier pair/solver cause or prove identity with the original 34-second outage. `groundPlayer` requires actual contacts; `backend-rapier.ts` rejects non-overlapping pairs. A nearby downward ray is not proof of sphere contact. Changing those guards without a deterministic contact regression would exceed a small justified repair. The new bot watcher retains 60 recent contact/pose samples, logs a `GROUNDING OUTAGE` candidate before recovery destroys the evidence, and includes the history in existing landing diagnostics. Final idle/jump assertions passing do **not** close the transient outage.

**Train gaps and visible partners:** injected 450 ms snapshot delay plus 120 ms page stalls reproduced an obvious presentation failure twice. The final run measured maximum partner displacement from its chair of **15.715 / 10.161 m**. `ride.on` was false in **299/450 and 231/386** samples even though the remote still advertised riding and had not left. The inspected consecutive frames show the partner beside/ahead of the train while its chair is empty; the ending still shows the partner seated again. A player would notice this placement error.

The code path is specific: Place sends periodic snapshots without an age adjustment, then nudges or snaps its local train toward the received position (`coop.ts`). Remote-player presentation trusts deck-relative riding only while its interpolated world presence is within `1.5 m + speed × 120 ms` of that deck (about 3.9 m at cruise). Beyond that it draws the world pose. The final telemetry records that fallback during the visible displacement. This explains the injected case; the earlier **6.2 m sequential cross-tab reading** cannot retrospectively be labelled a visible 6.2 m separation. Widening the generic trust radius or changing train reconciliation needs a separate justified fix; neither was guessed here. The new probe asserts on sampled carry loss and partner drift over 0.5 m.
