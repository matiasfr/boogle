# Personal rewards without power

_1 November 2026. Research for the personal half of "Three things you earn" in [docs/proposals/boogle-2.md](../proposals/boogle-2.md): **som** (a personal coin, earned only, spent on looks) and **knacks** (personal skill per activity that unlock options, never power), plus the cosmetics both pay for. Hats stay out of scope until the loop is fun, so this is research only. It builds on [0.1/8-collecting-tiers.md](0.1/8-collecting-tiers.md) (contribution tiers, Wobbly Life, Sea of Thieves' "no classes"), [0.1/9-brainrot-hooks.md](0.1/9-brainrot-hooks.md) (rarity done kindly) and [progression-models.md](progression-models.md), and doesn't repeat them. Sources are developer interviews, a GDC talk write-up, studio pages, one psychology study, and wikis where nothing better exists._

**Markers.** **[V]**: I fetched the page and the wording matched. **[S]**: from a search summary, a wiki or a guide only; likely, not certain.

## Summary

1. The games that keep veterans and newcomers playing together give no power for progress. Sea of Thieves turned it down because "one person sees that the other is 20 levels above them" and then they stop playing together. Rare's answer was cosmetics "like trophies".
2. A look is worth showing off when it records something you did. Players respect people who earned what they have more than people who bought it (Evers et al. 2015). The status comes from the effort behind the item, so Boogle's earned-only som already gives every item that value.
3. A reward only motivates if the player notices it and knows what it's worth. Most of what makes a look worth earning is being seen: on the body, in play, by friends standing nearby.
4. Players chase whatever pays best per minute. When Fall Guys went free-to-play, coming last paid almost as well as winning, so players talked about losing on purpose. Valve stopped tying TF2 unlocks to achievements after players farmed them. If som pays for time alone, players will farm time.
5. Nothing should expire. Deep Rock Galactic moves season rewards into its normal loot pools and later let players replay old seasons. Missing out felt bad to players, and the game lost nothing by dropping it.
6. Put the unlock where the play already is. Mario Kart World turned down "coins to buy clothes from clothing stores" because stopping to shop broke the drive. You unlock outfits by eating at drive-throughs along the road. A som shop works best as a stop you pass anyway.
7. Prestige can come without loss. DRG's promotion resets a class to level 1, keeps everything you unlocked and adds a coloured badge border, with tiers from Bronze to Legendary. A visible tally of time put into an activity works as a knack's display.
8. Badges for odd deeds give people stories to tell. Peak's badges each unlock a cosmetic and sit on a sash anyone can see. Some reward strange acts, like cooking and eating particular items and still escaping, alongside the obvious summit.
9. Rare looks have a social cost. In Sky, players "gathered towards the rarest combinations" until friends couldn't tell each other apart. Kept looks need a fixed identity anchor, like Boogle's name and colour.
10. Skill that unlocks options already exists in the precedents. A Hat in Time's hats are new verbs, crafted by choice, never a gate. Splatoon gear gives "new ways to play" but adds small stat power. A knack in Boogle can unlock a variant, a trick or a harder route that other players can see. It should never make you faster or stronger.

## Findings by source

### Why no power: the shared-session argument

- **Sea of Thieves (Rare)**
  - Design director Mike Chapman, before launch, described the stat treadmill as "how many of my twos turned into threes", and the social cost of levels as "one person sees that the other is 20 levels above them". He said "our progression system is not a single number" and offered "unique cosmetics that are like trophies" instead. The rewards for veterans are exotic quest chains that "won't be limiting to other players" because newcomers can join them [V, [Game Informer, 2017](https://gameinformer.com/b/features/archive/2017/11/17/how-rare-cast-away-its-developmental-process-for-sea-of-thieves.aspx)].
  - Joe Neate framed cosmetics and quest unlocks as letting casual and veteran players play together without barriers, "while rewarding time and effort" [S, [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/microsoft-puts-sea-thieves-progression-mechanics-spotlight), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sea_of_Thieves)].
  - Commendations are the achievement layer. Graded ones mostly pay a title or cosmetic only at the top grade, and some unlock an item for purchase rather than giving it [S, [Sea of Thieves Wiki](https://seaofthieves.fandom.com/wiki/Commendations)].
  - Later, Chapman said many rewarding moments "go unrewarded", which led to Seasons, a free 100-level track [S, [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/sea-thieves-seasons-interview-future-pirate-voyage-wjoe-neate-and-mike-chapman)].
  - Forum players still call cosmetic-only progress thin, and gold has too little to buy [S, [official forum](https://www.seaofthieves.com/community/forums/topic/47120/so-everything-is-purely-cosmetic-i-feel-cheated)].
  - **Take:** trophies, not numbers, but also enough to spend on and enough small moments that pay something.
- **Team Fortress 2 (Valve)**
  - Robin Walker shipped hats first, cosmetic only, to "ensure we don't break our silhouette based class identification" [V].
  - He said the big change was "the decoupling of achievements and unlockables", which had caused "most of the negative feedback" on earlier class packs [V].
  - Valve wanted "multiple ways for players to get items" because some players "want to use their skill" and "others want to be more casual" [V, [Shacknews, 2009](https://www.shacknews.com/article/58412/interview-valves-robin-walker-on)].
  - Elsewhere Walker said players "really value being able to display their status and their affiliation" [S, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/interview-valve-on-the-future-of-team-fortress-2/3/)].
  - **Take:** looks must not break readability. An unlock bound to one feat gets farmed, so several routes to the same item suit both kinds of player.
- **Evers, van de Ven and Weeda (2015).** The study covered Maple Story, Diablo III and World of Tanks.
  - Players who paid for advantages were respected less and seen as less skilled. Others were amused when they failed and wanted them on their team less.
  - Respondents said they respected those who earned what they had.
  - Facing a payer made respondents more tempted to pay themselves [V, [Jamie Madigan's write-up](https://www.psychologyofgames.com/2015/08/when-the-pay-to-win-button-backfires/)].
  - The study covered advantages, not looks. Madigan notes that most games hide whether an item was earned or bought.
  - **Take:** in Boogle nothing can be bought, so every look reads as earned. That's a status signal paid games can't offer.

### Earning economies: what went right and wrong

- **Fall Guys (Mediatonic)**
  - At launch, winning gave crowns, which bought shop cosmetics.
  - The 2022 free-to-play switch made crowns a slow separate rank, added Fame for every match played, and added bought Show-Bucks.
  - Players found Fame for surviving long "negligible" compared with an early knockout, and some argued it was faster to lose. One wrote that games "aren't fun if it feels like there are no rewards" [V, [Destructoid, 2022](https://www.destructoid.com/fall-guys-season-pass-frustrations-fame-kudos-show-bucks/)].
  - Every costume shares the same hitbox [S, fan guide].
  - **Take:** pay a little more for playing well than for just showing up, and never let the cheapest path be the boring one.
- **Soren Johnson, "Water Finds a Crack"**
  - "Given the opportunity, players will optimize the fun out of a game."
  - Players accept tedious small rewards, like Morrowind's skill by repetition, and a dominant strategy erases other choices. Some exploits are worth keeping, like Mario's 1-UP trick [V, [Designer Notes](https://www.designer-notes.com/game-developer-column-17-water-finds-a-crack/)].
  - **Take:** a som source that can be repeated without limit becomes the job.
- **Ben Lewis-Evans (Epic), GDC 2017**
  - "Rewards are feedback, fundamentally."
  - "If players don't know they got a reward, they can't get it again."
  - Variable-ratio drops are the most notorious conditioning schedule. Surprise wears off when it comes too often [V, [Shacknews](https://www.shacknews.com/article/99219/gdc-2017-epic-games-ben-lewis-evans-on-player-psychology-and-rewards)].
  - **Take:** each som payout needs a visible, audible beat at the moment it lands.
- **Deep Rock Galactic (Ghost Ship)**
  - Seasonal cosmetics went into the regular loot pools once a season ended [V, [Screen Rant interview](https://screenrant.com/deep-rock-galactic-interview/)].
  - A 2024 update lets players reactivate any past season and keeps their place in it ("nothing's going away") [S, [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/deep-rock-galactic-is-rolling-out-a-feature-to-let-you-play-old-seasons/), [Destructoid](https://www.destructoid.com/deep-rock-galactic-defeats-fomo-by-letting-you-play-old-seasons-whenever-wherever/)].
  - Paid DLC is cosmetic [S, Steam forums].
  - **Take:** a catalog can rotate for variety, but items never leave for good.
- **Among Us (Innersloth)**
  - Beans are earned by playing online and buy Cosmicubes. A cube's items unlock with Pods, which you earn only while that cube is active.
  - The MIRA cube is free to everyone, and a Stardew Valley cube was free for beans [S, [Cosmicubes wiki](https://among-us.fandom.com/wiki/Cosmicubes), [Innersloth](https://www.innersloth.com/announcing-among-us-x-stardew-valley/)].
  - In Nintendo's interview, each team member had a fixed signature look: one always wears the crewmate pet and tries to be Cyan, another sticks with the sticky note [S, search summary].
  - **Take:** you choose what you're working toward, and you earn it by playing normally. Many players settle on one look and keep it.
- **Sky: Children of the Light (thatgamecompany)**
  - Chen calls many monetization strategies ones "built on negative emotions" like greed and fear of missing out. Sky designs for giving instead: "Around 22 per cent of revenue is gifts", and 40–50 % of season passes were gifted, so a gift-only pass token followed [V, [PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/interview/78533/jenova-chen-sky-seven-year-development-monetising-his-way/)].
  - A pure-altruism model (buy only for others) failed in testing, and Chen settled on a mix [S, [MCV](https://mcvuk.com/business-news/pennies-from-heaven-thatgamecompanys-jenova-chen-on-monetisation-in-sky-children-of-the-light/)].
  - Players "gathered towards the rarest combinations" until friends couldn't recognise each other, so names were added [V, MCV].
  - Spirits' friendship trees sell cosmetics and emotes for candles and hearts. Hearts come mostly from friends' gifts [S, [Sky wiki](https://sky-children-of-the-light.fandom.com/wiki/Ancestors)].
  - **Take:** a reward that comes from other players builds bonds. Rare looks need an identity anchor.
- **Animal Crossing: New Horizons**
  - Kyogoku: Nook Miles exist "to facilitate the discovery of ways to play" (brief 8).
  - Character customization can be changed any time with no gender locks, which Kyogoku tied to "individuality" [S, [GoNintendo](https://gonintendo.com/stories/357681-nintendo-says-animal-crossing-new-horizons-approach-to-characte)].
  - The Happy Home Academy scores your rooms daily and gives bonuses for full furniture series. Some players love it and others call it "a nonsensical point of judgement" [S, [Nookipedia](https://nookipedia.com/wiki/Happy_Home_Academy), [dbltap](https://www.dbltap.com/posts/animal-crossing-new-horizons-happy-home-academy-how-does-it-work-01e4ke5sjpqn)].
  - **Take:** reward the discovery, not the routine. Free re-styling keeps looks expressive, and a score on taste divides players.

### Unlocks woven into play

- **Mario Kart World (Nintendo, Ask the Developer vol. 18)**
  - Yabuki: the team discussed "using coins to buy clothes from clothing stores", but stopping to shop "would have disrupted the flow".
  - Outfits now unlock by eating a region's food at drive-throughs while driving. Jikumaru says the regional outfit "fits the game perfectly", and Yabuki hopes it gets players to "try out different characters" [V, [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-18-mario-kart-world-part-3/)].
  - There are about 100 outfits [S].
  - **Take:** the purchase is a moment inside the activity, and it carries a sense of place (this outfit comes from that region).
- **A Short Hike (Adam Robinson-Yu)**
  - Collectibles pull players toward new areas (brief 8).
  - Golden feathers are a stamina resource found, earned in side events or bought with coins. Around seven are needed for the summit, and there are about twenty in all [S, [Culture Eater](http://cultureeater.com.au/a-short-hike/), [Hans Lick Girls](https://thehanslickgirls.com/2020/03/23/the-rock-climbers-association/)].
  - **Take:** extra copies of a resource make the goal easier, but the goal never needs all of them.
- **A Hat in Time (Gears for Breakfast, Jonas Kærlev)**
  - Late in development, hats became "the main, active ability" and badges became passive.
  - Players craft hats from yarn by choice ("Ice Hat" or "Brewing Hat"), and items are never "No, you need this item" gates.
  - Collectibles are "always a net gain to their adventure", and the finale opens at 25 of 40 Time Pieces so players finish when they like [V, [Goomba Stomp](https://goombastomp.com/interview-jonas-kaerlev/)].
  - Kærlev: "the player has to be rewarded for completing difficult tasks" [S, [Player2](https://www.player2.net.au/2017/03/interview-jonas-kaerlev-a-hat-in-time/)].
  - **Take:** in a solo game, hats that grant verbs are a joy. In Boogle that would be power, unless the verb is purely expressive (a trick, a trail, a sound).
- **Tony Hawk's Pro Skater (Neversoft)**
  - Each level's goal list unlocks the next level and equipment [S, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tony_Hawk's_Pro_Skater)].
  - A tester recalls "There's no boundaries", with points, combos and collectibles up to him. The scoring took "a long time thrashing around" to tune [V, [The Ringer](https://www.theringer.com/2019/08/30/video-games/oral-history-tony-hawk-pro-skater-soundtrack-les-claypool-neversoft-activision)].
  - **Take:** a short, readable list of feats per place (a high score, letters to collect, one odd stunt) is the template for a knack.

### Mastery you can see

- **Deep Rock Galactic promotions**
  - At level 25 a class can be promoted. It resets to level 1 and keeps every unlock.
  - Each promotion adds an Honor Badge portrait border (Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Legendary, each I–III, then a counter), plus cosmetic and overclock cores.
  - The first promotion opens Deep Dives and a perk slot, so it isn't purely cosmetic.
  - Prestige assignments pay cosmetics up to player rank 100 [S, [DRG wiki](https://deeprockgalactic.wiki.gg/wiki/Promotion)].
  - **Take:** a coloured tier that counts devotion to one activity, shown on the player. The early unlocks DRG attaches are what Boogle would leave out.
- **Peak (Aggro Crab and Landfall)**
  - The store page: "Show off your survival prowess with dozens of badges to earn and cosmetics to unlock" [V, [Landfall](https://landfall.se/peak)].
  - Each badge unlocks a cosmetic and shows on the sash and passport. Ascents (harder runs) each award a sash, and any earned sash can be worn. Collecting all badges gives a gold medal.
  - The Final Ascent update added the last ten badges and closed the set [S, [PEAK Wiki](https://peak.wiki.gg/wiki/Cosmetics), [allthings.how](https://allthings.how/peak-how-to-unlock-every-final-ascent-cosmetic-v2-0-a/)].
  - Badges give no stats [S, guide].
  - **Take:** a finite set, odd deeds alongside hard ones, worn where friends see it, and a harder mode that pays only in looks.
- **Rocket League (Psyonix)**
  - Season rewards go by the highest rank reached, not the final rank. The season reward level unlocks with wins at that rank (10 per level since season 7).
  - Titles exist for Grand Champion, Top 100, and live counters of wins and MVPs.
  - A season of unpopular reward colours drew community pushback [S, [Rocket League wiki](https://rocketleague.fandom.com/wiki/Competitive), [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/maxthielmeyer/2018/09/14/here-are-the-rocket-league-competitive-season-8-rewards/), [neuralboost.gg](https://neuralboost.gg/blog/rocket-league-titles-every-way-to-earn-one-in-season-24)].
  - **Take:** count wins at your own level, so every player can earn something. A title or counter is the cheapest mastery display there is.
- **Splatoon (Nintendo, Hisashi Nogami)**
  - Gear abilities were reworked to "make the weapon merits stronger, offset weaknesses and allow for new ways to play".
  - On why there's more gear: "we wanted the player to have more to choose from" [V, [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/splatoon-2-interview-hisashi-nogami/)].
  - Abilities are small stat changes (respawn time, ink cost), so gear is fashion with a power layer [S, [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Splatoon)].
  - **Take:** the counterexample. Even small stat boosts on clothing push fashion toward the optimal build.
- **Naavik, "Skin in the Game"**
  - Cosmetics pay off when others see them: kill banners, weapon skins always on screen, Fortnite Creative fashion shows.
  - Status is tied to effort as well as spending (battle-pass-only camos, early-season skins) [V, [Naavik](https://naavik.co/deep-dives/selling-cosmetics-part1/)].
  - **Take:** the slot that's always visible matters most, and player-made fashion events follow from having enough range.

## What this means for Boogle

Today: no som, no knacks, no worn items. The three mini-game toys keep a personal best in localStorage with confetti on a new one, and pay no rewards ([minis.md](../game-design/minis.md)). The Boogler's friendship reaches Best friend in about five visits ([boogler.md](../game-design/boogler.md)). Brief 8 has already set the sync shape: earned things are Local (localStorage) plus Owner presence for what you wear. Brief 8's tier table pairs cosmetics with conveniences (magnet range, stack never spills). The Sea of Thieves and Splatoon evidence suggests those conveniences need checking against "never power".

### Som

1. **Pay som for deeds, not time.** Fall Guys' Fame and Johnson's "water finds a crack" show what happens when time pays.
   - A small payout for doing a thing (a round, a haul, a gift).
   - A bigger one for doing it well or for the first time: a new best, a first visit, a par.
   - Nothing for idling, nothing for repeating the cheapest action.
   - Prototype: in `?proto=minis`, add a som counter. +1 per round, +5 per new best, +10 the first time you play each toy. Log som per minute for a solo session and check that the best minute isn't the most boring one.
2. **Aim for one thing to buy in the first session, then something every session or two.**
   - Lewis-Evans: a reward must be felt. Wobbly Life (brief 8) gives a costume the first time you do a job.
   - A starting curve: a cheap colour or pattern for about 10 minutes of varied play, a hat for 1–2 sessions, one dear item for about five sessions (the same scale as Best friend).
   - Prototype: the counter above plus a price list on a sign. Note when you first could afford something, and whether you wanted it.
3. **Put the shop where the play is.** Mario Kart World dropped the clothing store because stopping broke the flow.
   - The Emporium works as a stop you pass on a route you already take, or items unlock in place: hat stands at venues, a look found at an islet.
   - Prototype: one stand by the toys that sells three looks with Interact, compared with one that appears beside the toy when you can afford its item.
4. **Never expire, never sell rarity.** DRG keeps seasonal items in its pools, and Chen rejects designs built on fear of missing out.
   - A rotating catalog is fine if everything comes back. Gifting som or looks to a friend is the one social route Sky proves out.
   - Prototype: a "give this hat" Interact on a friend in the stall prototype, to see whether giving feels better than buying.
5. **Make earned visible, since nothing is bought.** Evers et al.: earned status carries respect. Boogle can say where each item came from.
   - Prototype: a tooltip or the pause view showing "Golf stand, par on the first hole" for each item.

### Knacks

6. **A knack is a short list of feats per activity, like Tony Hawk's per-level goals and Peak's badges.** Each feat unlocks a look or a variant of that activity, never speed, reach or strength.
   - Feats come in three kinds, after TF2's multiple routes: a skill feat (a hole in one), a devotion feat (50 rounds) and an odd feat (sink the ball after it bounces off The Boogler).
   - Prototype: topple with five feats. Each lights a stamp over the stump and unlocks golden crates, a taller stack or a trick target.
7. **Unlock options, as A Hat in Time does, but only expressive ones.**
   - Options that pass: a harder variant (a longer hole, Peak's Ascents), a trick (a spin flourish on a jump that only looks different), a trail or sound, and a "teach" role (your knack shows a ghost line for a friend).
   - What fails: anything that changes physics numbers. Splatoon's gear shows how a small stat pushes fashion toward the optimal build.
   - Prototype: a golf knack 3 that unlocks a second, harder hole for anyone you're with. A knack that opens content for the group fits "communal world, personal rewards".
8. **Show the knack as a tier on the body.**
   - DRG's coloured border and Rocket League's titles are the cheapest mastery displays. Count at the player's own level (Rocket League's wins at your rank) so newcomers earn quickly.
   - Prototype: a small coloured pip by the name label per knack (bronze to gold over the five feats), sent as one presence field.
9. **Let a maxed knack keep counting without resetting anything.** DRG's prestige keeps every unlock, and brief 9's heritage covers the rest.
   - Prototype: after the last feat, the pip shows a counter of rounds.

### Cosmetics

10. **Spend design effort on what's always visible and readable at distance.** TF2's silhouette rule and Naavik's always-on-screen point both apply.
    - That's head, colour pattern and a trail. Looks must never hide the body colour and name that tell friends apart (Sky's rarity crowding).
    - Prototype: the hat socket from [characters.md](characters.md)'s `?proto=bean` with three hats, worn by 4 bots, viewed at 10 and 25 m.
11. **Let looks tell a story.** Sea of Thieves' trophies, Peak's odd-deed badges and Mario Kart World's regional outfits all give the look a place or deed of origin.
    - A hat from the golf stand should look like golf, and one from the mine should look like the mine.
    - Prototype: three hats, each named after where it's earned. Ask a solo playtester which one they'd wear and why.
12. **Make re-styling free and instant, with no score on taste.** New Horizons lets you restyle any time with no gender locks, and the HHA's judging divides players.
    - Prototype: a wardrobe row in the pause view that swaps the presence field live.

**Smallest prototype of all three together:** in `?proto=minis`, a som counter, one three-item stand, five feats on topple with a name-label pip, and one hat socket. That's about a day's work, all Local plus one presence field. The solo question: "after 20 minutes, is there one thing I want, and did I earn it by playing, not by grinding?"
