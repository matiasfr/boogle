# Carried resources: let the load speak, show the number when it matters

_Research report, 4 November 2026. Carried-resource placement, duration, capacity, mixed loads, deposits and co-op readability. Live web research, focused source inspection at `64cedd84`, one staged desktop WebGPU capture and four illustrative mockups. Research only: no game code or design decisions changed. Serves DESIGN.md 7 (Telegraph everything), 11 (Show, don't tell) and 4 (Cooperation is automatic)._

## Recommendation first

**Use a hybrid: Buddy's worn cargo is the continuous display; a small, screen-readable tag beside the load appears when quantities change, when gathering is blocked by capacity, and while depositing. It fades during ordinary travel.** Make exact contents available through the existing pause surface. On a phone, dock the same temporary readout in a reserved upper-left HUD lane when there is insufficient space beside Buddy. Keep material requirements on the receiving frame.

**Runner-up: a compact, persistent left-side cargo summary while carrying anything**, below the roster and clear of the touch controls. This sacrifices some visual quiet for immediate arithmetic and is the better fallback if the moving tag distracts or players repeatedly reopen Pause to count.

The aesthetically strongest _travel_ state is the load alone. The strongest _usable system_ is the hybrid: current Boogle has mixed materials, exact bills, a shared capacity and visually compressed logs. Purely diegetic carrying would hide information that still affects the next trip. A permanent tag beside Buddy is prettier than the bottom-centre pill in this still, but it remains in the player's most valuable visual area throughout every jump and turn.

**Disposition: owner approved 4 November, built.** The hybrid, including reveal on Drop across all inputs, is implemented in [Place's carrying display](../game-design/place.md#carried-resource-display). The [round-4 tracker](../plans/round-4-notes.md) still calls for replay of shipped fixes. This report does not reopen bills, carry limits, the no-sway bundle decision or the parked hotbar. [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md)'s applicable wording is “Show, don't tell”; “the world, not a menu” is also used in the [decision log](../proposals/boogle-2.md). There is no separate HUD-principles heading in this checkout's DESIGN.md.

**Evidence key:** **C** = inspected current code or Boogle capture; **P** = developer/publisher text, slides or images; **S** = community documentation or independent UI analysis; **H** = recommendation or untested hypothesis. Reference games were not played for this report. A screenshot establishes placement in that frame, not persistence or an exact fade duration. No universal aesthetic preference or measured eye-tracking benefit is claimed.

**Missing input:** the brief refers to an attached “16 logs” still, but no attachment or local path was available in this session. A path was requested. The provisional mockups use a fresh, real **8-log** Boogle capture instead; they do not satisfy exact compositing over that missing still. Their paths and this departure are recorded below.

### What the player needs to know on a trip

**C, bills in [`layout.ts`](../../src/proto/place/layout.ts):** T1 needs 24 logs; T2 needs 32 logs + 8 stone; T3 needs 40 logs + one great beam. The forge uses 24 then 32 logs. Q1 needs 16 logs + 6 rails; later frames include 24 logs + 24 rails and the pier needs 32 + 32. These are construction bills, not screen-rendering costs.

**H:** while gathering, answer “what am I carrying, and can another piece fit?” Before leaving, answer “is this the useful mixture?” At a frame, answer “what landed, and what is still missing?” A full 16-slot bag containing 10 logs + 6 rails is a useful Q1 load, but it does not complete that frame's 16-log requirement. A single total of `16` is insufficient; three separate `x/16` rows falsely suggest three capacities.

Use, for example, **`10 logs · 6 rails`**, with a small **`16/16 · Full`** footer. Hide zero rows. At three types, wrap into at most three short rows without truncating their counts. Use an actual material icon plus text while learning, not colour alone. Exact bill arithmetic is useful; comic money and lifetime contribution totals need not accompany every log.

### What the sources justify

**P → H:** the directly relevant GDC source is Aaron Biddlecom and Elijah O'Rear's Astroneer presentation, [“Mining Your Own Design”](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1026869/Mining-Your-Own-Design-Crafting). Its slides support both tactile inventory and caution about cognitive load. Ghost Ship's dynamic-HUD announcements support conditional visibility. Coffee Stain's missing-cost option supports exposing only relevant arithmetic. These three ideas fit together: physical cargo continuously, precise quantities at a decision, requirements at the receiver.

**P, supporting reference:** Dino Ignacio's [“Crafting Destruction: The Evolution of the Dead Space User Interface”](https://www.gdcvault.com/play/1017723/Crafting-Destruction-The-Evolution-of) is an explicitly first-party UI-development talk. Only its abstract was fetched here, not the full recording; it confirms the topic and author, not a universal rule that body-mounted UI wins. Its science-fiction presentation is not a visual template for Boogle.

**H:** no single “usual” location spans these games. Small visible stacks favour physical representation; large heterogeneous inventories favour screens; construction needs favour context-specific bills. A floating text tag is **spatial UI**, not truly diegetic just because it follows a character. Buddy's actual load is the diegetic part.

## 4. Numbered recommendations for Boogle

1. **Fix the meaning before moving the pill.** The presentation should read Place's logs, rails, stone, big-cargo identity and `carryNow()` from [`chop.ts`](../../src/proto/place/chop.ts); the existing seam in [`haul-stack.ts`](../../src/features/haul-stack.ts) currently exposes only logs. Use one total/capacity for ordinary materials, separate type counts and an explicit `Full`. Keep incoming/reserved material from being falsely advertised as freely available space, but do not label it delivered. Leave inventory ownership, acceptance, wire shapes and bills unchanged. This is a presentation requirement, not approval for a state-system migration.

2. **Try the hybrid beside the load first, with the existing wood token.** In [`haul-stack.css`](../../src/features/haul-stack.css), retain warm brown `--wood`, cream text, rounded silhouette and tabular numerals; avoid a large panel, glass blur or an inventory grid. Target **18px text**, a **20–24px pictogram**, and **12–20 CSS px clearance** from the projected body/cargo silhouette. These are **H starting values**, not measured optima. Anchor to a stable torso/load envelope, not an animated log end. Prefer screen-right at pack height in the captured rear view; move left only when the right candidate is obstructed, with hysteresis so it does not flip every frame. Neither candidate may cover body, cargo, reticle, receiver text, feet or the next landing. If both fail, use the side lane. A permanent sidecar is not the goal.

3. **Use events and context, not only a timer.** Show the **resulting total**, not just `+2`, after pickup/drop, restoration of a carried load, capacity change, a capacity-blocked attempt, and while at a relevant source/receiver. Starting hypothesis: stay readable **2 seconds after the last change**, then fade over **200ms**; continuous gathering/deposit refreshes the same label, not a queue of toasts. At a full pack, show `16/16 · Full` while gathering or being refused; it may fade once travelling away. At empty, allow a short `0`/completion beat, then remove the tag. Existing money timing in [`signs.ts`](../../src/proto/place/signs.ts) makes this a coherent comparison, not evidence that two seconds is enough for every player. Reduced motion disables count bouncing and travel, not essential information. Pause should provide exact contents without requiring a new binding; an “always show carry count” fallback is worth testing if recall is poor.

4. **Keep mixed cargo and big cargo truthful.** For `10 logs + 6 rails`, show two named/icon rows and one `16/16 · Full` footer, never `10/16 logs` plus `6/16 rails`. For beam/boiler, briefly show the object name and, only if relevant, the existing carry/help state; no ordinary-slot denominator and no invented weight. The long beam's full silhouette must be in the avoidance envelope. Keep partner loads from [`chop.ts`](../../src/proto/place/chop.ts) and the [Place carrying contract](../game-design/place.md) as the main co-op display. Do not add permanent numeric tags to all six players or expose hidden inventory through walls. A later nearby partner-inspection cue is optional and needs its own justification.

5. **Make deposits explain the exchange at the frame.** Preserve [`run.ts`](../../src/proto/place/run.ts)'s physical journey and [`stations.ts`](../../src/proto/place/stations.ts)'s bill. While material transfers, keep the carried total visible and let the receiving bill update as pieces land; do not duplicate the whole bill by Buddy or make a premature “complete” announcement. After a partial delivery, make the retained mixed contents readable. If another player fills the bill first, show the real remainder instead of a fake success. A short, existing-style count accent is enough; no currency shower or replacement objective arrow. List any conflicting Boogler line for the owner rather than rewriting it; none was identified in the inspected carry-display code.

6. **Treat phone placement as a layout, not a smaller desktop.** [`place.css`](../../src/proto/place/place.css)'s Drop/Ping row and [`haul-stack.css`](../../src/features/haul-stack.css)'s 252px portrait lift demonstrate that the lower half is already crowded. Reserve an upper-left lane **below the actual roster bounds**, safe-area inset plus about **16px**, with room for three short lines. If roster growth leaves no slot, collapse the carry summary to two rows and test a reserved upper-side position; never cover controls or shrink type below readability.
