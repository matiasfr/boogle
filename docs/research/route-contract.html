Intended pad access without jump

The counterfactual model cannot reach apron or p2, even with later track. Mid-air interaction tolerances are not modeled; runtime softlock remains unproved.

Summit → Far Head

Distance versus current conservative glide budget. A future ability may cross it; Q5's freight and grant predicates must still hold.

26 pads, 10 optional secrets. The checker uses boolean jump ownership, fixed 1.68/3.03 m rises and 4.7/8 m ordinary reach. A third jump has no measured envelope here. No browser, phone, published Artifact or new gameplay validation.

Seven recommendations

  1. Separate travel shortcuts from required work.
  2. Put the find on the baseline southern approach.
  3. Make Q3 assemble the ramp and engage the winch.
  4. Make Q2 provide baseline access to the first hook.
  5. Allow optional and return-route shortcuts.
  6. Check missing-find states, each stage and directed returns.
  7. Keep room ownership explicit; test triple jump separately.
