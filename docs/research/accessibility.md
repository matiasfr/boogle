# Accessibility: one railway, different ways to play

> **Verdict:** Make access to the same railway configurable; try a comfort camera option first.
> **Top recommendations:** 1. Separate stabilizing follow from optional motion, and expose comfort before entry. 2. Give text a readable default, scalable layout and semantic equivalent. 3. Remap actions through every actual input path.
> **Status:** open. No decision cites it yet.

_5 November 2026. Research and recommendations, not implementation approval. Repository audited at `68ee082d` on `codex/accessibility`; live primary sources read on this date. **[V]** means repository code or primary-source text inspected, **[S]** an explicitly identified secondary example, and **[D]** a Boogle inference or proposal. No game, assistive technology or reference game was exercised for this report; not tested on a device. Costs are estimates, not measured delivery times._

## Recommendation and scope

**Make access to the same railway configurable before adding more ability gates.** Start with a bounded comfort experiment: retain Buddy's stabilizing camera follow while removing optional camera motion, then expose that choice before the opening fall. Next make essential information readable and essential actions remappable. A friend should be able to discover, haul, build and ride using their own settings, without becoming the designated spectator or asking the group to slow down. **[D]**

The [progression brief](../brainstorms/progression.md) proposes about eight hours of big material destinations, several uses for each resource, and smaller puzzle/ability stops. Its route and ability placement remain proposals. Accessibility must span that whole arc: a first-hour input solution is insufficient if a later hook/glide combination requires simultaneous controls; a new material must be recognizable in its source, carried bundle, installation and home upgrade. Assistance is available from entry, not earned as a material upgrade. This preserves the difference between removing an access barrier and the approved satisfaction of earning greater carrying or transport efficiency. **[D]**

The [decision index](../decisions.md#current-decision-index), [owner calls](../agents/owner-calls.md) and [DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md) control the proposal: keep the chosen 5.2 m camera and 5° sprint widening as the ordinary baseline, direct horizontal follow, scene-lit signs, physical machine work, hub arrivals and The Boogler's copy. Do not restore the removed objective marker, fork signs, contribution tiers or first-run controls card. Optional help that changes those choices needs an explicit design decision. The [latest playtest](../playtests.md#2026-11-04-owner-solo-on-iphone-the-default-url) is an iPhone solo run, explicitly not the co-op replay; the [tracker](../plans/round-4-notes.md) records subsequent fixes and remaining device checks. The older [direction view](../direction.md) is not fresh proof of replay acceptance. Research proceeds now; this report does not bypass the replay gate. **[V/D]**

## Primary evidence and transferable lessons

### Guidelines are a baseline, not a certification

The [Game Accessibility Guidelines full list](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/full-list/) covers configurable controls, alternatives to holds/repetition, readable text, multiple information channels and cognitive support. Use it to enumerate barriers in the actual railway loop, rather than count settings. Its basic/intermediate/advanced categories describe general implementation effort; Boogle's costs below reflect its own code. **[V/D]**

The relevant Xbox guidelines separate problems that a single “accessibility mode” obscures:

| Source | What it establishes | Boogle application **[D]** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [XAG 101: Text display](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/101) | Readable defaults and text/glyph enlargement up to 200%, without lost meaning or functionality. | Scale prompts, conversations, Controls and nearby sign reading together; test wrapping rather than scaling the canvas. |
| [XAG 102: Contrast](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/102) | Standard important text/visuals at least 4.5:1, large elements 3:1; a high-contrast option targets 7:1. Check the least contrasting background region. | Measure ink against the rendered paper under shadow and glare. UI contrast and world-object recognition are related but distinct tests. |
| [XAG 103: Additional channels](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/103) | Colour alone must not carry information; important sound and visual cues need alternatives. | Material silhouette + icon/name, stable ready/full states, and visible equivalents of useful bells and pings. |
| [XAG 107: Input](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/107) | Single non-simultaneous UI presses, configurable controls and generous touch targets; suggested defaults are 15 mm on phones and 24 mm on tablets. | Avoid stick-plus-camera-plus-button becoming mandatory. Physical sizes require device measurement, not a universal CSS-pixel conversion. |
| [XAG 109: Objective clarity](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/109) | Players need an accessible way to recall goals and current progress. | A requested explanation of the nearby machine can support memory without restoring a permanent objective HUD. |
| [XAG 117: Motion settings](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/117) | Control over camera motion, FOV and distracting moving interface content. | Separate decorative zoom/shake from stabilizing follow, and keep reading surfaces quiet. |
| [XAG 118: Photosensitivity](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/xbox/accessibility/xbox-accessibility-guidelines/118) | Test actual gameplay for flash/pattern triggers, including unintentional ones; removing problematic content is preferable to warnings. | Inspect overlapping rewards, cloud-white transitions, crystal emission and water glints. Reduced motion alone is not a photosensitivity assessment. |

[Apple's Accessibility HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/accessibility) recommends adaptable text, information conveyed through multiple means, alternatives to complex gestures, and reducing automatic/repetitive motion. Native iOS/iPadOS point sizes and Dynamic Type do not automatically apply to a WebGPU browser canvas. Boogle needs accessible DOM controls and its own text-sizing policy. The HIG's fades are an alternative to spatial animation, not evidence that every full-screen luminance transition is comfortable. Apple's Markdown version was read because its HTML required JavaScript. **[V/D]**

[WebKit's implementation guidance](https://webkit.org/blog/7551/responsive-design-for-motion/) exposes the OS preference through both CSS and `matchMedia()`. Boogle should share one effective preference between DOM and rendered effects, observe changes where delivered, recheck on foreground return, and allow an explicit in-game override. “Follow system / Reduced / Full” describes the behavior without asking someone to disclose a diagnosis. CSS alone cannot change a TSL animation or a camera controller. **[V/D]**

### The Last of Us Part II: presets with independent controls

[Naughty Dog's published feature list](https://www.naughtydog.com/blog/the_last_of_us_part_ii_accessibility_features_detailed) documents vision/hearing/motor presets with individual overrides; remapping; alternatives to repeated presses and sustained holds; camera assistance; high-contrast presentation; navigation/traversal audio; and separate motion options. It also supplies subtitle size/background controls. **[V]**

For Boogle, borrow composable controls and cue redundancy, not a sixty-option menu or combat systems. A motor preset might combine a one-hand layout, tap-to-work and camera assistance. A comfort preset should not silently activate automatic turning, which can conflict with motion sensitivity. Keep each setting editable and previewable. Menu narration alone would not make the island navigable without sight; that is a separate, substantial design and participant-testing track. **[D]**

### Forza: automate execution while preserving decisions

[Turn 10's accessibility account](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2023/04/27/forza-motorsport-accessibility-features-blind-driving/) describes Blind Driving Assists developed with blind/low-vision participants, audio-cue previews and a learning curve. One Touch Driving lets players choose combinations of steering, braking, throttle and other assists to reduce simultaneous inputs and sustained pressure. [Forza's current support documentation](https://support.forza.net/hc/en-us/articles/46524064744851-Forza-Motorsport-Accessibility-Support) retains configurable assist presets and individual settings. **[V]**

Boogle's equivalent is letting someone choose a tree or hook target, start the ordinary action and cancel it easily; it is not automatically choosing the route, spending materials or finishing the puzzle for them. A taught “target in reach” sound is more useful than making every nearby collectible emit a continuous sound. A small cue-preview panel can teach that vocabulary before it becomes a memory burden. **[D]**

### Celeste: assistance wording is part of access

[Celeste's official changelog](https://www.celestegame.com/changelog.html) records the revised Assist Mode introduction, toggle Grab, expanded rebinding including menu inputs, and removal of a hard-coded shortcut that could conflict with remaps. [Clinton Lexa's first-person account](https://halfcoordinated.medium.com/gaming-accessibility-and-language-my-full-interview-response-regarding-celestes-assist-mode-b52ee22d6821), as a contributor to the revised introduction, explains how the original wording made some players feel that assisted play was less legitimate. **[V]** The [GAG example's captured shipped menu](https://gameaccessibilityguidelines.com/celeste-assist-mode/) shows speed, stamina, dash and invincibility controls; this is secondary screenshot evidence, not a current build inspection. **[S]**

Use neutral labels such as “Tap to keep working” and “Camera follows movement”; describe the action changed, without “easy”, “cheat”, a public badge or reduced rewards. Unlike Celeste's single-player speed control, a per-tab slow-motion setting cannot slow Boogle's shared railway clock. Prefer input assistance and authored recovery routes; a shared timing change would be a separate gameplay decision. **[D]**

The [My Zelda Cane paper's abstract](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.08031) reports analysis of more than 70 hours of blind players' videos, including appropriation of game mechanics and trade-offs among access, agency and engagement. Only the abstract was inspected. It supports observing players' strategies, not prescribing a particular Boogle navigation system or claiming that more sound solves blindness. **[V/D]**

## Ranked fixes: impact and cost

Priority orders likely exclusion first, then breadth and effort; it is not an implementation schedule around the owner's replay. **High** impact can block participation in a core activity. **Medium** chiefly reduces fatigue, confusion or missed optional content. **S** is roughly 1–2 focused engineering days, **M** 3–7, **L** 1–3 weeks, including integration and focused agent verification but excluding participant recruitment and owner acceptance. These are uncertain planning estimates. A row combines related tasks; ship and judge smaller pieces. **[D]**

## Numbered recommendations for Boogle

1. **Separate stabilizing follow from optional motion, and expose comfort before entry.** In [Buddy camera](../../src/player/buddy/camera.ts), keep the existing height filter available while independently suppressing sprint FOV; do not change the owner's ordinary 5.2 m/direct-follow default. Replace the one-shot [constant](../../src/constants.ts) with an effective Local preference consumed by the existing owners, using Follow system / Reduced / Full and later individual overrides. Preserve explicit choices across reload; quality presets must not overwrite them. Put access to settings beside entry and in [Pause](../../src/features/pause.ts), not a forced controls card. Reconcile in-session CSS and JavaScript behavior. The first proof below is intentionally smaller than this full wiring. **[D; XAG 117/WebKit]**

2. **Give text a readable default, scalable layout and semantic equivalent.** Extend [UI tokens](../../src/ui/ui-kit.css), [Pause](../../src/features/pause.css), [talk](../../src/proto/place/talk.ts) and [sign painting](../../src/proto/place/signs.ts): start essential DOM text at the existing 18 CSS px play token, offer 100/125/150/200%, and reflow labels plus action glyphs without clipping. These are Boogle starting values, not a conversion of Apple's points or XAG's display pixels. Add an on-demand nearby sign transcription with the exact board content and adjustable opaque background; keep boards scene-lit, improve placement/light where necessary, and never alter Boogler wording. Make all settings reachable with keyboard, pad and touch; verify focus and complete-line announcements. Scope touch gesture suppression to gameplay so readable menus can scroll/zoom where supported. **[D; XAG 101/102, Apple]**

3. **Remap actions through every actual input path.** Extend [controls](../../src/player/controls.ts), [input](../../src/player/input.ts), [movement intent](../../src/entities/player/state.ts), [gamepad](../../src/features/gamepad.ts), [guide](../../src/player/guide.ts) and [conversation](../../src/player/conversation.ts). Include movement, look, interact, jump, use, drop, hook/glide, pause and menu navigation; detect conflicts, allow alternatives and expose a reachable reset/cancel. Prompts must show the effective binding. Release held/latching actions on blur, modal entry, device disconnect and profile change. Preserve default Interact X/Square and Back B/Circle. Keyboard-only camera pitch and user-adjustable sensitivity/inversion need inclusion, not just mouse remapping. **[D; XAG 107, Celeste]**

   Standard-engine precedent supports using action identities: [Unity binding overrides](https://docs.unity3d.com/Packages/com.unity.inputsystem@1.14/manual/ActionBindings.html), [Unreal Enhanced Input mapping contexts](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/enhanced-input-in-unreal-engine) and [Godot InputMap](https://docs.godotengine.org/en/stable/classes/class_inputmap.html) separate actions from input events. Boogle already has the action registry; the missing part is complete routing and player configuration, not importing another engine or adding an input framework. **[V/D]**

4. **Make the whole basic job possible with sequential inputs.** In [touch/input](../../src/player/input.ts), [touch layout](../../src/player/touch.css), [hands](../../src/player/hands.ts) and [Place moves](../../src/proto/place/moves.ts), offer left/right one-hand layouts, adjustable button positions/sizes, stick swap and optional camera follow that yields immediately to manual look. Prototype tap-to-continue ordinary chopping/feeding and toggle sprint on keyboard, with a second tap or cancellation stopping work; retain normal cadence, capacity, reach and material costs. Distinguish this from an earned faster axe or crane. Keep glide's existing toggle. Test selecting a valid hook while stopped and then acting, rather than requiring a moving aim/chord. Do not promise one-switch access from this preset. Optional auto-jump or route assistance would change gameplay and needs its own brief. **[D; Forza, XAG 107]**

5. **Make material identity and machine state survive loss of colour and animation.** Extend the existing stamp/seal approach in [finds](../../src/proto/place/props.ts), [gifts](../../src/proto/place/gifts.ts), [museum](../../src/proto/place/museum.ts), [station boards](../../src/proto/place/stations.ts) and the [progression brief](../brainstorms/progression.md). Each future material gets a stable silhouette plus icon/name reused at source, bundle, bill and fitting. Ready/full/locked needs a visible physical configuration and optional short explanation, not amber/green or a pulse alone. Keep useful player names independent of outfit colour. Check grayscale and protan/deutan/tritan simulations at normal camera distance, shadow and bright shore, then with players; simulations are triage, not acceptance. Offer selected cue-colour customization only after this redundancy works. **[D; XAG 103]**

6. **Offer calm scenery without changing the shared sea or hiding useful work.** Extend [Graphics](../../src/player/graphics-page.ts), [water presentation](../../src/render/water/), [opening fall](../../src/fall/index.ts), [celebrations](../../src/proto/place/celebrate.ts) and the [evening proposal](room-time-plan-2.md). Begin with the existing caustics-off control, then compare lower decorative glint/foam and stationary collectible presentation. Do not freeze gameplay time, water height queries, swimming or authoritative machine progress. Maintain visible construction stages when flourishes are suppressed. Propose an opt-in calm arrival with the same endpoint and gentle dark crossfade; the selected continuous fall stays default, and the alternate arrival needs owner review. Keep menus above transition veils and avoid a blacked-out live interaction. Evaluate flash/pattern risks separately on captured output; do not label a preset “seizure-safe.” **[D; Apple, XAG 117/118]**

7. **Pair useful sounds with readable outcomes and let players mix them.** Reuse [audio buses](../../src/render/audio.ts), [Pause](../../src/features/pause.ts), [ping](../../src/features/ping.ts), [notifications](../../src/ui/notify.ts) and the [Place verb prompt](../../src/proto/place/verbs.ts). Prototype separate ambience/music and useful-cue levels, mono output and previews for “in reach”, “accepted” and “cannot accept”. Give a cart call/arrival and a friend's ping an optional source/direction-labelled visual equivalent; never require headphones or vibration. Caption meaningful events, not every footstep. Preserve NPC text and offer complete-line announcement independent of babble. Confirmation must reflect accepted gameplay, not a queued action. Follow the current [sync contract](../agents/sync.md); this proposal changes cue presentation, not transaction ownership. **[D; XAG 103, Forza]**

8. **Keep onboarding world-led, with help a player can request again.** Build on [contextual verbs](../../src/proto/place/verbs.ts), [conversations](../../src/player/conversation.ts), [Controls](../../src/player/guide.ts) and the [progression brief](../brainstorms/progression.md). Teach one local action, show its physical result, then combine it with a previously learned action. A requested “What can I do here?” response can explain the nearby machine's input and refusal reason; a return recap can name the last shared change and an available contribution. Make text repeatable, untimed and optionally spoken. No automatic puzzle solution, elapsed-idle diagnosis, permanent objective marker or restoration of removed fork signs. These optional help surfaces need owner agreement because they change information delivery. Retain all existing Boogler copy. **[D; XAG 109, GAG]**

9. **Keep assists private and participation equal.** Apply to [progression](../brainstorms/progression.md), [co-op activities](../../src/proto/place/pair.ts), [moves](../../src/proto/place/moves.ts) and [reward presentation](../../src/proto/place/gifts.ts). A player can enable access options before failing, with no public assist badge, host permission, reward penalty or contribution ranking. Never reserve the “easy job” for them. For every required small-stop puzzle, document a sequential solo solution, safe retry and return path; a friend's departure must not strand the remaining player. Retain shared unlocked capabilities for late joiners. Local presentation/input preferences stay Local; assistance that changes reach, collision, accepted costs or puzzle state needs a separate design and authority review, not a client cheat or a per-tab slow clock. **[D; Celeste language account, DESIGN 4/8/10]**

10. **Judge access with the people affected, alongside agent evidence.** Attach a short accessibility pass to the existing [round-4 tracker](../plans/round-4-notes.md) and later [progression proof](../brainstorms/progression.md), when implementation is authorized. Invite compensated participants with relevant motor, vision, hearing and vestibular needs to choose settings and stop whenever they wish. Test with a friend as well as alone; the friend should not have to translate every cue or take over controls. Record the action that failed, effective settings and device/input combination, not a diagnosis or a single “accessible” score. Existing look/bot evidence establishes rendering and repeatable routes; it cannot establish comfort, comprehension or full screen-reader access. Stop at the owner's verdict rather than autonomously tuning beyond it. **[D]**

## What to prototype first

**Bounded experiment: reduced-motion jumps with height stabilization retained.** First compare the current reduced-motion camera with the same camera retaining its existing vertical filter, while sprint FOV and shake stay off. No new camera constants, route, art or gameplay rules. Own the experiment in [Buddy camera](../../src/player/buddy/camera.ts), with its flag/card following [prototype rules](../plans/find-the-fun.md#rules-for-every-prototype). This is proposed future work, not part of this research commit. **[D]**

**Gut question:** “Can you jump and ride while keeping the view comfortable?” **What's new:** “Reduced motion keeps the steady jump camera.”

Before the owner's short trial, the agent checks level walking, repeated jumps, a long drop, boarding/turning/leaving a train and entering a room, with matched stills, motion sheets and camera/FOV curves through the existing look harness. Confirm ordinary mode is unchanged, no late lens jump occurs and the landing/target stays visible. A participant comfort check must be voluntary; do not make someone replay a known triggering baseline. Keep a reachable stop. Reject the variant if it creates residual drift, pumping, obscured footing or worse comfort. Stop for feedback. **[D]**

After that verdict, wire entry-time preference selection and live updates; then do the readable-sign/text proof. The first motor proof is one tree → carry → feed → visible track sequence, solo, using sequential one-hand input with unchanged work rate and costs. Only after that passes should the same access path be tested through hook/glide gates and with a departing co-op partner. Do not build the eight-hour content arc to discover these constraints later. **[D]**

| Motion/flash | Water crossing, cloud entry, rewards and overlapping effects; matched baseline/comfort recordings | Camera/effect behavior matches settings; no invented comfort claim from stills; flash analysis separate from subjective comfort. |
| Co-op/cognitive | Newcomer plus experienced friend; muted play, departure and late return at a gate | Newcomer selects and completes a meaningful job, can recall help, retains shared access, and is not forced into spectator status. |

Device coverage is a representative release/accessibility pass, not a demand to retest every device after each tiny edit. The owner's Mac-first workflow and device-testing cadence still apply. Measure touch targets physically; CSS pixels, device pixels, native points and millimetres are not interchangeable. Test simultaneous large text, reduced motion and one-hand settings, since defaults that work individually can collide in combination. **[D]**

## Evidence limits and disposition

The audit confirms implementation structure, not the frequency or severity of player failure. It did not measure final-frame sign contrast, touch-target millimetres, screen-reader announcements, one-handed success, flash thresholds, real-time comfort or device performance. No claim of WCAG/XAG conformance, blind-play completion or all-iPhone support is made. The live sources establish documented patterns; commercial games were not played and no inaccessible talk recording is cited as watched. Native Apple features are guidance, not browser capabilities promised by this report.

Documentation validation: Prettier, targeted lint, local link-target checks and `npm run check` passed. These checks validate the repository change, not the proposed accessibility behavior.

Disposition: **research complete; bounded experiment proposed**. Prioritize the small camera proof, readable information and complete control routing; defer new route assists and optional onboarding surfaces to their design decision and current replay gate. This serves **Telegraph everything**, **Cooperation is automatic, not negotiated**, **Designed for drop-in, drop-out**, **Slapstick over precision** and **Small and readable**. The shared railway stays the same achievement; each friend gets a usable way to take part.
