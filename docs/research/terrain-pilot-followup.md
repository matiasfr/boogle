## Changes and diagnosis

- **Steep slopes:** `cove-ground.ts` patches the existing heightfield material with world-height sandstone beds and a continuous inset turf/soil boundary. All vertices of steep triangles receive zero turf coverage; the 0.72 upward-normal cutoff is more conservative than the controller's 0.55 walkable threshold. The pilot lies wholly inside the material region's full-strength bounds. No vertex displacement or new texture; only a static scalar attribute and colour/normal nodes. Large triangle shading is softened without changing the physical surface.
- **Continuous headland:** eight joined modules now follow 32 m of coast, z 42–74, turning back into the shoulder. Larger, tilted foot blocks replace the evenly detached stones. Render, collision and water-bed queries still consume the same mesh recipe.
- **Stopping:** the original ramp is walkable (roughly 32–35 degrees), and the surface audit found no collider mismatch. Player friction is deliberately zero; horizontal braking alone leaves gravity to generate a fresh downhill contact impulse. The flag's existing cove entity now cancels tangential gravity on actual idle, walkable static contact, retaining downward normal pressure. It skips input, swimming, moving decks, airborne support and upward impulses. This is Owner physics, with no wire/save/lockstep change and no default behavior change.
