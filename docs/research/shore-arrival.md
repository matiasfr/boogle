# Shore arrival: the first two minutes after landing

_Research report, 4 November 2026. Ten opening routes, first NPC asks, shore hazards, co-op arrivals and three placements for round 4. Builds on [wordless-onboarding.md](wordless-onboarding.md), especially sections 1–2 (teaching and lures), 4 (browser pacing) and 5 (joining friends), rather than repeating them. Live searches and page reads; repository facts rechecked at `392ee52d` after review (original inspection `45d042cd`). Recommendations only; the owner's shore idea is not a decision. Serves DESIGN.md 7 (Telegraph everything), 8 (Designed for drop-in, drop-out), 10 (Slapstick over precision) and 11 (Show, don't tell)._

## Summary

Try a small west-shore cove leading into the village, with a safe landing, one optional jump and one bend that reveals people at work. At town, a little guy can ask for help in one passing line, then leave the existing northbound path to do the guiding. Keep the sky trolley and title fall, all unlock timing and returning players' saved positions.

The existing village-to-machine walk already takes roughly 78 seconds without exploration. Aim for **25–35 seconds from standing on shore to town; review above 45 seconds**. Two minutes to the machine is a direct-run possibility, not a first-player guarantee. Blind discovery remains unproved ([round-4 notes](../plans/round-4-notes.md#still-to-find-out)). This **bounded experiment** waits for the owner's replay and authorization.

**Evidence key:** **P** = fetched primary text; **S** = walkthrough, community account or search-only evidence; **C** = current repository or calculation; **H** = hypothesis/estimate. No reference game was played or stopwatch-timed for this report. Reference timings below are deliberately broad **H pacing estimates**, excluding menus and opening movies, for the stated endpoints; they are not sourced measurements. Reliable metre scales were not found: reference path lengths use named traversal legs, not invented metres. Boogle lengths are in its authored metres.

Other sources are the corresponding route links above. **An NPC asking for help does not inherently conflict with rule 1**: residents already use spatial bubbles and optional Talk ([place: town](../game-design/place.md#the-town-you-can-go-into)). Persistent instructions, repeated chasing or required conversation to open the path **would conflict** with world-led direction and optional town activity.

Use a worker's gesture for **where**, one short line for **why**. A little guy fits visible labour; leave The Boogler's copy intact. Put the worker outside: Talk was offered eleven times and pressed zero ([playtest](../playtests.md#barely-tried)).

## 3. Shore failure modes

**Evidence strength: strong for current Boogle contracts; proposed dimensions/recovery targets remain H.** [First-run](../game-design/first-run.md) already supplies the place's target, yaw and terrain height to player, trolley and letters. The fall is about 7.5 s _in the air_, not the whole click-to-control sequence.

| Failure                             | Proposed prevention and proof                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sea immediately under/behind spawn  | A dry **14–18 m-wide landing clearing**, with its centre at least **10 m inland from the tested wet edge**. Height must clear wave crests, not just mean sea level. Test standing, backward input and low camera.                                               |
| Falling in becomes the first lesson | A shallow, walkable way out beside the clearing; failed optional jumps land on sand. Target **under 5 s** back to the route. Preserve swimming and existing recovery rather than adding a death/tutorial reset. Test both current and new-water routes.         |
| Wrong-way beach walk                | An opening in real rocks/vegetation frames the uphill path; short shore detours reconnect. No invisible wall, forbidden exploration, beacon or idle arrow. Avoid giving the open sea a brighter focal point than town.                                          |
| Camera faces water or a wall        | Set the arrival yaw toward the first inland segment; turn the path gently to reveal roofs and a moving worker. Keep the sea in peripheral view. No forced orbit once control returns. Check mouse, pad and touch framing.                                       |
| Title fall lands on a slope         | Grade a genuinely level patch in the terrain/collider, not a visual disc. Test the whole steering funnel and the later trolley/letter scatter, simultaneous arrivals and reload during falling. No stuck get-up, roll into water, or letters plugging the exit. |

**Binding order:** [Look: “2026-11-04, terrain follows water, with level design”](../decisions.md#look) puts terrain adoption **after the real sea lands**, in the larger terrain/level-design pass. If separately authorized, a local `?proto=` variant may test a disposable landing/ramp with matching mesh and collision, leaving default terrain unchanged; that is not adoption. **Every option's lasting shore grade belongs to the later pass.** The same section's 3 November coast call leaves `&shelf=demo` undecided: broader shelf adoption could change these default-terrain samples (today's demo affects the east shore). Default west samples rise about **2 m per 2 m horizontally**, so a coordinate swap alone is insufficient ([terrain](../../src/proto/place/terrain.ts)).

## 4. Co-op and return

**Evidence strength: strong for existing per-player presentation; proposed arrival behavior untested.** Keep the existing split: local arrival/camera and personal heard-lines; shared railway/unlocks. [Residents](../../src/proto/place/residents.ts) already select the newest town fact rather than a backlog. [Wordless onboarding §5](wordless-onboarding.md#5-joining-friends-mid-session) already explains why arrivals must not pause the room.

- **Two first-timers, second joins mid-walk:** second sees their own trolley/title fall and shore; first keeps moving. No party gate or scene on the first player's camera. Loading, falling and walking separate them: reunion is not guaranteed, and the route must work without an escort.
- **New person, advanced room:** same shore approach, inherited abilities usable immediately, optional glide over dry ground. Suppress the ask if the machine is awake; existing town-change dialogue and construction explain the present. No replayed device tutorials.
- **Ask repetition:** one automatic local bark per newcomer who reaches town while the machine still sleeps. It can repeat for a _different person_, never as a global announcement. After walking away, no automatic nag; optional **Talk (X/Square)** can recover the still-relevant line. If a partner wakes the machine during the approach, cancel the queued ask.
- **Returning person / reload mid-walk:** restore the room's valid saved standing pose and facing; no fall and no compulsory shore replay. Keep the existing unsafe/missing-pose fallback and Get unstuck distinct from the new first-visit landing ([first-run](../game-design/first-run.md), [return-spot](../../src/features/return-spot.ts)).

## 5. Three round-4 placements

**Evidence strength: C for anchors and arithmetic; H for proposed paths, safety and timings.** Coordinates below are **local x,z**; add 1000 to x for world space. Current village spawn is `(-97,14)`, town elevation 18 m, and the opening path starts `(-98,9)` ([layout](../../src/proto/place/layout.ts)). Node sampling of default-terrain `H` (no `&shelf=demo`) found coast candidates A `(-209,10)` at 2.27 m, B `(-205,65)` at 2.64 m, C `(-183,-65)` at 2.49 m. These are **shore anchors, not certified landing points**: move inland and grade the clearing/ramp. All routes aim for the same town threshold before `PATHS[0]`, subject to the terrain gate in §3. Moving the landing loses rule 1's immediate composition: **wide path, half-built forge with hammering little guys, crane, rail stub and curio shelf** ([place, rule 1](../game-design/place.md#the-run)). Threshold aims below are H, not verified visibility.

**Sign angles (C):** [both signs](../../src/proto/place/signs.ts) face `(-98,9)`: Timber at `(-104,1)`, Quarry at `(-104.5,18)`. Angle between front normal `(target − sign)` and `(anchor − sign)`, ignoring occlusion: A **122.0°/121.5°**, B **94.5°/150.9°**, C **166.7°/79.2°** (Timber/Quarry; 0° front, 90° edge, >90° back). A sees both backs until near the threshold; B starts behind both; C sees Timber's back and Quarry almost edge-on. Both face the threshold directly. Thus the NPC's turn toward Timber Camp belongs **at the threshold after the front is readable**, not on the shore approach. Final bends and actual sightlines still need testing.

Walking speed is **5.6 m/s**, sprint **9.5 m/s** ([player state](../../src/entities/player/state.ts)); budgets include looking/jumping, not prolonged detours. **Sprint is available from the start** ([Controls, Sprint row](../game-design/controls.md)); invite it on the climb without requiring it. Only the [second jump, hook shot and glider](../game-design/place.md#ancient-technology-round-4-exploring) are later abilities. All routes remain walkable without those abilities; unlocked glide stays optional. The three graded ramps are prototype hypotheses subject to §3's terrain gate.

Keep the [existing opening-walk handoff](../game-design/place.md#guidance-after-removing-the-objective): north path → sign/lookout → footbridge → ridge/camp smoke → machine → **Wake it up**. The document says **431 m/about 78 s**; current 3D `PATHS[0].pts` sum to **435.8 m / 77.8 s** at walking speed. Preserve the later **191 m** bridge shortcut's relief. With A, a 6 s greeting and 3 s wake, standing-to-awake is approximately **112–122 s**, before discovery pauses. The two-minute aim is town plus onward intention, not compulsory machine activation.

Round 4's **pier is at Far Head**, reached after trains; it is not the village arrival dock ([place, run step 10](../game-design/place.md#the-run)). None of these options moves that ending to spawn.

## What this means for Boogle

1. **Compare west-cove A with today's village landing first.** Keep the other shore placements as fallbacks. Aim for 25–35 s to town, review above 45 s; charge the addition against the existing 78 s opening walk. Owners: [layout](../../src/proto/place/layout.ts), [round-4 plan](../plans/round-4.md). **Evidence: C + H.**
2. **Teach move/look, then one forgiving jump; make sprint and unlocked glide optional.** Leave the main walk usable with starting abilities, and retain a dry recovery route. Invite starting sprint on the climb. Any authorized [terrain](../../src/proto/place/terrain.ts) test grade stays local to the variant; lasting grading waits for the [post-water terrain/level-design pass](../decisions.md#look). Supply target/yaw through [fall arrival](../../src/fall/arrival.ts). **Evidence: P/S precedent, C seam, H dimensions.**
3. **Let a little guy greet in passing: “Machine's asleep. Give us a hand?”** Proposed trigger: grounded newcomer within **6 m**, worker visible; worker steps out from the forge, waves and turns toward camp. One **4–6 s** bubble, no camera lock or response requirement; optional Talk afterwards. The path must read if the line is missed. Owners: [residents](../../src/proto/place/residents.ts), [town-lines](../../src/proto/place/town-lines.ts). **Evidence: C Talk non-use; H wording/trigger.**
4. **Make each newcomer hear only a currently true ask; let everyone else continue.** Keep returning poses, suppress the sleeping-machine ask in progressed rooms, and never require the group to regroup or replay a scene. Owners: [first-run](../game-design/first-run.md), [Place arrival/restore](../../src/proto/place/index.ts), residents' personal history. **Evidence: C contracts; H join experience.**
5. **Build only a bounded comparison if the owner authorizes it after the replay.** Proposed `?proto=place&v=shore-arrival&intro=1` in a dedicated test room, with today's landing as control and `&greeting=off` for the same shore geometry. Reuse meshes and the existing fall; one clearing, one ramp, one optional hop and one worker. Owners: [prototype rules](../plans/find-the-fun.md#rules-for-every-prototype), [protos card](../../protos/protos.ts), [Place](../../src/proto/place/index.ts). **Evidence: H; not implemented.**
6. **Ask one playtest question: “Did landing on the shore make you want to head into town and help?”** Before handing it over, inspect fall/landing/motion evidence and test wrong-way swimming, missed jump, two arrivals, mid-walk join, return and progressed-room suppression. Record stand-up→town, town→machine, water recoveries and whether the bark was seen using [existing marks](../../src/proto/mark.ts). Watch a first-timer without directions; separate enjoyable detours from confusion. Stop at the owner's playtest, not autonomous tuning. **Evidence: C existing observation gap; H success criteria.**
