## 1. Protect the work, permit the shortcut

**[H] Two separate graphs describe each connection:**

- **Travel:** where a person can stand, with which ability, from which direction, and how they return. A new jump, hook or glide may add edges here.
- **Work:** which delivered stock, completed construction and operated mechanism enable a service. Reaching the far side adds no edges here by itself.

For each work edge record `prerequisite → action → durable result`, and for each travel edge record `origin → destination; ability; return`. A shortcut is acceptable when it leaves the work edge intact. This does **not** prohibit carrying a bundle by a faster route: delivered materials still count. Nor does it make taking a train compulsory when an earned move is more fun. What must remain indispensable is building the connected railway and activating its freight service, not every prescribed metre of the player's commute.

**[R] There is already a partial semantic boundary.** [run.ts](../../src/proto/place/run.ts), `activeFrame`, `gateWaits`, `pierWaits` and `feedStation`, select the current station, reject Q4/Q5 deposits while their gates are shut and hold Q6 for trains. Flying beyond a slab does not advance those checks. But [tech.ts](../../src/proto/place/tech.ts), the Interact block, lets a nearby player pull the quarry lever without a construction prerequisite. Merely making that ledge taller or adding a ramp cannot make construction indispensable.

**[H] Proposed invariant:** with every movement ability available but Q3 unfinished, a player may stand on the quarry apron, yet the winch remains visibly disconnected, the quarry gate stays closed and Q4 accepts no stock. After Q3 finishes, a player with no extra jump can walk up, operate the winch and fund Q4. This is the smallest meaningful distinction between reaching a worksite and making it work.

## 5. Smallest quarry redesign

**[H] Reuse Q3, the apron, chain, winch, lever and gate. Add one broad access ramp and a visible drive connection; do not add another resource type, separate ramp bill or movement lock.**

1. **Before Q3 completion:** the quarry can be scouted from the southern path. The lever ledge may be reached by skilled movement, but its winch drive is visibly absent/disengaged. It cannot open the gate. The unfinished ramp and missing connection explain what Q3 will build.
2. **On completed Q3 construction:** assemble the service ramp and connect the drive. Derive readiness from the completed Q3 job, not a delivered-stock threshold, player position or local animation timeout. Keep deployment clear of the moving machine; a late join sees the settled completed assembly.
3. **Baseline walk:** keep the 26.2 m apron and lever. A starting envelope is **3 m clear width, 9 m horizontal run, 2.2 m rise** (24.4% grade), from the 24 m quarry side. These are proposed dimensions, not collision-checked placement. Fit its ends to actual ground/pad surfaces; preserve stock stance, the machine/cargo envelope and the lever's chain.
4. **Operate, then build:** any player walks up and pulls the existing lever. That shared result opens the quarry gate; Q4 still needs its 16 logs + 16 rails and machine work. Ramp readiness is derived; the already separate gate fact remembers the pull. A newcomer can do this alone after the builder leaves.

The logical rule is `Q3 complete AND lever operated → quarry gate open`. The ramp provides inclusive access; the winch connection protects the dependency when extra jumps, a longer hook or a glider can reach the ledge early. A permanently lowered lever, a permanently open gate, or a walkable ramp alone would remove the jump dependency but fail to make construction the required operation. Making an infinitely high wall would oppose the movement brief.

**First-hook companion change:** after Q2 completes, join the finished deck to p2 with a broad walking connector and a sloped 2.2 m rise. Keep the device at its existing destination and the hook chain intact. Exact connector length/turn needs machine clearance and a landing witness; the 7 m lateral offset is not a ready-made 7 m ramp. If scope only permits the quarry change, the weaker alternative is a compulsory pre-Q2 jump find. That avoids the cycle but leaves the hook dependent on noticing and executing the jump, so it is not the recommended contract.

The later remembered-balcony shortcut in the jump brief is a **different** ramp: an optional movement-earned shared benefit. Do not make the quarry's mandatory work ramp depend on that balcony, its lever or the expedition reward.

### Smallest implementation proof after approval

**[H] Add the new states to the existing checker, then use the existing look/bot harness for exact-build witnesses.** Do not create a new UI test suite. Check these distinct properties:

- **No-find run:** T1→T3, forge, Q1→Q3, p2 claim, quarry lever, Needle Bell switch and Q5 glider can progress without the extra jump. Each current stage's material source/rack and delivery stance must be reachable before its bill is paid.
- **Find run:** reach and leave the proposed southern site with baseline movement; unlock once for the room; absent/late player receives it; mid-air availability waits for landing as specified in the jump brief. Return to the optional course or balcony without making it required.
