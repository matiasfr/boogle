# World detailing: intentional places within a measured art budget

> **Verdict:** One accountable integrator, independent review, a visible-combination budget.
> **Top recommendations:** 1. Budget visible combinations and residency, not eight hours of props. 2. Give each area an intent and a reserved child budget. 3. Use one builder and one holistic review for the first corner.
> **Status:** open. No decision cites it yet.

_Research report, 5 November 2026. Live primary-source research and focused repository inspection at `68ee082d6484111991a59f7a8661338587573ad0`. Covers Mac/mobile art allocations, authored and procedural set dressing, bounded agent supervision, and a proposed one-corner pilot. Research only: no game changes, generated art, new captures, benchmark, pilot execution or owner acceptance._

**Evidence:** **[L]** repository source inspected now; **[R]** a dated local report's measurements, not rerun here; **[V]** primary text/documentation or named developers' interview answers read live; **[A]** official talk description only, recording not watched; **[P]** Boogle proposal or arithmetic, not a measured device limit. ## Answer and scope

**Use recursive review of authored intent, with one accountable integrator; do not start with a recursive tree of agents.** An island establishes the journey and visual identity; an area establishes a material or discovery; a landmark establishes the invitation; a corner makes one action and its history legible; a prop earns its place by supporting that relationship. Refine the least successful relationship, recapture it in context, and stop when it reads. An intentionally empty stretch is a finished outcome.

Start with **one builder carrying a corner through all passes, followed by an independent, read-only review of the integrated result**. The supervisor is a responsibility, not necessarily another continuously running model. Add parallel workers only for isolated assets or separate places with accepted boundaries. Reserve technical and visual budgets before assigning them, and make the parent review the combined scene. This is a hypothesis about Boogle's production process, not evidence that agent taste matches the owner.

The 5 November brief asks for this workflow **when ready to fill the world**. It does not authorize filling it now. [Progression](../brainstorms/progression.md) records the owner's material-based structure: big areas supply components that upgrade several systems; small stops carry puzzles, abilities and discovery; about eight hours in total. Its Glasshouse → Split Shell → Exhibition route and exact ability distribution remain proposals. Do not produce an eight-era asset catalogue, choose that route through an art task, or fill eight hours with decorative density. The current [decision log](../decisions.md#the-shape-of-the-game) and [owner calls](../agents/owner-calls.md#owners-standing-calls) outrank old research and the older derived [direction view](../direction.md).

The accepted anchor is current Place: chunky readable shapes, actual Buddy, current camera, Neutral/HDR exposure and the retained water/sky. Keep simple parts that already read well, including the little workers. New assets start with screenshot-based previz and detail reference sheets, per the owner's latest **Previz before modelling** call. Terrain and the larger beautiful-corner stage retain their explicit terrain/vertical-slice gate in [Look](../decisions.md#look). Early-work exceptions do not establish that the replay passed. This report serves [DESIGN](../../DESIGN.md)'s **Show, don't tell**, **Telegraph everything**, **Designed for drop-in, drop-out**, and **Small and readable**.

### Counting contract

Use these definitions for every future budget card [P]:

- **Draws:** actual backend submissions for a completed frame, with main, shadow and other passes separated. Sum the passes for the total. Geometry groups/material slots can create several draws from one mesh. Compute dispatches, fullscreen pixels and render-target traffic get separate columns; they are not free when the draw count is small.
- **Triangles:** submitted main-view triangles after LOD/culling, including every instance; also record shadow triangles and unique resident geometry. No converting asset-manifest triangles into a whole-scene number without placement multiplicity.
- **Unique props:** reusable geometry/recipe IDs used by that scope, not placement count. A tint does not make a new model. Whole-island counts are the union of child IDs; camera-facing subset and resident catalogue are separate.
- **Materials:** distinct live material bindings in the view and distinct shader/pipeline variants separately. Recolour with shared vertex/instance attributes when appropriate. Four role names do not guarantee four materials or four pipelines.
- **Texture memory:** actual decoded GPU format, dimensions, layers/faces, mip levels and sample count. Deduplicate by shared resource identity. Include retained invisible textures, shadow maps, water buffers, sky/LUTs and post/MSAA attachments in the total; list CPU decoded copies separately. Renderer texture counts and GLB bytes are not memory measurements.
- **Instancing:** record eligible placements, actual batch groups, capacity, active count, bounds and shadow policy. An island-wide batch that keeps every tree submitted is a poor result even with a low draw count.

For repeated geometry, [three.js InstancedMesh](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/InstancedMesh.html) documents shared geometry/material and explicit bounds/update duties [V]. The [kit report](art-kit-pipeline.md#count-instances-visibility-and-passes) inspected r187's WebGPU implementation and found per-range draws inside `BatchedMesh`; do not infer one command from one object [R]. Verify the installed backend before changing that conclusion.

Apple's [GPU architecture explanation](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2020/10602/) describes hidden-surface removal and the extra shading caused by interleaving opaque and translucent work [V]. That supports opaque chunky leaves and sparse transparent accents. It does **not** make hidden triangles, shadow rendering, alpha grass or multiple framebuffer passes free, and native Metal techniques are not automatically exposed by browser WebGPU.

The historical late-town **337** leaves only **113** submissions below the proposed Mac ceiling, and already exceeds the proposed mobile ceiling by **37**. Neither difference is verified headroom: different state, camera and current code must be remeasured. Dense-world production therefore needs both selective removal and batching, not a 113-draw entitlement per new area.

A worked allocation prevents double-spending [P]: reserve **120 main + 40 shadow** for the active area's composite (landmark, yard, corner/interior included), **50+20** for its visible neighbour, **10+0** for all distant proxies, **90+35** for terrain outside those areas plus shared characters/trains/rail, and **30+15** headroom; add **40 other-pass draws** = **450**. Mobile equivalents **70+20, 30+10, 5+0, 75+20, 20+10, 40 other** sum to **300**. A large overlap view must replace near detail with mid representations or reallocate the parent budget. It cannot assign every child its maximum simultaneously. The 40 other-pass reservation must be replaced by observed producer counts before accepting a build.

Keep an initial **20% of each area's allocation unassigned** for integration and changing construction states. Child tasks reserve from the same ledger; savings return to the parent. No worker may raise a cap, clone a global material, add a texture set or introduce a renderer pass to make its own screenshot pass.

### Memory arithmetic and frame calibration

A full mip chain is approximately `base bytes × 4/3`. One 1024² RGBA8 texture is **5.33 MiB**; at an actual 8-bit-per-pixel compressed GPU format it is **1.33 MiB**. A three-map set is approximately **16 MiB** or **4 MiB**, respectively. KTX2 download bytes do not establish the transcoded format. A texture-free kit can spend its texture allowance on nothing. [Art-kit texture evidence](art-kit-pipeline.md#trim-sheets-and-gltf-delivery).

The existing 1024 half-float sky alone consumes about **64 MiB** of payload; two prepared skies about **128 MiB**. At 1440×900, an RGBA16F colour surface is about **9.89 MiB**; four samples require about **39.55 MiB**, before resolve, depth and other targets. These are payload calculations, not observations of which attachments Boogle retains. They explain why the total texture ledger must be separate from the 24/64 MiB art pool. If the observed baseline exceeds the proposed total, document it and revise the allocation or architecture explicitly; do not discard approved sky quality by default.

Use a **60 Hz / 16.67 ms presentation target** on Mac, with **p95 measured GPU work ≤12 ms** as a provisional margin and stable main-thread pacing [P]. Try the same cadence on supported mobile at its chosen resolution; if a supported device needs 30 Hz, compare a **33.33 ms / ≤24 ms GPU** profile and label it as a proposed fallback, not an accepted device downgrade. The existing median span observations do not prove either p95 target. Report unavailable GPU timings as unavailable.

For calibration, use one source/build, fixed view and effective resolution first, then adaptive resolution; normal gameplay speed; empty, working and completed states; six visible friends where the scene supports them; trains/cargo/particles active; cold arrival, first use, 15–20 minutes sustained play and background/return. Serialize GPU measurements on the shared Mac. Retain p50/p95/p99 frame intervals, CPU update/submission, covered GPU spans, allocations, graphics errors and rendering scale. Hold a candidate until its matched baseline comparison separates cost from same-state noise. No new test suite is needed for dressing.

## Part 2: a studio-like detailing flow with agents

### Set dressing begins with causes and relationships

Smith and Worch's [What Happened Here? slides](https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc10/slides/Smith_Harvey_WhatHappenedHereWeb_Notes.pdf) treat environmental storytelling as information the player interprets from a scene, with props, composition and reactive systems working together [V]. For Boogle, use modest physical causes: a worn loading edge faces the railway; repaired timbers face the strain; an empty support frame anticipates a counted machine part. The story can be “people work here and improved this route,” without inventing lore or rewriting Boogler dialogue [P].

Distinguish the passes by what must read, not by how many objects get added [P]:

| Pass                    | Question                                                   | Typical result                                                                | Reject when                                                                         |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Intent/blockout         | Why visit; what can I do; where can I leave?               | Route, work space, reveal and quiet space.                                    | The task needs decoration to hide an unclear objective or broken return.            |
| Primary/hero            | What is this place from across the water or the next bend? | Strong mass, silhouette, destination and one dominant action.                 | Every building competes; hero is visible only in a beauty camera.                   |
| Secondary               | How does it work; who uses it?                             | Supports, loading relationships, structure, paths and coherent prop clusters. | Objects look solid but are not, or imply a resource/action that does not exist.     |
| Tertiary                | What rewards a closer look without competing?              | Sparse chips, tuft clusters, scuffs and one quiet visual joke.                | Detail reads as noise, creates false interactables or pushes beyond the allocation. |
| Subtraction/consistency | What can disappear with no loss of meaning?                | Fewer materials, clearer routes, restored negative space.                     | The agent equates occupied ground with completion.                                  |

An inert offcut that looks exactly like collectible timber is false communication. Use clearly fixed structural scraps, different silhouettes, or omit it. A pile representing counted stock must read accepted stock state; never add fake abundance to improve composition. This follows the owner's **Machines show their work physically** call and the [current station/stock owners](../../src/entities/railway/stations.ts), not an art-only exception.

### What procedural production should automate

Ubisoft's [Far Cry 5 account via SideFX](https://www.sidefx.com/community/far-cry-5/) describes Houdini tools for water, power lines, sand, cliffs, biomes, fog density and the world map integrated into the artists' existing editor [V]. Carrier's [talk abstract](https://www.sidefx.com/learn/talks/procedural-world-generation-far-cry-5/) frames the challenge as natural-looking results across changing terrain, rather than merely filling empty space [A]. The transferable pattern is **artist-authored inputs → bounded dependent generation → editable review**. It is not a reason to install a studio-scale Houdini pipeline for one Boogle corner [P].

Opara's [layered-rule talk](https://www.sidefx.com/learn/talks/believability-in-procedural-modelling/) explicitly frames procedural modelling as interacting simple rules [A]. SideFX's [Scatter and Align documentation](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/sop/scatteralign.html) provides concrete controls for orientation, scale, size-aware placement and inspecting the resulting copies, not just point distributions [V]. For Boogle, first automate fitting supports, orienting small clusters to their use, grounded placement, keep-outs and compatible material batches. Randomness comes last and remains bounded by authored masks [P].

Compare standard engines before inventing architecture:

| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |

| [Houdini Scatter and Align](https://www.sidefx.com/docs/houdini/nodes/sop/scatteralign.html)                                           | Instancing attributes and placement can be inspected and adjusted before committing results.                                                      | Generate candidates from approved rules, review the placed meshes, then commit authored transforms/recipe inputs with stable IDs.                                 |

Boogle keeps TypeScript-authored places, parametric Blender assets, its existing renderer and Rapier/game-state owners. Detail generation should be an **offline authoring step first**. No runtime LLM, new ECS, per-island room or general level editor is needed. Purely decorative variation may remain Local, but random render placement must never silently determine collision, a resource source, a puzzle answer or a shared route. Those changes leave dressing scope and require the relevant gameplay/state contracts.

### What agent research supports, and what it does not

[Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/research/building-effective-agents) distinguishes fixed prompt chains, dynamically delegated work, and evaluator/optimizer loops; it recommends starting with simpler structures [V]. A Boogle corner has a known sequence and strongly coupled composition, so use that sequence as the default. Evaluation becomes useful when it names a specific defect and a testable change; “make it prettier” is not a convergence criterion [P].

Anthropic's [multi-agent research system account](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multi-agent-research-system) reports benefits for independent, breadth-first research and costs for tasks requiring shared context. Its reported roughly 15× chat-token use concerns that system, not Boogle productivity. It also describes persistent artifacts, checkpoints and narrow delegation [V]. Use separate agents when the work is separable, with one integrator of shared palette/layout, and make handoffs point to artifacts rather than lossy prose summaries [P].

[Voyager](https://voyager.minedojo.org/) combines a curriculum, reusable executable skills and iterative feedback from execution, errors and self-verification in Minecraft [V]. It supports keeping successful procedures and testing outputs in the actual environment. Its results do not demonstrate artistic coherence, multiplayer navigation or human taste; Boogle needs real captures and owner judgment for those [P].

The supervisor may be the coordinating agent between passes, with a fresh reviewer taking a different evidence set. This is role separation, not a claim of statistically independent judgment from models. No automatic subagent spawning per landmark, corner or prop. Compare elapsed time, total agent effort, rework and owner attention before increasing concurrency.

### Intent ownership from island to prop

Each node gets a compact **intent card**: stable ID and parent; one player-facing sentence; expected action/state; entry/exit views; dominant shape/material; reuse set; protected envelopes/sightlines; allocation; owned files; baseline hashes; done/reject conditions. Store these beside the eventual owning plan/recipe, not in a new permanent decision database. The owner decides new direction; a supervisor can resolve ordinary composition inside accepted scope [P].

| Level            | Who decides                                                                         | Supervisor reviews                                                                                                    | Children may change                                                                    |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Island / journey | Owner sets experience, progression and look; supervisor proposes the composition.   | Journey silhouette, hierarchy of destinations, material identities, quiet/dense rhythm, return leg, whole-tab budget. | Areas within accepted purpose and geography; no new progression gates or “era” reskin. |
| Area             | Supervisor translates accepted material/discovery role; owner decides new gameplay. | Unique reason to visit, resource-to-upgrade relationship, approach/return, compatibility with adjacent areas.         | Landmark arrangement and local dressing inside protected routes.                       |
| Landmark         | Builder proposes form; supervisor checks area and island views.                     | Promise from distance, honest scale, recognisable silhouette, playable destination.                                   | Secondary pieces, contact, limited palette variants.                                   |
| Corner           | Builder owns the action composition.                                                | Receiver/door/landing legibility, work-space clearance, prop relationships, reverse approach and parent budget.       | Clusters, small orientation/scale choices within declared envelopes.                   |
| Prop             | Builder or bounded asset worker.                                                    | Purpose, silhouette beside Buddy, material fit, pivot/LOD/collider contract and cost.                                 | Named parameters and approved variants, not camera, interaction or parent intent.      |

A worker that cannot satisfy its card returns **evidence plus a proposed parent change**. It does not silently enlarge a corner, move a gate, change the camera or replace the island palette. After a parent changes, invalidate affected child approvals and recapture their views. This is how recursion avoids accumulating locally attractive but globally incoherent results.

### The concrete recursive loop

1. **Pin intent and baseline.** Resolve current decisions and file ownership; capture the real place with Buddy, normal camera and exact state. Record source/build, asset hashes, effective graphics and known problems. Choose the one hierarchy level currently failing.
2. **Allocate before generating.** Parent reserves draw/triangle/material/texture/collider allocations for this child and keeps 20% spare. Define no-placement volumes around walk paths, cargo sweep, machine motion, doors, jump/hook/glide routes, landing/recovery and camera sightlines. Baseline a walk in both directions.
3. **Compose coarse forms.** Produce screenshot-based previz for the selected corner plus a wider context view. Keep scale, route, water and Buddy fixed. Treat reference as intent, not measured geometry. A new asset also gets front/side/back and character-bearing close-ups. Machines need motion beats/parts/pivots before shell beauty.
4. **Build the smallest useful layer.** Reuse the kit. If new geometry is needed, follow [asset-author](../../.claude/skills/asset-author/SKILL.md): canonical recipe, built-GLB inspection, decoded metrics and actual Buddy stage. Do not use new props to repair an upstream navigation failure.
5. **Inspect and critique with evidence.** Builder opens captures, checks the action and counts. Each finding names view/frame, object, observed defect, violated criterion, likely cause and smallest repair. The reviewer also sees the parent view, unflattering reverse/underneath views and a version with tertiary detail removed. No combined “beauty score” can average away a collision defect.
6. **Recurse only on an unresolved reason.** If the landmark reads and secondary structure explains it, stop; a prop need not get a microdetail pass. If the corner fails because the area lacks a focal point, return upward rather than filling lower levels. At most one initial version plus two diagnosed repairs for a node; stop sooner when the same repair fails.
7. **Integrate and review siblings together.** Parent checks shared seams, repeated motifs, simultaneous draw/residency peaks and navigation. Refresh the shared ledger after integration; a worker's isolated asset-stage cost is not its cost in the island. Freeze accepted revisions and dependencies.
8. **Owner judges the playable result.** Present one line on what changed and one gut question. Keep the baseline/candidate and evidence accessible; stop at the owner's playtest. Record the answer in the owning playtest/plan before expanding to a second arrangement.

This is a bounded depth-first loop through **island → area → landmark → corner → prop**, with upward invalidation when a parent assumption fails. The content hierarchy has five levels; concurrency does not multiply by depth. A queue of cards and existing named jobs is enough to try it. Do not build an orchestration framework until the pilot shows a specific coordination failure.

### Supervisor criteria and consistency guards

The supervisor rejects a candidate for any of these, even if another view looks excellent [P]:

- **Intent:** cannot say why this cluster belongs here; new detail implies an unavailable interaction; counted materials are decorative fiction.
- **Composition:** destination/worker/receiver loses priority at normal game distance or phone framing; neighbouring landmarks lose their distinction; the reverse route is neglected.
- **Coherence:** changed Buddy proportions, camera, scale, material language, exposure or sky to improve one image; distinctive simple shapes are needlessly made noisy.
- **Playability:** blocked stance, cargo swing or camera; false foothold; route/secret revealed unintentionally; solid-looking structure lacks appropriate collision; return/recovery fails.
- **Budget:** peak states exceed parent allocation, new pipelines/textures lack allocation, batch count exceeds capacity, or residency depends on unimplemented eviction.
- **Evidence:** missing source identity, silent debug completion/teleport, a stale capture, incomplete spatial coverage reported green, graphics errors, or an asset-stage image substituted for in-game proof.

Keep the style anchor and approved palette/material roles fixed in every task packet. Use stable seeds for approved scatter and record recipe/dependency hashes; changing a common generator invalidates its descendants. Keep accepted and failed versions with concise cause/change/result notes. A visual critic suggests corrections; numeric checks enforce mechanical limits; only the owner accepts the target look and feel.

## Numbered recommendations for Boogle

1. **Budget visible combinations and residency, not eight hours of props.** Start with the provisional 450/300 total-submission and 700k/350k main-triangle envelopes, then calibrate exact late-game views through [place-draws](../../dev/look/evals/place-draws.ts) and [graphics measurements](graphics-switch-costs.md). Preserve current asset-file limits in [manifest.ts](../../dev/assets/manifest.ts).
2. **Give each area an intent and a reserved child budget.** Extend the eventual place recipe/owning plan with the compact card, parent allocation and protected routes; build on [places-as-modules](places-as-modules.md) and [progression](../brainstorms/progression.md), without treating proposed destinations as approved content.
3. **Use one builder and one holistic review for the first corner.** Apply the bounded loop through [existing Codex jobs](../../dev/codex/README.md); parallelize only isolated assets or accepted independent areas, with exclusive file ownership and serial GPU measurements.
4. **Dress hero, secondary and tertiary relationships in that order, then subtract.** Preserve current [Place composition](../../src/place/layout.ts), [look](../../src/place/look.ts) and [station stock truth](../../src/entities/railway/stations.ts); make materials and useful work legible rather than increasing decorative density.
5. **Automate constrained repetition with the existing kit.** Extend [look-kit](../../src/place/look-kit.ts) and [parametric assets](../../assets/src/place-kit.py) only when the pilot needs it; use spatial batches, role materials, stable placement inputs and explicit capacities from the [draw audit](freeze-draw-audit.md).
6. **Make screenshots, asset inspection and navigation a single review packet.** Combine [asset-author](../../.claude/skills/asset-author/SKILL.md), [look](../../.claude/skills/look/SKILL.md), [level-check](../../dev/level/check.ts) and actual route witnesses; stale images, unadapted solids and missing device evidence remain explicit failures or limits.
7. **Prototype T3's 12 × 12 m receiving corner first.** Use [spatial-station](../../src/place/spatial-station.ts) and the existing T3 audit; cap it at six IDs, 24 placements, at most one new prop within those IDs and zero textures. Preserve gameplay and the larger [beautiful-corner terrain gate](../plans/beautiful-corner.md).
8. **Let the owner accept the place, then measure reuse before scaling.** Record the verdict in [playtests](../playtests.md) and the owning plan, stop at that gate, and only afterward compare a second placement's integration/rework/owner time with the first. No automatic art acceptance or claim that a faster generator made a better world.
