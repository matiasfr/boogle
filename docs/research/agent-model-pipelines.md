# Agent-made models: keep the recipe, inspect the result

_2026-11-04. Research only: local source inspection and live primary-source reading; no generation, installations, paid tasks or benchmark reproduction. Links below were read on this date unless explicitly marked indexed-only. Vendor timings, prices and demonstrations are attributed, not independently measured. Proposed budgets and judgements are ours._

**Use Astra to author small, parametric Blender Python assets, then inspect the exported asset in Boogle.** The missing capability is a repeatable geometry-and-image review loop, not another generator. Image-to-3D is useful reference material or an occasional rigid prop source; it is not demonstrated as a dependable replacement for Buddy's authored proportions, topology and rig.

## Numbered recommendations

1. **Keep editable recipes in `assets/src`.** Extend the existing [Python generators](../../assets/src/) with named parts, dimensions, pivots and deliberate normals. Astra is the Codex-first default; evidence does not establish superiority over Opus **5.5** for this job. Preserve the [own-Buddy character decision](../decisions.md#characters-and-story): faceted toy scenery, softer Buddy, Bricktron-like proportions as reference, not copied assets.
2. **Make inspection part of authoring.** Require fixed multi-view silhouettes, wireframes, numeric checks, export/reimport and an [asset-stage](../game-design/asset-stage.md) turntable before owner review. Limit a trial to one initial result and two repairs; keep failures and the best version. An attractive front render is insufficient.
3. **Reuse the build, catalogue and material presets.** Add checks around [the existing builder](../../dev/assets/build.ts), not a second pipeline. Use [surface presets](../../src/render/materials.ts), preserve uncompressed interchange and provenance for [Unreal portability](unreal-alignment.md), and attach evidence to the proposed [review inbox](dev-tools-agentic.md). Its versioned review records are not implemented by the current stage.
4. **Use paid generation selectively.** Meshy MCP spends credits independently of an LLM subscription. Reserve it for an approved organic/static-prop experiment where rebuilding or retopology is affordable. Record task IDs, costs and source rights; reject fused parts, painted-on structure and unusable backs before spending on texture or rigging.
5. **Propose one small `asset-author` skill.** Borrow inspection, explicit measurements and export checks from the licensed examples below; retain Boogle's existing concept-art and look workflows. A future `assets:new` command should scaffold a recipe and review brief, not autonomously purchase generations or approve art.
6. **Prove the loop on a feeder roller and conductor cap.** Test a mechanical part and an attachment to existing Buddy, including a requested dimensional revision. Stop at the owner's visual judgement. Do not build a general modelling framework, agent swarm, automatic character replacement or another asset browser.

## What already works here

The [pipeline plan](../plans/asset-pipeline.md) is substantially real: top-level `.py`, `.blend`, `.glb` and `.gltf` sources feed Blender/export, glTF optimisation, Meshopt, optional KTX2 and a committed manifest. [Source hashing](../../dev/assets/manifest.ts) covers the source and same-stem JSON, **not imported helpers, textures or tool versions**; address that before claiming reproducibility across shared generators. Current checks cover freshness, download/texture budgets and related asset health, not comprehensive geometry validity or a per-asset triangle ceiling.

[Place-kit](../../assets/src/place-kit.py) already uses measured pieces, bevels, weighted normals and axle exceptions to base-centre pivots. [Scaffold-kit](../../assets/src/scaffold-kit.py) supplies named animation. [Buddy](../../assets/src/buddy.py) combines remeshed/decimated skin with rigid parts and existing bone conventions; its 5,000-triangle skin target is not the total: the [built manifest](../../assets/built/manifest.json) records 8,972 triangles. Replacing this with a generic humanoid would discard useful work.

There is a local failure report too: the [November 2 character decision](../decisions.md#characters-and-story) says earlier code-built figures and agent-authored run cycles did not satisfy the owner. Editable code is a production advantage, not an aesthetic guarantee; preserve the accepted Buddy direction and existing clips.

The [completed stage plan](../plans/asset-stage.md) provides `/character/` (`/assets/` alias), catalogue, metre grid, bounds, wireframe/bones/sockets, Raw versus As played, repeatable clip sampling and copyable camera/light/time URLs. Its shipped-light approximation excludes some world/post effects; still inspect the asset in context. Two-version comparison remains deferred. Contrary to the older [material report](material-pipeline.md), named presets now exist: timber, paintedToy, foliage, stone, steel and feedback. They are opt-in shared materials, not an automatic imported-glTF material translator; clone before per-instance mutation.

## Models, examples and actual evaluation

**Astra versus Opus:** [BlenderWorld, October 2026](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.23986v1), reports 223 tasks, 2,651 criteria and eight-view-plus-scene evaluation. Astra scored 79.9 versus **Opus 5** at 73.0; perfect-task rates were 42.2% and 25.6%. This is not an Opus 5.5 comparison: harnesses also differ (Codex versus Claude Code), with one run per task and model judging. It does not establish production rig quality or Boogle performance. Useful failures: Astra made people as textured planes that disappear sideways; Opus misread timber-leg height as including casters. These are exactly why side views and semantic dimension checks matter.

[BlenderGym, April 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.01786v1), studies generator/verifier iteration on 245 scenes across placement, lighting, procedural geometry/materials and blend shapes. Its older models and editing tasks do not answer today's model ranking; its multiple fixed views and measured comparison are transferable. Neither evaluation proves that autonomous low-poly character production is solved.

OpenAI's [Astra architectural walkthrough, September 4, 2026](https://developers.openai.com/blog/architectural-visualization-with-astra), and [Physics Museum showcase, undated](https://developers.openai.com/showcase/physics-museum), demonstrate editable Blender-script workflows and rendered/web outputs. They are curated first-party examples, not failure-rate, topology or runtime benchmarks. Anthropic's [Opus 5.5 launch, September 22, 2026](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-opus-5-5), provides no comparable Blender evaluation. Published base API token prices are $4/$20 per million input/output for Opus 5.5 and [$10/$50 for Astra](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/models/gpt-6-astra); context, caching and repeated images change costs, and these are not subscription prices. Measure **cost and elapsed time per accepted asset**, not just one successful prompt. A fair future comparison needs identical briefs, references, tools and retry limits.

## Workflow choices

Costs below exclude artist cleanup unless stated. Speed is workload-dependent; no local timings were taken.

| Route | What it can produce; topology, UVs and rig limits | Editability, verification and cost/speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blender Python / geometry nodes** | Strong fit for low/medium-poly props, kits and rigid accessories. Explicit loops, seams and weights are possible but must be authored; a procedural mesh is not automatically deformable. Exposed GN parameters help families of shapes. | Script/graph remains editable and inspectable. Numeric checks plus rendered views are strong; regeneration avoids hidden scene history. LLM tokens plus local compute; simple primitives are cheap, booleans/remesh/rendering can dominate. Existing Boogle scripts are concrete examples. |
| **Blender MCP** | A bridge to the same Blender operations, not a geometry model. Useful for scene inspection, small edits, documentation and render feedback. Topology/UV/rig quality depends on the invoked procedure. | Keep live experiments in the canonical script. Use headless rebuilds for reproducibility. Both [official Blender Lab MCP](https://www.blender.org/lab/mcp-server/) and [Ahuja's community MCP](https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender) exist; they are distinct projects. Official page: indexed text read, direct fetch blocked. Existing Blender tools were visible here, not health-tested. |
| **three.js procedural geometry** | Excellent for terrain, paths and parameterised assemblies; deliberate vertex buffers can be very lean. UVs, skin weights and animation remain explicit work. | Code is diffable; rendered screenshots and numeric geometry checks work. [GLTFExporter](https://threejs.org/docs/pages/GLTFExporter.html) exports supported scene/material data, not arbitrary TSL behaviour. Low local cost; avoid moving reusable authored props out of the established Blender path. |
| **CAD-like scripting** | Precise mechanical parts through constraints/booleans; tessellation gives triangles rather than animation loops. Usually needs game-oriented UV/material work. Poor default for Buddy. | Strong dimension verification and editable parameters. [CAD-agent's July 2026 proof](https://github.com/kerryshi/cad-agent) reports a physically printed drone plate and independent geometric checks. Author report, not our reproduction; no Astra/Opus 5.5 comparison. Local compute plus agent time. |
| **SDF → mesh** | Useful for rounded blobs, rocks and boolean shape studies. Smooth unions can erase toy planes; sampling creates dense surfaces without intentional deformation loops, UVs or bones. | Keep the field recipe; extracted mesh alone loses controls. [fogleman's SDF examples](https://github.com/fogleman/sdf) demonstrate constructive shapes to STL. Resolution and remeshing trade detail against cost; decimation needs silhouette checks. Not a shortcut to rig-ready topology. |
| **Image → 3D → cleanup** | Good candidate silhouettes and textured static props. Hidden views are inferred; texture can disguise shallow or missing structure. Low face count or all-quads does not establish useful edge flow. Retopologise for deformation, then unwrap/rebake and validate. | Delivered meshes are editable, but usually lack a semantic construction recipe. Cleanup may cost more than rebuilding a simple prop. Visual checks are necessary but less diagnostic than a parameterised source. Service details below. |
| **Kit-bashing** | Best control over repeated toy parts, topology, sockets and material count; inherited UVs/rigs still need consistent conventions. | Cheap, reversible assembly from owned/licensed pieces. Test loops, rotated joins and pivot exceptions. [Burgess's Skyrim GDC write-up, May 2013](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/skyrim-s-modular-approach-to-level-design), documents footprint failures and proof-before-detail; a shipped-game precedent, not an AI benchmark. |

**Rigging and motion are additional pipelines.** [Meshy rigging](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/rigging) documents textured biped inputs and limitations on unclear limbs/non-humanoids; its [text-to-motion](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/api/text-to-motion) produces motion to retarget, not proof that Buddy deforms well. ## Feedback that catches the bad result

Use a short fixed brief: dimensions in metres; labelled parts and attachment points; triangle/material budgets; reference views at consistent scale; required silhouette; forbidden details. The [local concept-art skill](../../.claude/skills/concept-art/SKILL.md) already advocates consistent reference sheets and controlled variations. Its images are references, not permission to regenerate a character indiscriminately.

1. **Block out named parts first.** Record measured dimensions separately from guessed reference dimensions. Match front, side, back and three-quarter silhouettes before bevels, texture or tiny details. Use world-registered cameras; fitting each object independently to its bounding box can hide scale errors.
2. **Inspect actual exported geometry.** Render neutral clay, flat part colours, wireframe and final materials, plus a turntable. Compare silhouette and landmark ratios against the reference. Image similarity alone can reward baked shadows or a billboard; numeric dimensions alone cannot judge charm.
3. **Check contracts automatically.** Count evaluated/exported triangles, vertices, primitives/materials and bytes; inspect normals, degenerates, unexpected disconnected components and manifoldness where a solid is required. Open leaves/sheets need explicit exemptions. Check metre bounds, transforms, pivot/axle/socket placement, unique names, UVs only where needed, bone hierarchy/rest pose, normalised weights and clip names/durations. Reimport the GLB into an empty scene and compare those facts. The official MCP article's indexed example missed render-only Solidify geometry in an earlier count: inspect evaluated render output, not merely base meshes.
4. **See it in Boogle.** Use the same camera, exposure, light and clip time for baseline/candidate; include game-distance views beside Buddy. Record source/build hashes and retained evidence. Stage sampling at 30 Hz is not evidence of the authored clip's original frame rate.
5. **Repair a diagnosed cause, then stop.** Initial result plus two targeted repairs; retain failed views and elapsed/token/service cost. Stop sooner if repeated repairs do not improve the measured defect. An owner judgement remains required; no aggregate image score should auto-accept art.

Blobs arise from indiscriminate smoothing/voxel unions, aggressive decimation and no silhouette specification. Broken results also come from invented node sockets, unapplied transforms, mixed axes, hidden geometry and vague whole-scene requests. The [community MCP's actual server guidance](https://github.com/ahujasid/mcp-for-blender/blob/main/src/blender_mcp/server.py) recommends frequent inspection, real RNA enum/socket discovery, one object at a time and checking imported bounds. Its timeout/serialization handling is another reason to keep resumable jobs and reproducible scripts, rather than one giant opaque command.

**Colour choice:** following the [material research](material-pipeline.md), prefer existing presets and simple part separation first. Vertex colours avoid UVs for coarse colour regions but need sufficient vertices and survive export only if actually retained. A tiny palette atlas can share a material across compatible meshes, at the cost of UV assignment and mip-safe padding. Painted textures need UVs and are useful for intentional detail; they should not fake required silhouette geometry. Neither a palette nor a low material count automatically reduces draw calls without compatible batching. Compare this in the stage before making it a new standard; preserve authored normals on rounded/bevelled parts rather than forcing flat shading everywhere.

## Proposed repo skill and smallest proof

`asset-author` would contain a brief template; the owned toy/Buddy style constraints; a parameterised recipe example; pivot/bone/material contracts; a headless export-and-report helper; fixed-view capture instructions using the current stage; and an evidence template with costs, failed attempts and one owner question. Reuse [art-kit guidance](art-kit-pipeline.md), not a parallel style bible. A proposed `npm run assets:new -- <id> --kind prop` would create the source/sidecar skeleton and print the existing build/stage steps. **Neither skill nor command exists as a result of this research.**

Proposed next proof, without expanding mechanics:

| Asset | Test and expected judgement |
| --- | --- |
| **Feeder roller derived from place-kit** | Proposed ceiling 1,000 exported triangles; retain its axle pivot and measured attachment dimensions. Change length by 20% through one parameter and rebuild. Expect a clean, readily editable result; reject a pleasing mesh that breaks the existing assembly. |
| **Conductor cap on existing Buddy** | Proposed ceiling 1,500 triangles, rigid `wear_head_…` attachment and existing stiff-drum silhouette/brass trim; use paintedToy and a deliberately tinted metal clone. Check front/side/game-distance appearance and walk/carry/jump clearance. Expect more silhouette iteration than the roller; reject clipping, oversized visual weight or loss of Buddy's plain rounded head. This does not prove new skinned-character generation. |

Both retain the existing default 1 MiB per-asset download budget. Deliver recipe, regenerated GLB, measured report, matched stills/turntable and a stage URL. Ask the owner: **“Do these belong beside Buddy, and does the cap stay readable in motion?”** Stop there. Record proposed [inbox](dev-tools-agentic.md) evidence fields now without building the inbox: immutable revision, source/build hashes, camera/light/pose, reference, retained media and acknowledged feedback.

For portability, keep the Python or explicitly designated hand-authored `.blend` as the sole canonical authoring source, plus licences, textures and uncompressed exports; do not allow script and hand edits to diverge silently. The builder currently deletes temporary raw GLBs, so preserve/regenerate a deliberate interchange deliverable when needed. [Unreal glTF support](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/gltf-file-format-support-in-unreal-engine) does not imply support for every web compression/material extension. Test metre/axis conversion to Unreal's centimetre convention, pivots, bones and clips on import; TSL effects need a separate material implementation. No Unreal migration, local diffusion stack, universal retopology service, palette editor or automatic art approval is justified by this research.
