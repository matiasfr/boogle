## Ten valuable unused ideas: recommendations for Boogle

“Unused” means the **remaining application**, not that none of its ingredients exists. These are ranked recommendations, not owner decisions. The latest [station playtest](../playtests.md) validates chop → carry → physical track, but leaves late-load motivation, home errands and town life unresolved. [Rail-slice](../plans/rail-slice.md) remains **a draft awaiting approval**; its gift round has no recorded verdict here.

1. **Bring both rails and timber to the next frontier station.** Keep recipes and carry capacity fixed, retain the final hand carry, and compare against repeated home fetching. [Next-load motivation, recommendation 1](next-load-motivation.md#numbered-recommendations), supported by [Astroneer's developer rail update](https://blog.astroneer.space/p/rails-update/), supplies the unused experiment. **Loop:** [`place/index.ts`](../../src/proto/place/index.ts), [`forge.ts`](../../src/proto/place/forge.ts), rail-slice step 1; measure waiting as well as walking.
2. **Extend the forge's visible cause and effect into a solid town district.** Follow one shipment from yard to scaffold to a useful building, readable from the arrival platform, with a street that remains walkable. [Growing towns, recommendations 2, 5–6](growing-towns.md#numbered-recommendations-with-the-smallest-prototype-for-each) and [construction, 4 and 12](construction-you-can-watch.md#what-this-means-for-boogle). **Loop / chapter one:** [`place/town.ts`](../../src/proto/place/town.ts), rail-slice steps 4 and 6. The forge exists; this coherent town application does not.
3. **Give the little guys visible jobs without a worker economy.** Add short cosmetic carry, inspect and return loops at actual cargo/build sites; compare with today's hopping villagers. [Growing towns](growing-towns.md) and [creature AI](creature-ai-and-ecosystems.md) offer bounded activity and cosmetic authority; the latter's fish/gulls already exist. **Chapter one:** rail-slice's “Town, life, gifts” section and [`place/town.ts`](../../src/proto/place/town.ts). Hiring, productivity and helper progression remain undecided.
4. **Finish one whole gorge job, then measure a second placement of its kit.** Use Station 2's approach, frame, feeder, machine, track and exit; record reuse/rework cost as well as appearance. [Beautiful corner craft, 2–6 and 9–12](beautiful-corner-craft.md#numbered-recommendations-for-boogle), drawing on [Nintendo's coordinated place-making](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/Iwata-Asks/Iwata-Asks-The-Legend-of-Zelda-Skyward-Sword/Vol-5-The-Dense-Sky-and-Town/4-The-Secret-to-Extreme-Density/4-The-Secret-to-Extreme-Density-204520.html). **Beautiful corner:** [`place-kit.py`](../../assets/src/place-kit.py), [`look-kit.ts`](../../src/place/look-kit.ts), [look-pass](../plans/look-pass.md); a staged still is not this proof.
5. **Finish the sea's moving glitter and shore/pier contact at the accepted look.** Preserve Sunshine shot 2, test at 20 m/s, and measure a sustained M4 iPad run before extending the treatment. [Sunshine look, 3–6](sunshine-look.md#recommendations-ordered-by-gain-per-cost) and [WebGPU on iPad, 5](webgpu-on-ipad.md#what-this-means-for-boogle). **Beautiful corner:** [`water-render.ts`](../../src/render/water-render.ts), [`crossing/sea.ts`](../../src/proto/crossing/sea.ts), look-pass's sea backlog. The first sea pass exists; these gaps and device evidence remain.
6. **Make a missed session easy to read and rejoin.** Show the one banked project's consequence and the next useful destination; let a returning friend use shared transport without re-earning access. [Friend-group return, 3 and 8](friend-group-return.md#what-this-means-for-boogle), [returning player, 8](returning-player.md#what-this-means-for-boogle), [eight-hour pacing, 8–9](eight-hour-pacing.md#numbered-recommendations-and-the-smallest-test-for-each). **Friends / switch:** rail-slice step 7 and [first-run behavior](../game-design/first-run.md). This extends the existing silent return; it does not respawn invitees beside friends.
7. **Make shared material and delivery facts survive a join, departure and reload.** Derive views from accepted counts/manifests, return abandoned freight claims once, and reconstruct current construction without replaying old effects. [Growing towns, 8](growing-towns.md#numbered-recommendations-with-the-smallest-prototype-for-each), [roundhouse, 8](roundhouse-management.md#numbered-recommendations-and-the-smallest-prototype-for-each), [deterministic sim](deterministic-sim-at-scale.md). **Switch:** rail-slice steps 2–7 and [world-rule contracts](../../src/world/rules/README.md). Local place/net saves do not supply this production contract.
8. **Test generous personal gifts with recognizable provenance, tied to cargo.** Keep one grouped reveal and readable Buddy cosmetics; check the pending gift verdict before expanding. [Maximal rewards, 1, 5 and 8–9](maximal-rewards.md#recommendations-ranked-by-likely-gain-per-cost), with [personal rewards' in-play identity](personal-rewards.md#what-this-means-for-boogle). **Loop / chapter one:** rail-slice step 8 and [`place/index.ts`](../../src/proto/place/index.ts). The remaining opportunity is durable, distinct gifts; drop rates, personal power and a full gacha economy are not approved.
9. **Try an occasional passage of the owner's music at a crossing reveal.** Fit the arrangement to the ride, preserve tempo and player control, and listen on repeat journeys with silence between highlights. [Journey music, 1–3](journey-music.md#9-numbered-recommendations-for-boogle), informed by [Kojima Productions' composer interview](https://kojimaproductions.jp/en/strands_of_harmony_interview). **Chapter one / later eras:** [`crossing/index.ts`](../../src/proto/crossing/index.ts) and [`line.ts`](../../src/proto/crossing/line.ts). An approved audio asset is still needed; do not restore placeholder groove or cut train sounds.
10. **Test a physical train upgrade before a management game.** Compare an automatic good configuration with Swift/Hauler on a working line; the turntable and next ride must be satisfying either way. [Roundhouse management, 2–5 and 10](roundhouse-management.md#numbered-recommendations-and-the-smallest-prototype-for-each). **Chapter one / later eras:** rail-slice step 14 and [`platforms.ts`](../../src/physics/platforms.ts). The turntable toy exists; a shared upgrade workshop is an optional, unapproved branch after the loop test, not a gate.

## Evidence and use

This is a repository synthesis, not a fresh verification of the reports' external sources. Inline primary links above are retained from those reports; their original evidence labels still govern. Prefer their developer accounts and engine documentation over their secondary timing estimates. No report's source count, review vote, hypothetical budget or prototype prompt establishes an owner decision or shipped behavior.
