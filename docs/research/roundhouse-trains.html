Shared world

Contribute to a named fitting. Protect the next required connection. Each shipment is used once; fixed bills do not grow with the party.

Personal rewards

Your flags and accessories stay yours. Equipping costs nothing. Joining late gives access to the world's trains, with no catch-up bill.

05 / Pacing, without padding

Use the train after you improve it.

Thirty minutes is a proposed slice budget, not the measured current run. The existing owner verdict comes first.

0–8 minBuild + walk
8–16 minSupply arrives
16–20 minReveal + fit
20–27 minTwo useful services
27–30 minEnjoy + leave

The shed uses only 60–90 seconds of its window. If the first island lies later, seed a completed connection for an isolated test. Do not silently move the unlock to the first timber segment.

A warning from the model: buying S1 currently makes solo completion about 0.4 / 3.2 minutes longer. Prove a local benefit before adopting the price. Passengers and management are not simulated.

Proposal: at most six functional yard visits, 6–9 minutes total, inside existing activity budgets. First useful payoff within 2–5 minutes; no clock gate.
