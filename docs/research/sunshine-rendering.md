# Sunshine rendering: author the colour, keep the good water

_3 November 2026. Super Mario Sunshine's GameCube water, sky and sunny light, translated to Boogle at `0e4376b1`. Live source research and local code inspection; no implementation. Not tested on a device._

**Recommendation: use metered Khronos Neutral for the Sunshine-feeling candidate, with an authored toy sky and restrained water retuning.** A tone curve is the finishing transform, not the main explanation of Sunshine's look. The evidence points to deliberately coloured materials, vertex lighting, animated texture combiners, selective glare and stage-specific atmosphere—not a modern filmic HDR grade. Keep Boogle's FFT, shore, caustics, foam and underwater treatment. This is a comparison recommendation, not a replacement of the [frozen demo-sky/AgX decision](../proposals/boogle-2.md#look).

## What builds the look

### Water: several effects, not one shader

- **Surface and sparkle.** [Jasper St. Pierre's firsthand breakdown](https://blog.mecheye.net/2018/03/deconstructing-the-water-effect-in-super-mario-sunshine/) reconstructs Delfino Plaza's `_umi` material: two scrolling uses of a small wave texture, unequal TEV contributions, specially authored mip brightness/opacity and an alpha test that rejects a middle band. Near water clears, intermediate water becomes bright, and distant detail fades. He explicitly says **no bump mapping in this material**. The bright band mimicking environment reflection is his interpretation; it is not a recovered Fresnel calculation. Sirena's band is tinted yellow. **[R]**
- **Refraction and render-to-texture are separate.** Noclip's [`SunshineRenderer`](https://github.com/magcius/noclip.website/blob/e47a8cc8a9fe16e83adc0bf19153ff04c666ceca/src/j3d/sms_scenes.ts#L534) draws sky/main, resolves scene colour, binds `indirectdummy`, then draws `seaindirect`. This demonstrates scene-copy distortion, not reflection of off-screen objects. [`TScreenTexture`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/MarioUtil/ScreenUtil.cpp) allocates half-width/half-height RGB565; [JDrama's EFB controller](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/libs/JSystem/src/JDrama/JDREfbCtrl.cpp) performs texture copies. GX renders into its embedded framebuffer and copies results out for later sampling. **[R]**
- **Reflection is not universally planar.** [`MapMirror.cpp`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/Map/MapMirror.cpp) contains a reflected camera and 256² RGB5A3 texture setup. That establishes a mirror facility; it does **not** establish that every sea surface mirrors the town. The familiar white water sparkle is also not proof of a physically aligned sun-glitter lobe. **[R]**
- **Geometry and coast.** [`TMapObjWave`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/MoveBG/MapObjWave.cpp) draws triangle strips around Mario, with two sine-height components, scrolling coordinates, edge-faded vertex alpha and depth-conditioned amplitude/opacity. This corroborates the separate local wave mesh described in Jasper's comments. It is not FFT. Transparency lets authored seabed colour contribute; **a universal optical-depth colour law and the exact shoreline-foam material were not established**. Do not relabel highlight texture as foam or caustics. **[R/I]**

**Keep:** clear shallows, saturated water body, localized broken brilliance and readable contact. **Drop:** fixed-resolution mip bands, alpha-test aliasing, low-bit copies and limited local geometry. Boogle already has better continuous optics and waves.

### Sky: authored scene content plus separate sun effects

[`TSky`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/Map/Sky.cpp) loads `sky.bmd`, follows the camera position, starts sky animation and can substitute a material table. Noclip loads the same sky model with associated material/texture animation and draws it as a skybox. **This supports an authored model/material sky, not a procedural atmospheric-scattering simulation.** The precise split of gradient/cloud colour between textures, vertex colours and individual stage materials was not inspected; the model-loading code alone cannot answer it. **[R]**

[`TSunModel`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/Camera/sunmodel.cpp) projects **17 depth probes** and uses `GXPeekZ` to estimate sun visibility. Separate [`TLensGlow`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/Camera/lensglow.cpp) and [`TLensFlare`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/Camera/lensflare.cpp) animate geometry/materials and ease opacity, with indoor/occlusion handling. A bloom-like sun does **not** establish scene-wide HDR bloom. **[R]**

For the **Delfino Plaza / Gelato Beach / Sirena Beach** reference, distinguish clear daytime blue and white cloud masses from Sirena's warm evening mood **[I, visual characterization, not a fresh capture comparison]**. The source loads separate stage/episode scene content; noclip lists multiple Plaza progression states and beach episodes. Jasper directly documents Sirena's coordinated sunset water tint. **No continuous global day/night system, exact stage sun/ambient values, or fog distances were verified here.** Treat these as authored stage moods, not one recovered astronomical clock. **[R]**

GX's fog mixes fragments toward a specified colour over a configured range; [libogc's `GX_SetFog`](https://github.com/devkitPro/libogc/blob/master/gc/ogc/gx.h) and [noclip's fog implementation](https://github.com/magcius/noclip.website/blob/e47a8cc8a9fe16e83adc0bf19153ff04c666ceca/src/gx/gx_material.ts#L1239) document that mechanism. They do not establish how far each Sunshine stage fades. **[P/R]**

**Keep:** bold cloud silhouettes, blue-to-light horizon separation, coordinated sunset colours and selective sun glare. **Drop:** any need for a literal sky mesh, CPU depth readback or hardware fog precision. A single coherent baked cube fits Boogle.

### Grade and light: colour construction before display conversion

**TEV is material shading, not a film LUT.** It combines textures, rasterized vertex lighting and constant/register colours through staged interpolation, add/subtract, bias, scale and clamp; indirect texturing modifies subsequent lookup coordinates. [libogc's TEV API](https://github.com/devkitPro/libogc/blob/master/gc/ogc/gx.h) and [YAGCD's BP/TEV register reference](https://www.gc-forever.com/yagcd/chap5.html) document these operations. Bright combining and blending can create an overbright _impression_ in a bounded framebuffer without HDR exposure or a filmic shoulder. **[P/R]**

[`LightUtil`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/MarioUtil/LightUtil.cpp) reads ambient/light groups and sets GX diffuse/specular lights; noclip reads separate object/player ambient colours. Vertex colours can carry authored shading into TEV. [`ShadowUtil`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/MarioUtil/ShadowUtil.cpp) contains bound-part/circle requests, shadow-volume drawing and destination-alpha compositing: **“all shadows baked” is false**; this is not evidence of modern cascaded shadow maps either. The static-art/dynamic-shadow balance remains unaudited. **[R]**

[`TShimmer`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/Map/Shimmer.cpp) loads an indirect-textured model and texture-matrix animation, substitutes the screen texture, and positions it relative to the camera. It has FLUDD/cap/environment conditions. This supports screen-copy heat distortion; exact texture field, strength and complete pass ordering remain unknown. [`TAfterEffect`](https://github.com/doldecomp/sms/blob/fbde18b23c2e92351cdba0899710dd471e48bb10/src/MarioUtil/ScreenUtil.cpp) also composites a screen texture with colour/alpha and changing coordinates. Neither proves a global colour-grade LUT or HDR bloom. **[R]**

[Dolphin's Hybrid XFB explanation](https://dolphin-emu.org/blog/2017/11/19/hybridxfb/) distinguishes EFB effect copies from final YUV XFB scanout. Copy filtering, video mode, analog connection and CRT response affect perceived softness/colour; an emulator screenshot is not a calibrated CRT match. Do not treat the water article's single resolution as universal across regions/modes. Reproduce the sunny colour relationships, not interlace, blur or quantization. **[P/I]**

The readable hot/cool separation—pale sunlit solids, coloured shade, blue water, white highlights—is principally an **art-direction inference [I]**. The [2002 developer interviews](https://shmuplations.com/mariosunshine/) support childhood water-play and a tropical setting **[P, read in translation]**, not an exact shader recipe.

## Boogle recommendations, in order

Current-code statements below are **[L]**; proposed settings are **[I]**.

1. **Choose metered Neutral as the lead, and judge a whole look.** [Khronos Neutral](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/ToneMapping/blob/main/PBR_Neutral/README.md) preserves colour relationships while compressing/desaturating bright highlights; it is not a recovered Sunshine curve. Apply TSL `toneMapping(NeutralToneMapping, 1, ...)` to exposed linear HDR, then encode sRGB once. [`post.ts`](../../src/render/water/post.ts) currently applies [`tone.ts`](../../src/render/tone.ts)'s custom water AgX, including power **1.12**, saturation **1.3**. Its Post contrast slider is a subsequent power, not AgX Punchy. Keep the existing meter: **two tiny draws**, no new fullscreen pass for a curve swap. Keep vivid body colours below the shoulder rather than saturating clipped whites.

2. **Retune the existing water; do not port `_umi`.** [`surface.ts`](../../src/render/water/surface.ts) already combines optical thickness/absorption, sky reflection, GGX, slope-variance filtering, footprint-based glitter and shore foam. Preserve all of it and caustic receivers. First compare RAW with **water saturation 1.1, reflection gain 0.9/cap 0.8, foam brightness 0.8**, retaining glitter **0.6** and FFT energy **0.25**. These are Boogle starting hypotheses, not Sunshine measurements. Adjust scatter/absorption by eye against our sand; do not layer a second depth tint over `exp(-absorption * thickness)`. In TSL, keep colour emphasis on `body` before `mix(body, reflection, Fresnel) + specular`, so foam/glints retain white headroom. **Cost: uniform changes, zero added passes/samples. Panel already reaches** these controls plus roughness, refraction, sun gain, caustics and foam; `water.glitter` changes breakup, not total sun energy. Keep existing filtering rather than adding hard sparkle thresholds.

3. **Have `sunshine-sky` author one sky for the whole picture.** [`frozen-sky.ts`](../../src/render/water/frozen-sky.ts) already bakes once at `tod=0.24`; [`sky.ts`](../../src/render/water/sky.ts) supplies background, water reflection, environment and fog. Replace the bake's colour function with an original TSL horizon→zenith `mix` and a few broad, smoothly masked cloud shapes. Derive/author sun direction, warm key, cool open fill and cloud transmittance together; preserve cube alpha's sun-visibility meaning and keep the sharp sun out of the reflection cube to avoid doubling the separate GGX sun. Fog should sample the same horizon, retaining distant town silhouettes. **Cost: six bake draws/mips, no new runtime scene pass.** The current 1024² RGBA16F cube is approximately **64 MiB with mips**; a 256² trial is **4 MiB**, excluding PMREM/other targets, and needs corresponding LOD adjustment. **Panel already reaches** time, sun angles/intensity, cloud coverage and turbidity; authored sky colours/cloud shapes, separate fill and fog range require code. A painted sky is another source for the same cube, not a new rendering architecture.

4. **Make light readable before adding screen effects.** Keep Boogle's existing shadows and warm-key/cool-fill relationship; use original broad vertex/material colour accents on solids, not a global saturation rescue. The existing bake derives fill from the cube, so sky edits affect characters too. Start with bloom off and the existing analytic sun halo. If glare is still missing, compare a small occluded sun-local halo before global bloom; use GPU depth visibility, not CPU EFB peeks. **Panel bloom exists**, but [`bloom.ts`](../../src/render/water/bloom.ts) allocates **12 target draws**: one full-resolution HDR materialization, six downsamples, five upsamples. It is not one cheap blur. Heat shimmer is last: a depth-masked subpixel UV offset in the existing output chain, excluding nearby characters, would require scene/depth sampling and has no current panel control. No millisecond saving or motion quality is claimed.

5. **Give the three-look comparison a controlled question.** The running `sunshine-sky` job on `codex/sunshine-sky` owns the opt-in toy sky and water trial; its inspected launch brief requests current / sky-only / sky+water under AgX and Neutral. `agx-punchy` separately owns its tuning selector. Do not duplicate either. For the coming **metered Neutral / current water AgX / AgX Punchy** board, first hold sky, water, camera and lighting identical; then show the toy-sky/water ablation. Reuse [`tone-comparison.md`](tone-comparison.md)'s settled meter and fixed middle-grey calibration (**Neutral 1; current AgX 0.771116**); calibrate Punchy independently. Show shore, open sea, purple Buddy, foliage and warm town, plus one authored Sirena-like dusk sanity check. [`tone-compare.ts`](../../src/features/tone-compare.ts) currently disables those flags on `water=new`: verify actual output selection after the jobs integrate. Record exposure and then freeze it for an additional curve-only comparison. One owner question: **Which feels like a bright seaside toy world while the water still feels rich?** Motion evidence must check glitter, shore seams and sun occlusion. Stop at that comparison; this research approves no rollout.

These proposals serve **Show, don't tell** and **Small and readable**. [`water-tuning.ts`](../../src/debug/water-tuning.ts) and [`live.ts`](../../src/render/water/live.ts) establish the panel inventory above at this revision; bindings require `water=new&tune=1`.

**Engine fit [P/I]:** [Unity's environmental ambient light](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/lighting-ambient-light.html) and [Godot's Sky material/radiance cube](https://github.com/godotengine/godot-docs/blob/master/classes/class_sky.rst) support treating the sky as lighting input as well as backdrop. Boogle already does this; keep its frozen bake rather than adding runtime sky updates. The [Unreal Sky Light documentation](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/sky-lights-in-unreal-engine) returned 403, so its current implementation was not verified.

## Does `sunshine-look.md` hold up?

**Its historical evidence and colour intent largely hold; its implementation recipe is superseded.** It correctly identifies scrolling/mip/alpha tricks, distinguishes reflection inference, and avoids claiming verified HDR bloom. This pass adds concrete sky, screen-copy, local-wave and alpha-shadow code evidence. Its old analytic sea, proposed normal maps, optional Beer–Lambert, no-FFT starting point and fixed exposure **1** are not instructions for today's water. Its Neutral choice remains a good candidate, not binding authority over the later owner call. Its device budgets are estimates and its iPad-first acceptance prescription predates the [M1 Max/mobile standing call](../agents/owner-calls.md#owners-standing-calls). Retain it as dated research; use this report for the present comparison.
