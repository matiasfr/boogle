## Rules

- **What goes in:** an effort the owner has committed to in his own words ("we want this", an approved plan, a decision that orders future work). One row per effort, one line per row.
- **What stays out:** ideas he has not committed to stay in [ideas.md](ideas.md); bugs, playtest notes and proof debts go to the tracker ([round-4-notes](plans/round-4-notes.md)) and the state file's Leftovers; calls made without him stay in [agent-calls](agent-calls.md).
- **When a row is added:** the orchestrator adds it the same turn the owner commits to something, with his words and the date, and ships it with the docs.
- **When a row leaves:** only when the effort has shipped (delete the row; git keeps it), or when the owner drops it (move one line under [Dropped](#dropped) with his words and the date). A row never leaves for ranking low or going quiet.
- **Status** is one of: `scoped` (wanted, no plan yet), `planned` (a plan exists, nothing built), `building`, `blocked: <on what>`, `parked by owner`.
- **Rank** is P1, P2 or P3 (owner call "Rank every note"). Within a rank, rows are in the order to pick them.
- Keep the table below as the file's only table, with these column headers unchanged: a build script reads it.
