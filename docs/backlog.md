Decisions still live in the [decision log](decisions.md#decided), standing preferences in [owner calls](agents/owner-calls.md), tasks in each plan and live job status in the [orchestrator state](agents/orchestrator-state.md); this file only says which efforts are owed and where each one stands.

## Rules

- **What goes in:** an effort the owner has committed to in his own words ("we want this", an approved plan, a decision that orders future work). One row per effort, one line per row.
- **What stays out:** ideas he has not committed to stay in [ideas.md](ideas.md); bugs, playtest notes and proof debts go to the tracker ([round-4-notes](plans/round-4-notes.md)) and the [orchestrator's leftovers](agents/orchestrator-leftovers.md); calls made without him stay in [agent-calls](agent-calls.md).
- **When a row is added:** the orchestrator adds it the same turn the owner commits to something, with his words and the date, and ships it with the docs.
- **When a row leaves:** only when the effort has shipped (delete the row; git keeps it), or when the owner drops it (move one line under [Dropped](#dropped) with his words and the date). A row never leaves for ranking low or going quiet.
- **Status** is one of: `scoped` (wanted, no plan yet), `planned` (a plan exists, nothing built), `building`, `blocked: <on what>`, `parked by owner`.
- **Rank** is P1, P2 or P3 (owner call "Rank every note"). Within a rank, rows are in the order to pick them.
- **P1 is the focus, at most five rows** ([16 November](decisions-process.md#d-2026-11-16-focus-choice-and-payoff)): the next free agent goes to a P1 row. A P2 or P3 row gets a job only when no P1 row can take another without sharing files. A row with shipped steps and no job running keeps the status `building`; its rank says whether it is being worked on now.
- Keep the table below as the file's only table, with these column headers unchanged: a build script reads it.
