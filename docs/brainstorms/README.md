# Brainstorms

Briefs from the [brainstorm skill](../../.claude/skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md): an owner's seeded idea worked by three
panelists (play designer, systems architect, creative director) and synthesized into one brief with ranked suggestions
and open questions. A brief with `Status: proposed` binds no one; once the owner answers its questions it becomes
`approved` and is the build spec for its feature, like a plan. Parked one-liners stay in [ideas.md](../ideas.md).
- [ring-and-economy.md](ring-and-economy.md): a co-op incremental around a ring with Home as the hub; hands first, machines later per tier, a shared wallet, a paused world with a soft away credit, an end-game tier, with an editable spreadsheet model (proposed, partly answered).
- [jump-upgrades.md](jump-upgrades.md): earn the extra jump together and let it lower a shared ramp at a remembered balcony (proposed).
- [gate-puzzles.md](gate-puzzles.md): the machinery remembers your help; Split-rock splits a seam, Ring braces an aperture (proposed).
- [telescope.md](telescope.md): same framing, changed railway; later telescopes add viewpoints, not upgrades (proposed).
- [coop-abilities.md](coop-abilities.md): a new ability reaches everyone at once with a flourish wherever they are, a late friend gets their own moment, and Home keeps the trophy; storyboard in [coop-abilities.html](../world/coop-abilities.html) (proposed).
- [great-beam-clarity.md](great-beam-clarity.md): your first axe fells the great tree once Station 3 opens, logs and beam in either order; the frame shows two empty saddles, the tree wears the same rope, and you drag the trunk on it while a friend lifts the end; the proper axe moves to the forge; storyboard in [great-beam-clarity.html](../world/great-beam-clarity.html) (proposed).
- [upgrade-choices.md](upgrade-choices.md): upgrades wait as parts beside the machine they change, and anyone fits one for the room with one press; the boiler fit is the first, the cart keeps 24, and a works board on the unified Home station becomes the diegetic tree later; 2D page in [upgrade-tree.html](../world/upgrade-tree.html) (proposed, designer-reviewed).
- [loop-graph.md](loop-graph.md): the rail loop as one typed graph, a headless run of it through the real rules, and one trace table comparing prediction, bot and human sessions; retires the headless ball and player runs (approved, building).
- [starting-area-folk.md](starting-area-folk.md): five little guys you can talk to at Home, each with one fixation, prop, gesture and voice, the rest ambient; lines change on progress, time away and co-op; one typed lines table checked by `npm run check` and previewed with a look eval (proposed).
- [ring-island.md](ring-island.md): the Meet, where the island folk's orange half-crossing lowers its counterweighted leaf onto your bents and the room strikes the gold rivet; a goofy daylight religion about the perfect floating shard (someone must always look up at it); steel comes home as the longer boiler (decided by the orchestrator, building).
- [engine-garage.md](engine-garage.md): a racing-game garage for the room's train at the Roundhouse turntable: a works copy of the engine, parts previewed on the model, three felt readouts, explicit cargo cars with loaders at the sources, one shared Fit; mock in [engine-garage-mock.html](../world/engine-garage-mock.html) (proposed).
- [pause-wardrobe.md](pause-wardrobe.md): Pause opens on You, where Buddy turns to the camera and you cycle hat, accessory, outfit, pet and colour, applied at once and seen by friends; today's menu moves behind Options; finding stays in the world (proposed).
- [station-deposit.md](station-deposit.md): spares go in a yard at each halt (logs, stone, rails), taken back only where they lie and later moved on by trains towards whatever their line still owes; never a bank; shouldering big cargo with a full back sets the load down instead of refusing; the recovery rack becomes the Village yard (proposed).
- [junction-stations.md](junction-stations.md): halts stay generic, but the seven places where lines meet are designed stations, each with a plan for its place, a walk-in building with something in it, its own works and one joke; engine rotundas at Home and Port Quay only; a shared kit; Ring Island's halt designed first (proposed).
- [express-train.md](express-train.md): call the next service train at a junction station's hall map, it waits 20 s for friends with a bell and runs non-stop to your stop wearing a headboard; interchanges, bought per junction and laid by the track gang, let it run through onto another line; Home and Bell Rock never get one (proposed).
- [passenger-trade.md](passenger-trade.md): little guys ride between towns and every finished trip pays Boogles (the owner's name for the currency, decided) into the room's one wallet, a strongbox at Home's ticket window; Boogles buy seats, trains, station comforts and looks, never materials or the main route; verdict build smaller: fares in the economy model and the 2D prototype first, then a flagged Village to Far Head till on v26 with one car to buy (proposed).
- [track-layers-double.md](track-layers-double.md): the track layer gets a second hand where the second track is, a folding wing with its own trolley, stack and a truck on the up rails, so both pairs go down in one beat; look only, no rule change (proposed).
