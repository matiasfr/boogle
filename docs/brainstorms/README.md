# Brainstorms

Briefs from the [brainstorm skill](../../.claude/skills/brainstorm/SKILL.md): an owner's seeded idea worked by three
panelists (play designer, systems architect, creative director) and synthesized into one brief with ranked suggestions
and open questions. A brief with `Status: proposed` binds no one; once the owner answers its questions it becomes
`approved` and is the build spec for its feature, like a plan. Parked one-liners stay in [ideas.md](../ideas.md).
- [ring-and-economy.md](ring-and-economy.md): a co-op incremental around a ring with Home as the hub; hands first, machines later per tier, world stock in a visible Home Yard, a branch choice after Ring Island, with an editable spreadsheet model (proposed).
- [jump-upgrades.md](jump-upgrades.md): earn the extra jump together and let it lower a shared ramp at a remembered balcony (proposed).
- [gate-puzzles.md](gate-puzzles.md): the machinery remembers your help; Split-rock splits a seam, Ring braces an aperture (proposed).
- [telescope.md](telescope.md): same framing, changed railway; later telescopes add viewpoints, not upgrades (proposed).
