# Lane C Part 7: Canopy Town's first room-picked hangout (with cross-town signal answers)

## Pitch

Home's Round is full at its four priced plots (Parts 1–4), Ring Island has its first hangout (Part 5, #2691, world v85), and High Gorge now has its first hangout (Part 6, #2710, world v87). The next hub town on the decided route — **Canopy Town** (Area 6, reached by `bell-canopy`, `RAIL_LEGS[3]`, already dressed in `greybox-look` step 3, #2580) — still sits with an empty 30 × 30 m flat `stand-in town` reserve (`n: -72..-42, e: -50..-20`, `ISLE = 12 m`) and nothing to play with between works. Part 7 brings Lane C to **Canopy Town (`town = 3, slot = 48`)**, staking its first room-picked hangout on the near corner of that reserve (~12 m / ~2.1 s from the bottom of `platform-home-ramp`, ~28 m / ~5 s from the platform middle) the moment leg 3 reaches Canopy (`after: 'canopy'`, i.e., `legDone(w, 3)`), priced fresh at base 300 (**150 Boogles** at the room's 50% tune) from the one shared strongbox, with `+1 rider / minute` on line 2's far queue (`farTowns: [1, 2, 3]`). Twin blueprint boards offer a Canopy-fitted Hangout-vs.-Hangout pick built around the giant mother trees, fibre, and glider cloth: **`[Placeholder: Canopy Bounce Net]`** (walk-in timber cabin with a taut woven-fibre bounce net stretched on the porch that launches players ~11 m up into a glide through three hanging cloth bunting hoops over the forest clearing) or **`[Placeholder: Treetop Wind Chime]`** (walk-in timber pavilion with a tall bamboo-and-brass chime frame and striker cord that sends a pentatonic chime cascade and glowing leaf-mote rings drifting up beside the nearest mother trunk, harmonizing if a friend honks within 3 s). At the same time, riding the room-wide cosmetic broadcast `hosted-room.ts` already sends, **every built signal hangout across towns** (Ring Island's Signal Bellhouse, High Gorge's Echo Horn House, and Canopy Town's Treetop Wind Chime) **answers another town's call** with a soft sympathetic ring/chime and a high sky-mote pulse visible to friends riding trains between them — zero extra server rules, zero extra world bumps.

**Principles served:** Core loop ("choice and hanging out live in the towns, where every building adds something to do"), **2. Physics toys are optional fun**, **3. Side activities are never required**, **6. Communal world, personal things**, **7. Telegraph everything**, **8. Enjoyable alone and together**, **10. Slapstick over precision**, and **11. Show, don't tell**. **Principles bent:** None.

## Gut question

When a friend steps off the train at Canopy Town between works (or rides past Ring Island and High Gorge while a friend is hanging out there), do they immediately spot the room's chosen forest hangout off the homeward ramp, bounce into a hoop glide or ring the wind chimes, and feel the other towns answer across the water?

## Best suggestions (ranked)

1. **`[Systems]` Extend `TRADE.plots` to `town = 3` (Canopy Town) with `after: 'canopy'` (`legDone(w, 3)`) and `farTowns: [1, 2, 3]`.** Part 6 already generalized `TRADE_TOWNS = TRADE.plots.length`, `TradeRoute.farTowns`, and `checkTrade`'s `ts.slice(1)` outer-town check, so Canopy Offer 1 is a pure data row (`slot = 48`, `base: 300`, `riders: 1`, `choices: [10, 11]`) plus one `after === 'canopy'` branch in `plotsStakedOf` / `checkTrade` and one pure integer `CANOPY_HANGOUT` / `CANOPY_HANGOUT_BOARD` anchor in `src/sim/railway-route.ts`.
2. **`[Play]` + `[Creative]` Pair `[Placeholder: Canopy Bounce Net]` vs. `[Placeholder: Treetop Wind Chime]` as Canopy Town Offer 1.** Both grow straight out of Canopy's fibre, glider-cloth, and giant-tree identity (seeded in [lane-c-part6](lane-c-part6.md#parked) and [campaign-outline](../plans/campaign-outline.md#3-the-finite-area-list)) and need no imported Home ball or frisbee. **Canopy Bounce Net** (`kind: 10`) gives kinetic solo/co-op aerial play: step onto the taut woven-fibre porch net to bounce ~11 m into the air (matching Seed Shed's `15 m/s` mushroom bounce), open the glider (`Jump`), and swoop through three hanging cloth bunting hoops over the forest clearing — each hoop fluttering with a soft wooden *clack-chime* as you pass through — back to the platform or meadow. **Treetop Wind Chime** (`kind: 11`) gives musical, atmospheric co-op comedy: yank the striker cord (or jump-bonk the clapper) to ripple a 4-note pentatonic bamboo-and-brass chime up the nearest 120 m mother trunk with rising leaf-mote rings, answering with a warm harmony chord if a friend honks within 3 s.
3. **`[Creative]` + `[Systems]` Fold in cross-town "sympathetic signal answers" over `hosted-room.ts`'s existing room-wide broadcast.** Extend `hosted-room.ts`'s 2-entry signal relay (`bell` on slot 16, `echo` on slot 32) to a 3-entry table adding `chime` on slot 48 (same 3.2 m proximity, pose freshness, `world.hasSignalHouse(slot)`, and 1.5 s per-peer cooldown). On the client, when a room has built a signal hangout in another town, receiving a remote `bell`, `echo`, or `chime` plays a soft, delayed (0.8 s) sympathetic answer at the local resident signal hangout (a gentle bell tap, low canyon hum, or soft wind-chime shimmer + high sky-mote ring), and friends riding any train between hubs hear a soft distant answer — making built hangouts feel like one connected archipelago with zero new server rules or saved state.
4. **`[Systems]` Site Canopy Offer 1 at the near corner of `leg3.ts`'s existing `stand-in town` reserve (`n: -72..-42, e: -50..-20`, `ISLE = 12 m`) and reuse `createHangoutBuilding` with `flat: true`.** Because `canopy-isle` is dead flat at `y = 12 m` and `stand-in town` is already clear of all 17 trunks and platform reserves, placing the `5.4 × 4.4 m` walk-in shell at `n ≈ -49, e ≈ -26` (boards at `n ≈ -45, e ≈ -24`, facing north-east toward `platform-home-ramp`) puts the twin boards ~12 m (~2.1 s) from the bottom of the homeward ramp and leaves the rest of the 30 × 30 m pad open for future freight-staked Plots 2 and 3.
5. **`[Creative]` Frame the Canopy Bounce Net hoops and Treetop Wind Chime leaf-motes against `mother-2` (`-1050, -300`, 120 m tall).** `mother-2` stands ~33 m off the homeward side right beside the town reserve; arcing the three bunting hoops toward the clearing beside its giant trunk and letting the chime's leaf-motes rise beside it gives Canopy Town an unmistakable silhouette from both the bay platform and the Wall overbridge.

### Real tensions resolved

- **Canopy Bounce Net launch height vs. Route Contract Rule 2 (`route-contract.md`):** `[Systems]` flagged that a bounce net on Canopy Isle must never skip a work edge or let a player glide to Lantern Notch early. **Resolved:** Canopy Offer 1 only stakes *after* `legDone(w, 3)` (`bell-canopy` complete), when the room already holds double jump, hook, and glider and the `cloth` pickup on the `13.9 m` homeward deck is an open walk with no lock; the Notch barrier (`RAIL_GATES[4]`) is a server-checked gate (`cloth` + `wall` complete). Capping the bounce at `15 m/s` (~11 m apex, `y ≈ 23 m`, matching Seed Shed's mushrooms) gives a fun ~50–60 m clearing glide through the three hoops (`y = 16–20 m`) while staying 15× short of Lantern Notch (~900 m away) and 95 m below the mother-tree tops.
- **Cross-town signal answers vs. audio noise & solo play:** `[Play]` worried distant town answers could feel spammy or pointless solo; `[Creative]` argued that a quiet 0.8 s sympathetic echo at another town's built hangout sells the "friends in a shared archipelago" fantasy without adding any HUD. **Resolved:** Cap sympathetic answers to one soft, low-volume distant chime/ring per 4 s per town (separate from local pulls), never interrupting local dialogue or rides, and keep both Canopy buildings 100% fun solo (bounce-and-glide hoops; pentatonic chime cascade).
- **Canopy Town (`town = 3`) vs. Port Quay (`town = 4`) order:** `[Systems]` noted that under `order-choice` (`#2523`) a room can build the cave leg to Port Quay before Canopy. **Resolved:** Follow the decided fixed campaign order (`High Gorge → Bell Rock → Canopy Town → Port Quay`, [d-2026-11-12-campaign-outline-answered](../decisions-core-loop.md#d-2026-11-12-campaign-outline-answered)), which puts Canopy Town (`Area 6`) next and leaves Port Quay (`Area 7`) free for its active Roundhouse work and a later Part 8 (where `?proto=soapbox` fits Port Quay's long quay street).
