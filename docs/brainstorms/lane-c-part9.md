# Lane C Part 9: Port Quay's first room-picked hangout (`town = 4, slot = 64`)

## Pitch

Home's Round, Ring Island, High Gorge, and Canopy Town all have room-picked hangouts that react to players and passing trains (Parts 1–8). **Port Quay** (`Area 7`, the fifth and final plot hub town on the route in [campaign-outline](../plans/campaign-outline.md#towns-at-the-hubs), reached by the `cave` line, `RAIL_LEGS[4]`) already has its five-bay Roundhouse (#2563–#2598), cross-laid timber freight platforms (#2753), and a flat 30 × 30 m `town` reserve (`a: 33..63, b: -67..-37, y = 3.02 m` in `src/content/levels/leg4.ts:1013`), but nothing to play with between works.

**Part 9 brings Lane C to Port Quay (`TRADE_TOWNS = 5, town = 4, slot = 64`) in one small, Part 7-sized build with exactly one `WORLD_NS` bump.** Staked the moment the cave line reaches Port Quay (`after: 'quay'` via `legDone(w, 4)`, working in either branch order) and priced fresh at base 300 (**150 Boogles** at the room's 50% tune) from the one shared strongbox, twin blueprint boards stand at the near corner of the `town` reserve (**13.9 m / ~2.5 s** off the foot of the Roundhouse `yard-ramp`). Both finished buildings are walk-in timber wharf sheds (`5.4 × 4.4 m`, `flat: true`) that add **`+1 rider / minute`** to line 2's far queue (`farTowns: [1, 2, 3, 4]`, keeping line 2's riders' far end at High Gorge, fare 34) with no new reward kind:

1. **`[Placeholder: Harbour Foghorn House]` (`PLOT_KIND.foghorn = 12`):** A walk-in wharf shed topped with a chunky brass-and-iron foghorn trumpet, ridge harbour lantern, and doorway pull-chain. Pulling the chain (`{ t: 'foghorn', id: 64 }`, validated in `hosted-room.ts`'s existing signal relay table) blasts a deep two-tone maritime foghorn with a curling mist ring over the harbour apron, harmonizes with a low tug-horn reply if a friend honks within 3 s, and **completes the five-town archipelago signal choir** by joining both Part 7's cross-town sympathetic signal answers (`signal-answers.ts`) and Part 8's passing-train honk greetings (`greeting.ts`).
2. **`[Placeholder: Quay Buoy-Alley]` (`PLOT_KIND.skittles = 13`):** A walk-in wharf shed with a 14 m timber-curbed buoy-skittle lane laid flat inside the `town` reserve, a chunky kickable/rollable wooden buoy-ball on a chalk tee, 6 striped timber harbour-buoy pins that scatter and clatter when hit by the buoy-ball or a sprinting Buddy (or a remote friend), a cheerful harbour-bell strike flourish when all 6 fall (zero HUD score), and a porch reset lever that snaps all 6 pins back upright.

**The soapbox stays parked:** `[Placeholder: Soapbox Garage]` does **not** earn one of Part 9's two slots. The owner retired the soapbox mini-game ([d-2026-11-02-the-soapbox-is-removed](../decisions-process.md#d-2026-11-02-the-soapbox-is-removed)), and `src/proto/soapbox/` is no longer in `src/` (only historical comments and a `localStorage` prefix remain). Rebuilding an Owner-synced physics vehicle, seat/steering mode, suspension visuals, and harbour water-recovery from scratch would turn a small Part 7-sized hangout pass into a multi-week vehicle project. It stays parked for a dedicated vehicle pass once Port Quay's wider town layout is designed.

**Principles served:** Core loop ("choice and hanging out live in the towns, where every building adds something to do"), **2. Physics toys are optional fun**, **3. Side activities are never required**, **6. Communal world, personal things**, **7. Telegraph everything**, **8. Enjoyable alone and together**, **10. Slapstick over precision**, and **11. Show, don't tell**. **Principles bent:** None.

### Findings

1. **Port-from-Place / reuse fit check (owner call "Port from Place only what still fits"):**
   - **Retired `?proto=soapbox`:** Does **not** earn its place in Part 9 — its prototype code is already deleted from `src/` and rebuilding a drivable vehicle from scratch would exceed a small Part 7-sized build. Stays parked.
   - **Signal relay table (`hosted-room.ts`), `createSignalAnswers`, `townGreeting`, and `createHangoutBuilding`:** All live production systems; extending them to `town = 4, slot = 64` earns its place at minimal code cost.
2. **Level design & Route Contract check (`level-design.md`, `route-contract.md`):**
   - *Flow & walk-seconds (`5.6 m/s`):* From Port Quay's Platform 1 (`a: -20..20, b: -7.8..-3.8, y = 3.90 m`) across the Roundhouse yard to `yard-ramp` (`a = 22..25, b = -40..-34`) is ~29.7 m (~5.3 s); from the foot of `yard-ramp` `(25, -37)` to `QUAY_BOARD` `(38, -42)` is **13.9 m (~2.5 s)** (~43.6 m / ~7.8 s total from Platform 1's east end, ~38.1 m / ~6.8 s from the Roundhouse table `(0, -39)`). Fits the 10–20 s activity-cadence guide.
   - *Sightlines & look-plan check:* Unseen in stills until the build pass (flagged as a build proof in Done means 1). Standing at `yard-ramp`, the twin boards and walk-in wharf shed frame cleanly against the flat `3.02 m` apron without encroaching on Card 5/21's dressed freight platforms (`b = ±5.8`) or the Roundhouse yard (`a ≤ 22`).
   - *Route contract (Rule 1: next goal findable; Rule 2: movement may skip distance, never the work; Rule 3: nobody stranded):* Offer 1 opens only after `legDone(w, 4)` (`cave` complete); neither the foghorn nor the flat 14 m buoy-alley grants any vertical boost, movement unlock, or work-edge skip; the White Wall's first frame/rack on the seaward/outbound side stays untouched; nobody can be stranded on the flat `3.02 m` apron.
   - *Unproved clearance (build prerequisite):* Exact placement of the `5.4 × 4.4 m` shell, `±1.2 m` twin boards, and 14 m buoy-alley inside `quay.reserve('town', 33, 63, -67, -37, 3.02, 12)` against `quay-edges.ts`, `shores.quayBed`, and `port-isle`'s beach edges must be proved by `dev/level/route-leg4.ts` / `npm run level-check` and `npm run look` stills in the build pass.
3. **First meeting, solo vs. co-op, late arrival:**
   - *First meeting:* Walking past the Roundhouse down `yard-ramp` onto the quay apron, a player immediately sees the twin boards with a chunky brass foghorn topper vs. a striped harbour buoy-pin topper and the live-swapping gold ghost — readable in one glance with zero tutorial text (DESIGN 11).
   - *Solo:* Both hangouts work alone (pull the foghorn chain and hear other built towns answer across the water; sprint-kick the buoy-ball into 6 clattering pins and snap them back up with the porch lever).
   - *Co-op & late joiner:* One shared strongbox pays 150 Boogles; server-atomic `rail.buy` settles races; late joiners read `Plot.slot = 64` and see the finished wharf hangout immediately; friends can honk into the 3 s foghorn harmony window, ride past on the cave/Wall train to trigger a welcome foghorn, or bowl and body-check pins together on the buoy-alley.
4. **Research alignment:** Follows [minigames-and-physics-minigames](../research/minigames-and-physics-minigames.md) recs 1, 2, 4, 6, 8 (toy first, one-glance read, funny scatter, fun to spectate from the yard ramp, place-with-a-prop not a mode, no scoreboard) and [comedy-in-games](../research/comedy-in-games.md) recs 1, 2, 3 (clean controls + messy pin scatter, cheap instant reset). No research recommendation contradicted.
