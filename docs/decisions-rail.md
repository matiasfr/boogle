- <a id="d-2026-11-12-greybox-waits-and-build-2-answered"></a>**2026-11-12, a looser wait limit for the greybox; leg 1's kit by itself; the last sea platform a request stop; the plain install for now** (owner, 12 November UTC, on the orchestrator's questions page). (1) Asked "Should the platform wait limit be a rate instead of a hard maximum?", he picked "A looser rate for the greybox": "Ship build 2 and leg 1 now with waits up to about 2 minutes 15, and tighten when train spacing lands. The grey route arrives sooner and feels slow at the platforms." **Orchestrator's reading, not his words:** until train spacing lands, the wait gates for [Ring Island build 2](plans/ring-island-build2.md) and [greybox leg 1](plans/greybox-route.md) are a worst lever wait of 1,350 ticks on a settled line and 1,600 while a line is growing (measured: build 2 worst 1,100; leg 1 settled worst 1,343, growing worst 1,408), with the mean and the share over 1,050 printed and not gated; the [train-spacing](plans/train-spacing.md#c-the-limit-as-a-rate) table (mean at most 525, at most 1.0% over 1,050, none over 1,158 settled) is the target the gates tighten to when spacing lands. This supersedes the designer's 12 November wait-bound call ("1,050 ticks plus 108 for each resource source") for these two builds only ([archive](decisions-archive.md#archive-rail-construction-levels-and-travel)). (2) After the rivet, leg 1's first kit comes by itself, the steel kit first (recommended). (3) Once the line reaches High Gorge the last building platform at sea stays, as a request stop (recommended). (4) The longer boiler's install stays a plain hold at Home's table until the Home flip: "yes, plain for now", with his note "but prioritize the garage moment"; the garage's install moment is the next Ring Island item after greybox leg 1 step 3. **Changed:** (1) replaces in part the 10 November "line 2 runs three trains for now" worst wait of about 105 s, for these two builds.
