## Saves and rooms

- <a id="a-recent-reload-returns-you-where-you-were"></a>**A recent reload puts you back where you were** (owner, 14 November 2026, after playing the whole built route to High Gorge: "I really think if I'm just refreshing my browser in a recent enough time, I should go back to where I was rather than the town. Because for now that the distances are pretty vast, we'll want to remember where the player was, I think."). Orchestrator's reading: a player who reloads or rejoins soon after leaving returns to where they stood (or the nearest safe spot to it), not to Home; Home is where you arrive when you have been away a while or have no remembered place. It extends [2 November's "a return goes straight in"](../decisions-controls.md#d-2026-11-02-a-return-goes-straight-in-and) ("spawn in where they were"), which the live game only honours on Home island's open ground: a deck, a platform or anywhere past Ring Island is not remembered today. How long "recent" is and what a long absence does are open with him. Plan: [resume-where-you-were](../plans/resume-where-you-were.md).

_Archived 16 November 2026: the rule lives in [the current entry](../decisions-controls.md#d-2026-11-14-a-recent-reload-returns-you-where)_
