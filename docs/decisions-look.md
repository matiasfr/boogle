- <a id="d-2026-11-10-the-sea-is-rebuilt-small-water"></a>**2026-11-10, the sea is rebuilt small: Water B's depth plane, Ultra Water deleted** (owner, 9 November: "Let's go with water B and fully clean up. also fix the geometry of the shallow bed so it matches the terrain more realistically than the sharp edges/squares it has today"; 10 November: "since underwater doesn't matter, is that taken account for the water optimizations?", "yeah no essentially we should rebuild the water from scratch, we don't need almost anything from ultra water and don't want to carry the perf baggage", "I do like that the water has the lighter blue and foam near shore, so make sure something like that stays, it should still be cheap", and "the water should look somewhere in the range of Mario odyssey and Mario sunshine"). The sea is one blended draw with one opaque-depth copy and no water compute; its CPU work per frame is the swell uniforms and the water field. Kept: the cyan shallows grading to deep blue and the white foam lip (one coast-texture read), the sand showing through the first metres, the cell sparkle, splash ripples and droplets, a scrolling caustic tile on the seabed seen from above. Underwater is one flat fog. Removed: Ultra Water (FFT, shore atlas, GPU queries, spray, contacts, colour copy, marine snow, lens droplets, shafts, Snell window, the underwater `+0.81 EV`), candidate A and `?water=`. The look sits between Sunshine (flat readable colour, cyan shallows, sparkle) and Odyssey where it costs nothing (smoother depth, clear sandy shallows, a soft breathing foam edge, a glint band, larger softer cells): two presets on one Dev-only value, Odyssey-ish the default (owner, 10 November, on seeing both: "Yeah ship the odyssey one if not already") and Sunshine-ish one constant away; no refraction, colour copy, real reflections or FFT. Supersedes the 9 November plan's "Ultra stays behind `?water=ultra` for one release" (agent call), the 4 and 3 November calls' Ultra water, underwater look, marine snow and surfacing droplets, and the 5 November underwater offset; surface swimming and automatic plunges stand. Ultra is kept in the local tag `archive/water-ultra-2026-11-09`. See [water](game-design/water.md#the-sea-10-november-2026) and [the plan](plans/water-sunshine.md).
- <a id="d-2026-11-09-the-water-s-target-is-super"></a>**The water's target is Super Mario Sunshine, lighting and grading included, and it must be cheap on a phone** (9 November, owner: "for our water refactor/remake, visual target should be something like super Mario sunshine, I like the lighting/grading there too and should be really efficient for mobile without issue"; earlier the same day: "a lot more inexpensive instead of ultra water, that also fits the style of the game better", and "we will also revisit toon shading on characters and env"). Per "Use references by name", the pass names the features of Sunshine's water and light it takes (how the surface, sparkle, shore and colour read, and how cheaply they are made) and builds our own; the Ultra water stops being the default once a candidate is picked. Earlier research to start from: [sunshine-look](research/sunshine-look.md), [ultra-water](research/ultra-water.md). The toon shading revisit shares this look decision.
