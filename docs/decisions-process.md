- <a id="d-2026-11-07-evaluate-shared-progression-adventures"></a>**2026-11-07, evaluate shared progression adventures** (owner: "I totally agree" with the reevaluation after retiring principles 1 and 4). Judge the project from wanting the result through work, coordination, completion and use. Goals, rewards, shared purchasing and deliberate task division can provide satisfaction; each action need not work as an isolated toy. The first producer proof must explicitly reach the hand-to-machine crossover in the test tuning: the ring-and-economy brief's unmodified levels 1 and 2 do not. Treat its proposed 5.8-hour endgame as future direction until the first complete automation cycle earns more.
  - **Briefs:** judge jump upgrades by access to remembered destinations and improved subsequent journeys; extra laps are optional evidence. Judge the great beam's clarity and the satisfaction of moving it together. Review gate puzzles for meaningful complementary tasks while retaining the approved rigs until a specific revision is approved. Shared milestone gatherings may be considered; co-op abilities option E remains approved, with no automatic presentation redesign.
  - **Delivery and proof:** keep approved ECS, replication, greybox and line-2 work moving. Finish the connected line-2 experience; use the economy prototype, after its existing prerequisites, to test choices, division of work and what upgrades retire; play the sequence together and check solo viability alongside it.
