## Decided

- <a id="d-2026-11-11-the-game-has-acts"></a>**2026-11-11, the game has acts** (owner, 11 November 2026: "At the beginning of the game, we should have some sort of full screen, like, title, like, when you first start, that says, like, Act 1: the beginning. And then when we hit big checkpoints as a server, maybe we get that on the screen as well, like Act 2, etc. And that way the players have a sense that there is a considered story and that there are chapters ... and that is a motivating factor to progress to find out more about the story and what changes as well."). He then answered the designer's review of [the brief](brainstorms/acts.md#design-review-designer-2026-11-11). (1) On Act 1 over the knock-down you already have, full screen, no extra hold: "I like the idea of the act being a short 3-5 second aerial camera move with the title appearing, title can be sort of botw inspired". (2) On Act 1 ending at Far Head's show: "Sure". (3) On "Next stop: (place)" under the title, and number-only for acts he has not titled: "Sure for now" (he gave no titles for Acts 2 and 3). (4) On holding a card until its act is built: "Sure, makes sense". (5) On leaving the acts past Ring Island unnumbered: "Agreed". The orchestrator's reading of answer 1, not his words: an act card is a short aerial camera move of 3 to 5 seconds with the title over it. Act 1's is the first visit's own arrival (your own camera, nobody else in the room is held); Act 2's is the room's existing shared shot at Far Head, trimmed to 5 s, never a second move after it, with its skip and reduced-motion rules (no move, the title over a still frame). "Botw inspired" is a feel direction, not something to copy: a quiet location-title look from fonts already in the repo. The rule: the room's act is derived from railway progress already held (Act 1 until Q5 is laid, Act 2 after), with no saved state, wire change or `WORLD_NS`; only an act whose opening content is built has a card (today Acts 1 and 2); "Act 1 · The beginning" is the only title, every other act shows its number and one "Next stop: (place)" line naming a built place, with no teaser, recap or count; a returning player gets the act as the first line of the Welcome back card instead; two full-screen moments never stack. "Chapter" is The Boogler's word: the cards say acts.

- <a id="d-2026-11-10-shared-moments-play-for-everyone"></a>**2026-11-10, shared moments play for everyone** (owner, after the co-op playtest that morning: "I wasn't sure if everyone got the cutscene for opening the gate. So all that stuff should be under a unified system so that even in multiplayer, everyone gets the..."; his message was cut off there). The orchestrator's reading, which the owner can correct since the sentence is unfinished: every world reveal and cutscene (the gates, firsts, the finale) goes through one shared system, so every player in the room gets it, including late joiners where that makes sense. Today the quarry and sea gate shots and the Works throat shot play only for whoever pulled or struck, and the glider's card only for whoever took it, while Far Head's show and the pier's rockets already play for everyone ([round 4 notes](plans/round-4-notes.md#owner-co-op-session-10-november-ipad-and-desktop-v24-ranked)). Supersedes "only the striker gets the shot" ([place](game-design/place.md), "Signposting") and, on this reading, answers the 3 November open note that the device cinematic goes only to whoever takes it. Built 10 November (`src/entities/railway/shared-moment.ts`): the quarry and sea gate shots and the Works throat shot play on every page in the room from the accepted pull or strike (the actor's from the prediction, everyone else's as the server confirms it), near or far since this is silent on distance, and never on a reload or late join; the glider's card stays with the finder and the others keep their flourish; each page marks `moment played` with who saw what. Not tested on a device.
- <a id="d-2026-11-09-four-more-answers"></a>**Four more answers** (9 November, owner, each the recommended option). (1) Nothing is drawn under you in the air on a jump: the filled teal disc during the first jump goes as well as the hollow ring after the second; the glints are the double jump's effect. (2) Whoever fits the boiler gets a card afterwards, with the copy "Steam" / "Trains run both lines." (his to reword). (3) Until replication R3 makes island throwing work, the live game offers no "Throw": the prompt is hidden in the railway room, and the two nightly checks that test throwing are dropped until then. (4) Model routes stay as set that day: the main session and the independent pre-merge review on Fable, the planner on Fable at medium effort, implementers on Opus ([owner calls](agents/owner-calls.md), "Models and effort").
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-the-hotbar-is-cut-one-thing"></a>**The hotbar is cut: one thing in your hands** (2 November, owner, after playing `?proto=onehand`: "No I didn't [miss it] at all. I think in that case we cut it. We can always bring it back if a real gameplay element demands it."). Picking something up replaces what you hold, which lands by you; no slots, no LB, RB or 1-5. ### Controls

- <a id="d-2026-11-09-a-cinematic-can-t-be-interrupted"></a>**2026-11-09, a cinematic can't be interrupted by accident** (owner, after the quarry lever's gate shot ended as it began on desktop: "the cinematic should be uninterruptible unless by maybe a specific highlighted button press"). No key, click, tap, wheel, mouse move, pad button or stick ends a camera pull-out (the lever and summit-switch gate shots, the Works throat, Place's celebrations). The one exception, his "maybe", is built and removable in one place: a single highlighted button shown while the shot plays (the pick is logged in [agent calls](agent-calls.md)). A reward card opening mid-shot queues the shot behind it instead of dropping it.

- <a id="d-2026-11-09-a-world-reset-starts-the-game"></a>**2026-11-09, a world reset starts the game over** (owner: "upon world resets, if I log in it shouldn't spawn me in town, it should start me at the beginning of the game with the character creation menu I believe"). The first load after a `WORLD_NS` bump is a first visit: the fall, then the name-and-colour card, landing on the shore rather than at the village spawn. The card comes pre-filled with the saved name and colour, so a reset costs one Jump in; what the reset already forgets (room progress, the railway wardrobe, reward claims, The Boogler) is unchanged, and identity and preferences stay. A reload after that, or any load without a reset, is a returning visit as before. Built in `src/player/world-reset.ts` (`newWorld`) and `src/player/arrival.ts`; [first-run](game-design/first-run.md#existing-arrival). The open calls it left are in [agent-calls](agent-calls.md).
- <a id="d-2026-11-09-a-room-reset-starts-the-game"></a>**2026-11-09, a room reset starts the game over too, The Boogler included** (owner, on an operator `reset` still landing them in town: "yes"; "room reset should also wipe boogler memory, he's just another NPC"). Resetting one room (`npm run server`) is a new game for that room's players: the fall and the card on their next join, with that room's local progress, wardrobe and reward claims forgotten, and The Boogler's memory with them even though it is shared across rooms. Other rooms' progress, identity and preferences stay. A restore brings the backup's world back under its own id, so it starts over only the browsers that joined a different world in between (undoing a reset is seamless for everyone who did not play the reset room). Built on a per-room world id from the server (`born` in `welcome`, `server/src/store.ts`) and `src/features/room-reset.ts`; [first-run](game-design/first-run.md#existing-arrival).
- <a id="d-2026-11-03-no-first-run-controls-card"></a>**2026-11-03, no first-run controls card** (owner: "the pause menu has sufficient context for now"). The "Getting around" card that popped up on entering is removed; the pause view's Controls page is the one place controls are listed.

- <a id="d-2026-11-01-interact-moves-to-x-square"></a>**2026-11-01, Interact moves to X / Square** (the west face button; owner, iPad playtest with a controller: "I want to use X/Square and it prompts me B for a lot of things"). B becomes back/cancel. Dialogue with The Boogler gets a controller pass designed to scale to every NPC.

- <a id="d-2026-11-01-no-petting-the-boogler"></a>**2026-11-01, no petting The Boogler** (owner: "not in the nature of the Boogler"). The △ pat added in the controller pass is rolled back. Talk hints: ✕ Next while text plays, ✕ Answer only when there are choices, ○ Leave; □ does nothing in a talk.

- <a id="d-2026-11-01-no-contribution-tier-labels-on-the"></a>**2026-11-01, no contribution-tier labels on the player** (owner: the "Porter" tag under his character "shouldn't be the case"). Tiers stay as data; nothing shows them until rewards are designed.
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-the-first-visit-is-a-fall"></a>**2026-11-02, the first visit is a fall into the world** (owner: "I really like A and D, especially D, and having the sort of fall animation where you're knocked down right at the beginning"; "the player has to start above a cloud for it to last long enough to be fun"; "I really love this"). `?proto=fall`: you meet your character riding a flat trolley on a rail above the clouds (owner: no handle needed, and "I like that the platform is flat enough that you can see the character jump and switch colors"), one continuous shot with no separate screen, then the rail runs out and you fall through a BOOGLE title in timber. The rail going nowhere is the tie to the game (the owner asked whether it would feel random, and took the director's answer). The cart is already travelling while you're on the card, and Jump in is when you fall (owner, after playing it: "the idea is very sound"). The card offers colour only for now. The letters aren't synced ("they can just be an independent thing because they'll sort of dissipate slowly"). It's for the first time only, and long, "like 6 seconds or so"; rejoining "should either be really fast or the character should just ... spawn in where they were" (owner). It's the game's first visit since the same day (owner: "we can ship this version already of what we have and continue iterating"). Today a return goes straight in at the usual spawn; coming back where you left off isn't built.
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-the-title-is-in-mid-air"></a>**2026-11-02, the title is in mid-air** (owner: "in between the fall and hitting the ground, you basically hit the title and it falls with you"; "when I smash through it and the letters come through the air, that's a yes"). BOOGLE hangs under the cloud, you smash through it and the letters fall with you. The fall starts higher, at 150 m (about 7.5 s), so you see it for longer (owner: "have me fall from a higher height ... since this is now only a new player experience"). It replaces crashing into it on the ground, and settles the title-drop question below.
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-a-return-goes-straight-in-and"></a>**2026-11-02, a return goes straight in, and you start fresh from the pause menu** (owner: "we just go straight in, and then you're able to start fresh from the pause menu", with "a are you sure type of thing"; "if you redo a fall and rename, that's essentially a new person"). No Continue or Start Over screen. You come back where you left off, per room ("spawn in where they were"). Research: [returning-player.md](research/returning-player.md).
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-get-unstuck-in-the-pause-menu"></a>**2026-11-02, Get unstuck in the pause menu; invite links wait** (owner: "build it"; "Let's skip the invite links for now, as we're still testing with a very small group"). Coming back where you left off means a reload no longer frees you, so one press puts you at the spawn and forgets the saved spot. Landing near the friend who invited you isn't built.
- <a id="d-2026-11-02-leave-goes-to-a-menu-with"></a>**2026-11-02, Leave goes to a menu with the game stopped** (owner: "pull you out to maybe just a menu and not be rendering the game"; it "would probably let you type in a new lobby name when we have more lobbies, potentially"). Replaces Leave reloading onto the card. The lobby box isn't built.
- <a id="d-2026-11-01-first-visit-and-returning-visit-are"></a>**2026-11-01, first visit and returning visit are different** (owner): the name-and-colour card shows on a player's first visit only; a returning player goes straight in, with their identity kept and editable from the pause view, and a light welcome back that shows what changed. Built for persistent progress and more UI later.
